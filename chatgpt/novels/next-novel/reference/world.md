# Setting and world reference

Only record new-novel facts established on page. Earlier novel lore is excluded.

## Cultivation and access

- Ch1 P43-P44, P50-P53: A great calamity is approaching; an age of universal cultivation has begun. Earlier, sects, dynasties and powerful clans controlled access to cultivation and usually excluded commoners without extraordinary talent. The broadened access is attributed to the impending calamity.
- Ch1 P44-P45, P53-P56: Even some spirit embryo aptitude permits entry into cultivation. A Pseudo Spirit Embryo lacks that route; the text presents body tempering as their sole remaining option. Breaking through the first cultivation barrier with body tempering is extremely difficult, requiring hundreds, thousands or tens of thousands of times as much effort as for spirit embryo holders. This is difficult, not declared universally impossible.
- Ch1 P63-P67: Admission to the Dao Sect is regarded as impossible for someone with a Pseudo Spirit Embryo. No hierarchy, location or comprehensive charter of the Dao Sect is yet established.

## Objects and locations

- Ch1 P5-P16, P77-P114: An oil-paper umbrella, short blade and long blade appear in the rainy forest-path duel. The umbrella is practical shelter; the blades are physical weapons in the depicted fight.
- Ch1 P18: Heavenly Prison is mentioned in a captor's threat; the man's office and its actual institutional details remain unverified.
- Ch1 P29: A hundred-tael silver note is carried by Chu Huaixu; the scene compares fifty taels to over 50,000 yuan in buying power on Earth.
- Ch1 P2-P11, P114: A gaming helmet precedes apparent entry into the scene; at the end the exit game button is missing. No game rules, interface properties or explanation of the world have yet been established.
