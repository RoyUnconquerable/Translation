# Continuity ledger

Only record facts stated in the new novel. Cite chapter and source-paragraph indices. Preserve the protagonist's limited knowledge and reveal order.

## Current story state

Ch1 P2-P11: Chu Huaixu puts on a gaming helmet, opens his eyes in a dark rainy woodland setting, and uses a two-finger upward gesture that makes the visible world brighter. Whether the world is an ordinary game remains unresolved.

Ch1 P12-P22: An unnamed stubbled man in black carries an umbrella and long blade and demands that Chu Huaixu either return for confinement in the Heavenly Prison or pay fifty taels. The man then steps aside as if to allow passage.

Ch1 P25-P39: Chu Huaixu estimates fifty taels at over 50,000 yuan of buying power back on Earth and finds a hundred-tael silver note on his person. His request for change leads the man to see him as wealthy prey.

Ch1 P42-P70: The man believes Chu Huaixu to be a mortal with a Pseudo Spirit Embryo, and mocks the idea of his entering the Dao Sect. Chu Huaixu notes the man is similarly limited and answers with the modern joke "I'm a sports recruit."

Ch1 P77-P108: The two throw away their umbrellas and fight with blades. The attacker has superior raw physical strength and some body-tempering experience, but Chu Huaixu repeatedly evades him through superior control and precision. Chu Huaixu cuts his throat, kicks him away to avoid a dying counterattack, and taunts him. The man is left bleeding and convulsing; the scene presents his defeat as decided.

Ch1 P111-P114: Chu Huaixu retrieves his umbrella, takes pride in his performance, and then discovers the exit game button is missing. He is shocked and angry. The exact nature of his predicament is still unknown.

## Dated events and reveals

- Ch1: Rainy-night encounter, attack, and apparent inability to exit the game.
- Ch1: Dao Sect's exclusion of Pseudo Spirit Embryos is known to these characters; do not assume a full admissions policy beyond what is stated.
