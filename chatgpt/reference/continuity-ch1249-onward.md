## Chapter 1249

Lü Yang realizes why the Primordial Saint wanted to kill the World-Honored One two lives ago. If the World-Honored One dies, Karma collapses and the great net of karma returns to the River of Time, allowing the Primordial Saint to use Destiny to command Variables. Lü Yang concludes that the World-Honored One has always been a handpicked Dao Lord-grade talent and the Primordial Saint's greatest fallback if Divine Transformation and Ascension fails. He compares the Saint Sect's methods to a crafty rabbit keeping three burrows and wonders whether the Primordial Saint has a lower strategy beyond Divine Transformation and Ascension and the combined command of Destiny and Variables.

After Lü Yang reveals the Divine Transformation and Ascension plan, Si Sui argues that it is too ingenious to be a normal scheme. Variables stand first among the Great Daos of the Sea of Light, so devising a plan and successfully carrying it out should be very different. The Primordial Saint attained Time through Empty Attainment and gave it form as the River of Time, yet somehow arranged for Myriad Treasures to prove Karma, transform the river into the great net of karma, and draw Variables into it. Si Sui suspects the Primordial Saint knew the result in advance rather than gambling the foundation upon which He proved the Dao.

Lü Yang considers whether the Heavenly Book supplied that foreknowledge, but Si Sui says the page can deduce only fragments of the future and cannot reach such precision. Lü Yang then raises Grand Ancestral Master, a named realm among the seven Heavenly Person's Remnant Consciousnesses that has reportedly remained under the Primordial Saint's control. The name is drawn from the chapter `大宗师` of the *Zhuangzi* and must be treated as a place, not a person or title. Dao Tianqi suspected that the Primordial Saint's Empty Attainment of Alchemy, Artifact Refinement, Dual Cultivation, and Beast-Taming was connected to Grand Ancestral Master, which may contain a Divine Transformation inheritance and evidence of that higher realm.

Si Sui learns that Dao Tianqi proved the Underworld roughly forty thousand years after the Dao Lord war, was cast down by the Dao Lords, remained sunken for tens of thousands of years, and was only recently awakened by Lü Yang. The Underworld remains confined to the Immortal Pivot and has not realized its original vision across the Sea of Light.

Lü Yang and Si Sui reason that if Grand Ancestral Master contains the Primordial Saint's deepest secret, He would hide it where any inevitable flaw remained within His own sight and control. The answer is the River of Time, the foundation upon which He proved the Dao. When Myriad Treasures later proved Karma and transformed the river into the great net of karma, Grand Ancestral Master gained a second layer of concealment. Finding it would require the World-Honored One's death, while Dao Lords remain immortal so long as the Other Shore stands.

Lü Yang is both furious and awed by the Primordial Saint's layered design. He checks the Book of a Hundred Lives and ends with the relieved thought that it is fortunate he never closed it.

Draft title rendering: `幸好我没关` = `Good Thing I Didn't Close It`.

Locked owner ruling: `大宗师` = `Grand Ancestral Master`, a Remnant Consciousness realm named after the *Zhuangzi* chapter, never `Grandmaster` and never a person or honorific.

Cultural imagery to preserve includes the crafty rabbit's three burrows in `狡兔三窟`, the upper, middle, and lower strategy structure in `上策 / 中策 / 下策`, and the Saint Sect principle that in the end one must rely on oneself.

## Chapter 1250

Si Sui narrows the World-Honored One's test piece to an exceptionally talented Foundation Establishment True Person from the Immortal Pivot in the True History, because directly seeking the fundamental Five Elements requires both talent and brilliance. With this higher vantage point, Lü Yang identifies Chongguang: apart from Demon-Purging True Person, Chongguang ranks first among the Immortal Pivot's Foundation Establishment cultivators, serves as the Saint Sect's acting sect master, and has reached the True Monarch realm in three of Lü Yang's restarts, including one through the unfamiliar Heart Demon Dao.

Lü Yang reconstructs the method behind the move. Chongguang was an Immortal Spirit in his previous life, so his ancient original body exists in Jiangbei and naturally draws the Heavenly Lord's attention. The Heavenly Lord's earlier obstruction and the One Mind lead Lü Yang to conclude that the World-Honored One controls the Heavenly Lord and chose Chongguang as His piece. The crucial information Si Sui has provided gives Lü Yang confidence that he can negotiate with the World-Honored One and perhaps draw Him into his own camp.

After withdrawing from the Foundation Establishment puppet, Lü Yang decides to seize Jiangbei. Chongguang cannot move freely as an Immortal Spirit and must avoid human habitation, but his identity and birthplace make discovery inevitable once Lü Yang controls the region. The three years spent bringing Jiangxi and the Overseas into order are complete.

At the Heaven-Connecting Sea of Clouds, Lian Tiandou predicts Lü Yang's approach. Jiangdong and Jiangnan remain linked, and the Grand Sword Patriarch's presence makes Jiangnan too hard a target, leaving Jiangbei as the soft persimmon. Lian Tiandou chooses to fight Lü Yang because he believes Lü Yang may hold answers to certain questions, and Bu Tianque agrees to follow him.

Lian Tiandou then senses something wrong with his memories that Eldest Senior Brother died and Little Junior Brother disappeared. Looking at the familiar sky, he silently asks what lies beyond the Immortal Pivot and why no True Monarch or Dao Lineage ever thinks of venturing into the Sea of Light. The world he remembers begins to seem illusory.

Draft title rendering: `一切明晰` = `Everything Becomes Clear!`.
