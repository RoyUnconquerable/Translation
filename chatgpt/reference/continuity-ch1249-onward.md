## Chapter 1249

Lü Yang realizes why the Primordial Saint wanted to kill the World-Honored One two lives ago. If the World-Honored One dies, Karma collapses and the great net of karma returns to the River of Time, allowing the Primordial Saint to use Destiny to command Variables. Lü Yang concludes that the World-Honored One has always been a handpicked Dao Lord-grade piece of material and the Primordial Saint's greatest fallback if Divine Transformation and Ascension fails. He compares the Saint Sect's methods to a crafty hare keeping three burrows and wonders whether the Primordial Saint has a lower strategy beyond Divine Transformation and Ascension and the combined command of Destiny and Variables.

After Lü Yang reveals the Divine Transformation and Ascension plan, Si Sui argues that it is too precise to be a normal scheme. Variables stand first among the Great Daos of the Sea of Light, so devising a plan and successfully carrying it out should be very different. The Primordial Saint proved Time through Empty Attainment and gave it form as the River of Time, yet somehow arranged for Myriad Treasures to prove Karma, transform the river into the great net of karma, and draw Variables into it. Si Sui suspects the Primordial Saint knew the result in advance rather than gambling the foundation upon which He proved the Dao.

Lü Yang considers whether the Heavenly Book supplied that foreknowledge, but Si Sui says the page can deduce only portions of the future and cannot reach such precision. Lü Yang then raises `Grandmaster`, one of the seven Heavenly Person's Remnant Consciousnesses and reportedly long controlled by the Primordial Saint. Dao Tianqi suspected that the Primordial Saint's Empty Attainment of Alchemy, Artifact Refinement, Dual Cultivation, and Beast-Taming was connected to Grandmaster, which may contain a Divine Transformation inheritance and evidence of that higher realm.

Si Sui learns that Dao Tianqi proved the Underworld roughly forty thousand years after the Dao Lord war, was cast down by the Dao Lords, remained sunken for tens of thousands of years, and was only recently awakened by Lü Yang. The Underworld remains confined to the Immortal Pivot and has not realized its original vision across the Sea of Light.

Lü Yang and Si Sui reason that if Grandmaster contains the Primordial Saint's deepest secret, He would hide it where any inevitable flaw remained within His own sight and control. The answer is the River of Time, the foundation upon which He proved the Dao. When Myriad Treasures later proved Karma and transformed the river into the great net of karma, Grandmaster gained a second layer of concealment. Finding it would require the World-Honored One's death, while Dao Lords remain immortal so long as the Other Shore stands.

Lü Yang is both furious and awed by the Primordial Saint's layered design. He checks the Book of a Hundred Lives and ends with the relieved thought that it is fortunate he never closed it.
