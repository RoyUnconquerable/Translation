# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1265 Owner Final

True Monarch Flying Snow reaches the Heavenly Palace as a soul carrying Fate-Reforming and the Flowing Downward she attained through Empty Attainment. Lü Yang's earlier strand of power from the Great Dao of Yin allowed her to escape beneath the Primordial Saint's gaze before the Ancestral Dragon could possess her through the Usurpation of the Huai.

At the Palace of Soaring Clouds, Flying Snow meets the Heavenly Emperor. Although his aura is identical to Lü Yang's, his domineering bearing and indifference reveal a fundamentally different personality. He directs her to the Divine Rank corresponding to Divine Offerings.

Four exalted seats stand above the palace's ordered ranks of divine statues. Two already have masters, while auras occupy the other two. Flying Snow uses Flowing Downward to overcome the resistance of the Divine Offerings rank and merge with it. The Heavenly Emperor realizes that Lü Yang anticipated this outcome.

After descending from the seventh layer to the sixth, the Primordial Saint invokes Destiny to anchor the final result regardless of the lost process and methods. The Ancestral Dragon still draws upon Tribulation, the Other Shore rises, the Immortal Pivot shatters, and the Ancestral Dragon's body becomes fuel for Divine Transformation and Ascension.

All ten required Great Daos are assembled in patched-together form. Fate, Fortune, Destiny, Spells, and Spiritual Power have Dao Lords presiding over them. Tribulation answers the Ancestral Dragon, Divine Transformation and Ascension supplies Variables, the Underworld replaces the Five Elements, Karma replaces Yin and Yang, and the Ancestral Dragon's body replaces the Dharma Body.

The Primordial Saint warns the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky that the Other Shore's ascent is weakening Their control over the False History and may destroy the Heavenly Dao. They stop opposing Him because They will accept Si Sui's escape only after Divine Transformation and Ascension and after the God-Sealing Method's Dao Lineage has spread throughout the Sea of Light, allowing the Heavenly Dao to be rebuilt without someone else plucking the peach from Their hands.

The World-Honored One acts before those conditions are met. He burns away His golden body on the Other Shore while True Monarch Radiant World Western Light abandons the final step of proving the Dao within the False History.

By altering cause and changing effect, the World-Honored One gives up proving the Dao five thousand years ago and ceases to be a Dao Lord. The rewritten past means He never participated in the battle 129,600 years ago and Si Sui never died. The False History is about to return, while the disappearance of Karma from the position of Yin and Yang abruptly halts Divine Transformation and Ascension.
