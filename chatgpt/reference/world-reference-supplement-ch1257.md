# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1257

## Extreme Heaven Cliff and the battle's outcome

At Extreme Heaven Cliff in Jiangnan, Skyward cannot see the battle above the firmament. The Grand Sword Patriarch can discern its general outcome and recognizes that the Heavenly Lord's aura broke through the limit of Treading the Heavens into a true Primordial Spirit aura.

Because the Heavenly Lord, the Immortal Pivot's first Immortal Spirit, has no Dao Heart cultivation, the Grand Sword Patriarch infers that the figure wearing its identity is a Dao Lord who descended from the real world outside the False History. Skyward immediately identifies the World-Honored One, citing His well-known habit of descending into lower realms to challenge opponents across cultivation realms.

The Grand Sword Patriarch judges that Lü Yang gained the upper hand, but did not decisively defeat or kill the World-Honored One. If the battle continued, he estimates Lü Yang's odds at six to four. His sight is then deliberately obscured.

## False History cosmology

Beyond the False History's Immortal Pivot lies no Sea of Light, only a vast firmament that can contain cultivators battling within it. Higher cultivation places a cultivator farther from the mortal world, but no ascent can pierce the firmament and escape the False History.

The False History lacks the Foundation Establishment Realm, the Sea of Suffering, the dark side of the Sea of Light, and the Underworld. It contains only false imitations shaped by Karma. Lü Yang infers that supplying the absent real counterparts might transform the False History from illusion into genuine reality. This remains Lü Yang's hypothesis, not confirmed cosmological fact.

## Dao Lord methods and comparative strength

The World-Honored One acknowledges that Lü Yang's Fate-Reforming Mystery is closer to Variables than His Karma. Lü Yang offers Him a Fate-Reforming cultivation jade slip, which He refuses.

The World-Honored One gives the following same-realm comparison among the six Dao Lords:

- The Spiritual Power Dao Lord is weaker than the World-Honored One.
- Setting aside status, the Spell Dao Lord's methods are roughly comparable to the World-Honored One's.
- Vast Sky is somewhat stronger than the World-Honored One.
- The Sword Sovereign is weaker only than the Primordial Saint. The Heavenly Dao's empowerment makes Her stronger still.
- The Primordial Saint remains the clear highest benchmark in this comparison.

Lü Yang infers from this that, at his current combat strength and the same realm, he may be able to kill the Spiritual Power Dao Lord, edge out the Spell Dao Lord, draw with Vast Sky, face a real risk of death against the Sword Sovereign, die against a Heavenly-Dao-empowered Sword Sovereign, and count surviving one hundred moves against the Primordial Saint as success. These are Lü Yang's estimates, not objective confirmations.
