## Chapter 1246

At Kan-Yang Lake, Chongguang greets the World-Honored One after the hidden Buddhist method planted in his sea of consciousness takes effect. He fears that the One Mind may already have converted him so completely that he only believes he remains Chongguang.

The World-Honored One takes over the lucid Heavenly Lord long enough to digest its memories and understand the current False History. From Lü Yang's expansion of the God-Sealing Method, the Fire and Water territories, and Skyward's hidden cooperation, He deduces that True Monarch Sovereign of Fate intends to control the timing of the False History's return during the Primordial Saint's Divine Transformation and Ascension. He further infers that Lü Yang has contacted Si Sui and gained his approval for an attempt likened to an ant swallowing a dragon.

The World-Honored One realizes that the Other Shore falling, the False History returning, Si Sui escaping, and the Sea of Light becoming a pot of porridge would actually serve His own interests. Outside the False History, He deliberately cracks His golden Buddha manifestation, spits and re-swallows Buddha blood, and falsely tells the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky that the lucid celestial demon refused to soul-search Chongguang, destroyed him, resisted conversion through a Dao Heart, allied with the Grand Sword Patriarch, and damaged the World-Honored One through His False History incarnation.

The Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky identify that incarnation as the present body True Monarch Radiant World Western Light, which exists five thousand years before the False History's present and forms the foundation of the World-Honored One's Dao attainment. The World-Honored One claims to have lost at least one-tenth of His mighty power and requests authority over the Heavenly Dao so the present body can trace karma backward and eliminate all Variables at full strength.

The Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky distrust His one-sided account because only the World-Honored One can enter the chaotic False History through the One Mind. They will grant authority only to True Monarch Radiant World Western Light, whose peak Golden Core status leaves him unable to reverse the roles of guest and host and places his life, death, and the World-Honored One's Dao-attainment foundation under Their control.

The World-Honored One outwardly protests beneath a wrathful Vajra glare, but inwardly His heart blossoms with laughter. He is willing to exchange a foundation He already plans to abandon for genuine benefits. Since the Other Shore is a broken ship destined to sink, He decides that remaining its lowest Dao Lord is inferior to jumping ship and seeking another path.
