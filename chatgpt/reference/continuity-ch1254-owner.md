# Owner-Final Continuity, Chapter 1254

Read after Chapter 1253 continuity. This records the owner-reviewed Chapter 1254 events.

Emperor Cang remains pinned beneath Heavenly Fire and Inscribing the Heavenly Chapter. The manifested judicial label `Beheading, Pending Review` weighs upon him like Mount Tai and prevents him from even raising his head. Lü Yang explicitly warns the unseen Heavenly Lord that if it still refuses to save Emperor Cang, he will kill him.

The Heavenly Lord does not intervene. Emperor Cang realizes he was never important to Lü Yang, abandons dignity, and begs the Heavenly Lord for rescue. When none comes, he tries to switch sides and offer Lü Yang his help.

Lü Yang executes him instead. The judicial label changes from `Beheading, Pending Review` to `Beheading, Immediate Execution!`, and Emperor Cang's dharma body splits into his three avatars before shattering. His premature death, combined with his identity and karma, leaves a visible trace in the chaotic river of the False History.

Lü Yang admits he could have recruited Emperor Cang and safely constrained him through the God-Sealing Method, but chooses not to because he now has enough strength to indulge himself.

The Heavenly Lord then reveals the power it had been accumulating. A figure appears within the thunderclouds. Lian Tiandou and Bu Tianque react strongly, initially identifying him as their Eldest Senior Brother, the first-generation Myriad Treasures Peak Lord. Bu Tianque quickly rejects the idea that this is merely a remnant shadow extracted by the Heavenly Lord.

The Grand Sword Patriarch independently notes that a true Primordial Spirit leaves no trace in heaven and earth, so the Heavenly Lord could not simply call up such a remnant without consent. Lü Yang therefore recognizes the figure as the World-Honored One genuinely descending through the Heavenly Lord's power.

The chapter explicitly frames this through the recurring `smurfing` joke. The World-Honored One has descended to overpower lower-level opponents many times before, usually through the Five Directional Tathagatas of the Vajra Realm or with Dao Lord status. This time the False History limits Him to roughly Treading the Heavens and below peak Golden Core, making this His fairest smurfing attempt yet.

Lü Yang concludes that the World-Honored One can be fought and possibly defeated at the same realm. He decides to use this battle as a benchmark for how he might someday fare against the Primordial Saint, who once killed the World-Honored One at the same realm within one hundred moves.
