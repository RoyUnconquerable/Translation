# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1262

## Refinement of the Nascent Soul Pill

The Nascent Soul Pill is refined within an enormous pill furnace among the Underworld's mountains. Purple-Azure Tushita Fire flows throughout the range. Wind and clouds converge, dragon roars and tiger howls sound faintly, and ancient imagery accumulates within the furnace until it forms a gold-and-white halo.

The completed pill is gold and white, wreathed in fragrant mist, and marked by winding dragon-and-tiger patterns. It contains intensely consummate imagery capable of bringing all things to flawless completion.

## Dao Lord intervention

The Sword Sovereign is the first Dao Lord to attempt to stop the pill's completion. Her sword cry carries imagery of inevitable fate like a boundless river. Dao Tianqi blocks the attack from before the Underworld Sovereign's Hall.

Vast Sky then sends golden light down from the Outer Heavens. Talismanic characters depicting mountains, rivers, the sun, moon, and stars assemble into the character for Heaven and manifest a radiant world. The Primordial Saint stops it with one illusory fair finger, which forms a heavenly chasm between the attack and the Underworld.

Because Lü Yang exposed the plan in advance, the Dao Lords understand Divine Transformation and Ascension. The Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky are testing the Primordial Saint rather than committing Their full strength. The World-Honored One does not react, while the Spiritual Power Dao Lord and Spell Dao Lord observe coldly.

## Tai Yitian and the apparent seizure

Tai Yitian is the Primordial Saint's true name. True Monarch Azure Firmament invokes it immediately after taking the completed Nascent Soul Pill. A second finger from the Primordial Saint slips past Vast Sky and the Sword Sovereign, lifts the pill away ahead of Dao Tianqi, and returns it to the Other Shore.

The narration states that the Cangjiang Dragon Monarch used the Primordial Saint's true name to resolve the immediate crisis. Preserve this identity sequence without silently collapsing the titles or identities into one another.

## Dao Tianqi's countermeasure

Dao Tianqi's Eldest Senior Brother has long criticized him for possessing exceptional comprehension while squandering it and neglecting schemes or maneuvering. He predicted that Dao Tianqi would eventually be schemed to death.

Dao Tianqi never accepted that judgment. He considers himself clever and says he normally chooses not to think about such matters. His apparently weak accusation and furious pursuit conceal a countermeasure: once the Primordial Saint brings the Nascent Soul Pill to the Other Shore, it explodes without warning and releases a world-destroying storm.
