# Owner Edits: Chapter 1266

The owner's supplied Chapter 1266 prose is final. The following terminology and stylistic changes from the draft are reusable.

## Approved terminology

- `七情参天诀` is *Seven Emotions Heaven-Fathoming Art*.
- `真我` is *True Self* in the Heavenly Emperor's independent-self context.
- The chapter title remains *But Incapable*.
- The final `但无能` beat is *He simply lacked the ability.*
- The classical state remains *Heaven and Earth are not benevolent.*

## Approved prose refinements

- Mighty power descends *in that instant*, fills the universe, and freezes *the mortal world* in place.
- The repeated emotional image is a *calm lake*, followed by the *calm indifference of an unruffled lake*.
- *Si Sui?* is italicized as the Primordial Saint's direct internal question.
- The Primordial Saint's transformed eyes brim with every kind of *emotion*, avoiding unnecessary synonym drift.
- The revealed Heavenly Palace begins with *What met His eyes* and develops through flights of jade steps, rows of palaces, clear laws, and an unmistakable celestial order.
- The Heavenly Emperor is referred to naturally as a *person* where identity, rather than gender, matters.
- The Primordial Saint wants to crush *both the Heavenly Emperor and the Heavenly Palace into ash*.
- The Heavenly Emperor's temperament is summarized as *Seeking only the Dao, regarding all else as false, and therefore stopping at nothing.*
- The Primordial Saint's voice carries *a note of patient persuasion*.
- Traversing the Mortal Dust permits *rapid success* but *leaves behind considerable hidden dangers*.
- The Seven Emotions Heaven-Fathoming Art *first awakened the seven emotions, then cast them aside one by one*.
- *Both reason and inclination told him to accept* the Primordial Saint's offer.
- Lü Yang is *keenly self-aware*; the Heavenly Emperor is *wholly devoted* to seeking the Dao.

## Style lesson

Apply the owner edit as a model of sentence construction, not a word-replacement list. It favors coherent visual sequencing, compact causal explanation, neutral and natural referents, reduced redundancy, and an understated comic payoff after elevated setup.
