# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1269

## The struggle between good and evil consciousnesses

The return of the False History compresses one hundred thousand years into an instant and brings Lü Yang to the final step of Traversing the Mortal Dust. His consciousness and the Heavenly Emperor confront each other on opposite sides of a pitch-black abyssal surface.

The two consciousnesses normally synchronize every method, cultivation gain, and measure of Dao Attainment. This keeps them in absolute equilibrium and explains why cultivators who attempted to refine a Primordial Spirit through this shortcut often died after both sides gravely wounded each other.

Lü Yang breaks the equilibrium through the Book of a Hundred Lives. The treasure and its talents are bound only to him as the true body, so the Heavenly Emperor lacks permission to use Supreme Dao Transformation. Lü Yang's temporary wisdom light lets him overpower and refine the Heavenly Emperor.

## Divine Offerings suppresses the Heavenly Emperor

The good-and-evil Primordial Spirit method normally requires one side's ideals to overwhelm the other's, producing stable unity with one consciousness dominant. Lü Yang instead uses the thoughts of the countless gods under his Dao Lineage to suppress the Heavenly Emperor.

Divine Offerings was originally designed to gather the consciousness of all living beings on the Dark Side of the Sea of Light and piece together a Primordial Spirit. That vast but unfocused consciousness cannot rival a Dao Lord's Primordial Spirit, but it can overwhelm the Heavenly Emperor when combined with Lü Yang's Supreme Dao Transformation.

The Heavenly Emperor can mirror cultivation methods but cannot understand this operation without Supreme Dao Transformation. The gods' thoughts become murky chains, submerge him, and establish Lü Yang as the dominant consciousness.

## Lü Yang completes his Primordial Spirit

A frail golden flame appears within Lü Yang's chest and illuminates a corner of the darkness, preventing him from losing himself. His true body awakens in the False History while the Heavenly Emperor's separate manifestation in the Heavenly Palace shatters like an illusory bubble.

The suppression is not a permanent philosophical victory. Lü Yang invokes the line "Long have I contended with myself, and still I would rather be myself" and recognizes that the word "long" matters: complete unity will require time and slow, patient tempering.

Guided by the World-Honored One and the Great Dao of Variables, the False History breaks through the Primordial Saint's blockade and converges with the real world. Lü Yang's Primordial Spirit flame opens a path through the Fathomless Void, allowing him to return as his Status surges beyond Treading the Heavens.
