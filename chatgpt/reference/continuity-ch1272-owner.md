# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1272 Owner Final

Lü Yang uses his Primordial Spirit to enter the true historical scene of the Grand Sword Patriarch's final Dao Heart struggle while history and karma intertwine around him.

Sanhe's concerned spirit admits that killing the True Monarchs was not morally right. Beginning in the first year of the Immortal Calendar, he spent two hundred years asking Pan Huang, Bu Tianque, Alchemy Cauldron, Emperor Cang, and the Heavenly Lord for another solution, but none existed.

The cold spirit argues that Sanhe should remain alive and use the Ancestral Dragon's connection to him as leverage against the Dao Lords. Sanhe exposes this as fear of death because the same argument was never raised while he was killing Pan Huang and the others.

The demonic third spirit is the Ancestral Dragon Himself. His will descends across the void, and He comes to resemble a True Dragon in human form.

The Ancestral Dragon reveals the plan to create a false history, replace the present one, and suppress Si Sui. He says the Primordial Saint's command of Destiny will prevent another Dao Lord from ever rising to defeat Him.

The Ancestral Dragon argues that Sanhe's sacrifice will be reduced to an illusory bubble within karma and forgotten by everyone. Sanhe answers that it still has meaning to the people living at that moment and that he himself remembers that meaning.

Sanhe's three divided spirits merge back into one beneath a single conviction of the Dao Heart. He places his sword against his neck and takes his own life in Jiangnan during the first month of the 264th year of the Immortal Calendar.

His spotless blade bears a seal-script inscription declaring that giving one's life for righteousness means valuing righteousness above life itself, for nothing under the heavens is more valuable than righteousness.

The Ancestral Dragon's fury lingers around Sanhe's corpse for three full months. He cannot understand or accept Sanhe's choice because history and karma are about to be rewritten and the Sword Sovereign would never have allowed Sanhe to survive.

Lü Yang reaches through time, parts karma, and stands before the corpse. He tells the Ancestral Dragon that he saw the sacrifice, remembers it, and will bring its meaning back from history.

Lü Yang's Dao resonance carries the deed's karma downstream into the Sea of Light. He declares that all shall know this sword, blameless before the world though not before men.
