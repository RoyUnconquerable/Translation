# Chat Translation Continuity, Chapters 1221 to 1228

This supplement records owner-reviewed chat translations that were intentionally not added as chapter files. Read it after `continuity.md` before continuing with Chapter 1229.

The Nether Whisper Ancestor returns to the forefront and overwhelms the six Dragon Monarchs. The Cangjiang Dragon Monarch revives because his life is entrusted to the Immortal Pivot, but the cause and memories of his death are erased. He flees to the Old Dragon Monarch and finds the Qujian Dragon Monarch beheaded but kept alive by Demon-Purging True Person.

Demon-Purging True Person unveils a third Sword Intent, Perfected Person, and one strike beheads five Dragon Monarchs at once. The Grand Sword Patriarch appears and explains the Primordial Spirit: a cultivator's good and evil consciousnesses contend until one is refined into the True Self. The Heavenly Book can make those consciousnesses real. He refined an evil-consciousness body, while the Spiritual Power Dao Lord Du Xuan refined and suppressed a good-consciousness body.

Lü Yang deduces that the Nameless Dao Lord was born from the World-Honored One's evil consciousness and Du Xuan's good consciousness. The resulting being calls Himself the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord. Lü Yang, posing as the Ancestral Dragon, induces Him to carry Si Sui's remnant consciousness and a Marionette-controlled Foundation Establishment soul through the Heavenly Dao.

Outside the False History, the Sword Sovereign, the World-Honored One, and Vast Sky consider accelerating the Primordial Saint's Divine Transformation and Ascension plan through the Nascent Soul Pill. The Primordial Saint anticipates this. At 129,600 years before the False History, the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord and Lü Yang reach a five-colored lock and the black vortex containing Si Sui.
