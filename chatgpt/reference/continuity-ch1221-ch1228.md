# Chat Translation Continuity, Chapters 1221 to 1237

This supplement records owner-reviewed chat translations that were intentionally not added as chapter files. Read it after `continuity.md` before continuing with Chapter 1238.

The Nether Whisper Ancestor returns to the forefront and overwhelms the six Dragon Monarchs. The Cangjiang Dragon Monarch revives because his life is entrusted to the Immortal Pivot, but the cause and memories of his death are erased. He flees to the Old Dragon Monarch and finds the Qujian Dragon Monarch beheaded but kept alive by Demon-Purging True Person.

Demon-Purging True Person unveils a third Sword Intent, Perfected Person, and one strike beheads five Dragon Monarchs at once. The Grand Sword Patriarch appears and explains the Primordial Spirit: a cultivator's good and evil consciousnesses contend until one is refined into the True Self. The Heavenly Book can make those consciousnesses real. He refined an evil-consciousness body, while the Spiritual Power Dao Lord Du Xuan refined and suppressed a good-consciousness body.

Lü Yang deduces that the Nameless Dao Lord was born from the World-Honored One's evil consciousness and Du Xuan's good consciousness. The resulting being calls Himself the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord. Lü Yang, posing as the Ancestral Dragon, induces Him to carry Si Sui's Remnant Consciousness and a Marionette-controlled Foundation Establishment soul through the Heavenly Dao.

Outside the False History, the Sword Sovereign, the World-Honored One, and Vast Sky consider accelerating the Primordial Saint's Divine Transformation and Ascension plan through the Nascent Soul Pill. The Primordial Saint anticipates this. At 129,600 years before the False History, the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord and Lü Yang reach a five-colored lock and the black vortex containing Si Sui.

Inside the vortex, status and ordinary temporal order collapse. The Cultivating Truth Dao Lord shields Lü Yang as they cross shattered fragments of time, where a single step can advance or reverse thousands of years of lifespan. Si Sui's repeated strikes against the seal reveal the correct fragment at exactly 129,600 years in the past. When Lü Yang's borrowed Foundation Establishment soul begins to vanish, Si Sui grants it more than 200,000 years of lifespan. They arrive before a three-story pavilion, where a young, teacher-like Si Sui greets both Fellow Daoists in person.

Si Sui restores a physical body to Lü Yang's puppet through the Dharma Body Dao and immediately recognizes the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord's origins, lack of a foundation anchoring Him in the world, and resulting Primordial Spirit flaws. He draws both visitors into a quiet room and expounds the Dao. The lecture enthralls the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord but means nothing to Lü Yang, whose complete lack of wisdom light Si Sui notices at once. Si Sui says he expected later successors even though he does not recognize Lü Yang, then reveals that the apparent paradise is only an illusion within his sea of consciousness. In reality, he is still hammering the dark seal without pause.

Si Sui moves the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord aside and explains Traversing the Mortal Dust to Lü Yang. He stresses that good and evil are subjective: his own good consciousness wanted the Dharma Body Dao to reign supreme and destroy the other ancient paths, while his evil consciousness wanted to spread the Dao throughout the Sea of Light. Si Sui chose the latter and says only his True Self remains now. He openly admits engineering the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord's eventual birth by teaching Myriad Treasures a method that could stabilize Du Xuan, benefit Myriad Treasures, and leave Si Sui a sliver of life if he lost to the Primordial Saint. Lü Yang concludes that this revered Saint's benevolence has a sharp edge, backed by calculation and ruthless methods.

Lü Yang recounts the Sea of Light's present decline. Si Sui says the Primordial Saint merely imposed His own preferences on all beings, just as Si Sui once did, and accepts that his defeat left anger powerless before fact. He had anticipated Myriad Treasures proving the Dao through Rampart Earth and returning through karma, but the Heavenly Dao devised by Nian Yao and Vast Sky exceeded his predictions; he also regrets neglecting Cang's instruction. Their grave discussion gives way to gossip: Nian Yao left a less-talented childhood sweetheart and later accepted Vast Sky's pursuit, while Si Sui knows far too many details to be merely repeating hearsay. When he instantly recognizes gossip about Alchemy Cauldron and Heaven-Mending, Lü Yang realizes this legendary Saint feels far more human than the stories suggested.

Lü Yang explains his plan to establish another Other Shore through the God-Sealing system, but Si Sui identifies a fatal gap: the seven Heavenly Person's Remnant Consciousnesses cannot remain under Lü Yang's control after he reaches peak Golden Core, because Dao Lords are barred from entering them. Even the Equality of Things no longer welcomes Si Sui. Si Sui estimates that Lü Yang's system, after he attains a Primordial Spirit, could raise him to peak Golden Core and perhaps near Si Sui's former level if he mobilized the Sea of Light, but not enough to match the Primordial Saint. To free Si Sui without the other Dao Lords' cooperation, Lü Yang must attain the Primordial Spirit, break the Heavenly Heart Lock with Si Sui, and prevent the World-Honored One from proving the Dao in the False History. When Lü Yang asks about the Heavenly Book, Si Sui reveals that the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord lied: the golden book has been in Si Sui's possession all along.

The Book of a Hundred Lives does not react to the golden Heavenly Book, and divine sense cannot perceive the volume even while Lü Yang holds it. Si Sui explains the Primordial Saint's restriction: the Heavenly Book works only when its holder neither knows what it is nor intends to use it. Once recognized or deliberately invoked, it becomes ordinary. The Primordial Saint intended to discard it rather than leave Si Sui anything with a discoverable solution. Its true function is to create a separate causal timeline, turn illusion into reality when supplied with enough power, and potentially create an entirely new world. If the False History became real, it could no longer serve as Si Sui's seal. Lü Yang suspects a deeper relationship between the Heavenly Book and the Book of a Hundred Lives but conceals how badly he wants it and remains guarded around Si Sui.

Lü Yang concludes that the Heavenly Book's restriction should weaken if the Primordial Saint's status falls, and keeps another restart through the Book of a Hundred Lives as a final contingency. Si Sui reveals that everything Lü Yang has experienced inside the False History is already part of cultivating the Primordial Spirit; the missing step is severing the consciousness there from Lü Yang's main body. Lü Yang decides he can instead send his main body's soul into the False History and leave the separated consciousness controlling the Heavenly Palace, trusting the Book of a Hundred Lives as his safety net. Si Sui gives him the complete, heavily annotated Traversing the Mortal Dust. At equal status, Si Sui says he could not defeat the Primordial Saint, but the Primordial Saint could not kill him. Si Sui also permits Pan Huang and his other disciples to switch from the Dharma Body Dao to the God-Sealing Method, though Lü Yang insists on recording the permission as proof.

The Cultivating Truth Dao Lord awakens after Si Sui's lecture with His Primordial Spirit foundation perfected. Si Sui temporarily reconstructs a matching dharma body for Him through Yin and Yang, raising Him in a breath to peak Golden Core and Dao Monarch, but the body can exist only inside Si Sui's sealed sea of consciousness and will vanish when He leaves. Desperate to retain the power, the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord listens when Lü Yang offers the God-Sealing Method as a way to establish a divine statue outside the Sea of Light and cultivate without a physical body. Si Sui recognizes that Lü Yang has deliberately baited Him and concludes that the young Fellow Daoist truly is not a good person.

Lü Yang forces the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord to accept the God-Sealing Method by threatening to destroy Si Sui's Remnant Consciousness and strand Him inside the seal. Because Lü Yang has not yet refined a Primordial Spirit and the Heavenly Palace has not reached peak Golden Core, he refuses to let the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord leave and risk seizing control of his system. He also probes for leverage over Sublime Bliss, but the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord denies having any means of controlling him. After sending Him away, Lü Yang argues that Si Sui can use the confinement to reform Him through teaching. Si Sui warns that rescue may ultimately fail and offers Lü Yang his dharma body and Yin and Yang as an additional foundation, saying he can follow the Ancestral Dragon's example.
