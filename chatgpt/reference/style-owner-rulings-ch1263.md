# Owner Style Rulings: Chapter 1263

The owner's edited Chapter 1263 prose is governing. Apply these choices to later chapters where the same terms, imagery, or narrative situations recur.

## Modern xianxia register

- Keep the prose elevated enough for Dao Lord scenes, but use clean modern English rather than ceremonial or archaic phrasing. Prefer *seemed perfectly at ease about it*, *flew into a rage*, *fell silent*, *settled on*, and *tore ... out* when those plain verbs carry the full meaning.
- Do not make exposition sound older merely because the subject is cosmic or profound. The narration should remain direct, confident, and easy to process on the first read.
- Prefer natural English collocations such as *the grave furrow of His brow*, *running with clockwork precision*, *carried the weight of a mountain*, and *any link in the chain*.
- Let the source's scale and imagery create grandeur. Avoid stiff abstractions and ceremonious inversions that make a simple causal statement sound translated.

## Subjects, causality, and progression

- Keep the actor as the grammatical subject when it improves movement: *The Primordial Saint withdrew His gaze from the Underworld*, rather than making the gaze itself withdraw.
- When a plan is explained step by step, preserve that progression explicitly. The approved sequence repeatedly uses *schemed against* and closes with *Step by step, He had built the overwhelmingly favorable position He enjoyed today.*
- Prefer a concrete result over an abstract restatement: a conjecture remains a conjecture, the Primordial Saint has yet to uncover anything concrete, and the Ancestral Dragon's trembling divine thoughts betray the struggle within Him.
- In comparative constructions, use smooth English rather than mechanically retaining the Chinese frame: *The more elaborate the plan, the less likely it is to keep up with change.*

## Idioms and images

- `庸人自扰` retains the mediocre man causing trouble for Himself. Keep it readable within Dao Tianqi's direct insult rather than expanding it into a formal explanation.
- `蛛丝马迹` is *clues as faint as spider silk and horse tracks*. Preserve both images without making the sentence sound like a glossary entry.
- `饮鸩止渴` is *drinking poison to quench your thirst*. Preserve the complete poison-and-thirst image.
- The altered plan runs with *clockwork precision*, then grinds to a halt. The Primordial Saint's contingency is described through *any link in the chain* going wrong.

## Cosmology and action wording

- Use *the summit of the Other Shore* for `彼岸顶点` in ordinary scene description.
- `光海暗面` is capitalized as *the Dark Side of the Sea of Light* from this owner final onward.
- The Primordial Saint strips away the Five Elements' *outer shell*, then forcibly *tears the Heavenly Dao out of the Five Elements*.
- The Ancestral Dragon seeks to *draw out the Tribulation*. The emphatic arrival beat is *Tribulation had descended!*
- When the Nascent Soul Pill's destruction is compensated for, the Ancestral Dragon regains Tribulation's favor, *filling the void left by the Nascent Soul Pill's destruction*.

## Mechanical normalization

The owner's phrase *Old Dragon Monarch,abruptly* contains an obvious missing space. Normalize it to *Old Dragon Monarch, abruptly* without changing the approved prose choice.
