## Register correction

The draft was often accurate but intermittently too ceremonious. Later prose should not default to phrases such as *was entirely candid*, *bore a mountain's pressure*, or other abstract and elevated constructions when the source only requires someone to be at ease, a voice to carry weight, or a character to fall silent. Preserve xianxia grandeur through imagery, scale, and cultivation concepts while keeping the sentence structure modern and direct.
