# Owner Edits: Chapter 1258

The owner's attached Chapter 1258 revision is the governing prose. Chapter text remains chat-only.

## Errors in the provisional draft

The provisional translation failed in several ways that the existing instructions already covered:

- It used the present conditional in the title and echoed boast, *would fall apart*, instead of the counterfactual perfect, *would have been finished*. Lü Yang is judging completed alternative history.
- It used *whose True Spirit was reconstructed* where the reconstruction is anterior to the hypothetical death and therefore requires *had been reconstructed*.
- It expanded an allowed natural contraction, *would've*, even though only contractions ending in apostrophe-d are forbidden.
- It failed to capitalize the proper group *Overseas True Dragons*.
- It used an unexplained *sexagenary cycle* where the owner prefers *a full sixty-year cycle*.
- It produced avoidably stiff wording, including *the fundamental cast of his nature* and the mirrored *something of you in me and something of me in you*, instead of applying the standing natural-English rule.
- It weakened exact affiliation with *at the Sword Pavilion* instead of *of the Sword Pavilion*.
- Its final review emphasized mechanical compliance and failed to catch these tense, terminology, relationship, and prose defects.
