# World Reference Supplement, Chapter 1246 Onward

## Scope and authority

Read the verified world reference through Chapter 1202, `chatgpt/reference/world-reference-supplement-ch1203-ch1243.md`, and the latest owner-edit supplements before this file. This supplement records only later facts directly stated by the source. The canonical glossary and owner rulings control English terminology.

## Chapter 1246 additions

### The World-Honored One inside the False History

Chapter 1246 confirms that the Buddhist method planted through Chongguang allows the World-Honored One to manifest through the clear-minded Heavenly Lord within the False History. He digests the Heavenly Lord's memories and learns the present state of Jiangxi, the Overseas, Skyward's cooperation with Lü Yang, and the expansion of the God-Sealing Method.

From those facts, the World-Honored One deduces that True Monarch Sovereign of Fate intends to time the False History's return to coincide with the Primordial Saint's Divine Transformation and Ascension, seize control of the Sea of Light, and strike at the Dao Lords. He further infers that Lü Yang has contacted Si Sui and received his approval. This remains the World-Honored One's deduction rather than an independently stated fact.

### True Monarch Radiant World Western Light

Chapter 1246 states that the World-Honored One possesses bodies corresponding to past, present, and future. His present body is True Monarch Radiant World Western Light, located five thousand years before the False History's current point, and this body forms the foundation upon which He proved the Dao. Harm to that foundation can make the World-Honored One fall from the Other Shore.

The present body is only at peak Golden Core. Even if it receives authority over the Heavenly Dao, the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky can suppress it completely in status, prevent it from reversing the roles of guest and host, and control its life and death.

### The World-Honored One's attempted change of course

The report the World-Honored One gives the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky is knowingly fabricated. He claims that the clear-minded heavenly demon refused to soul-search Chongguang, destroyed him, resisted conversion with a Dao Heart, allied with the Grand Sword Patriarch, and damaged His False History incarnation. He then claims a loss of at least one-tenth of His mighty power and requests authority over the Heavenly Dao.

The Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky distrust the report because only the World-Honored One can enter the chaotic False History through the One Mind. They will grant authority only to His present body, thereby taking control of the foundation upon which He proved the Dao.

The World-Honored One no longer objects in principle to losing that foundation. He concludes that He originally chose the Other Shore only because Si Sui's path could not be replicated, but now regards the Other Shore as a broken ship destined to sink. He intends to trade the foundation He plans to abandon for real benefits, jump ship, and seek another path. The exact exchange and timing remain undecided.

## Chapter 1247 additions

### The World-Honored One's timing problem

Chapter 1247 establishes that the World-Honored One still considers the timing of His defection crucial. He is willing to surrender the foundation upon which He proved the Dao, but not before Divine Transformation and Ascension, when the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky could otherwise make Him fall from the Other Shore without granting Him sufficient compensation. He therefore withdraws His request for authority over the Heavenly Dao and continues feigning cooperation while waiting to exchange His foundation for the most valuable possible bargaining chip.

The Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky understand that losing True Monarch Radiant World Western Light would permanently cost the World-Honored One His seat even if the Other Shore were later rebuilt. The Sword Sovereign's Fate cannot calculate what the World-Honored One is hiding because Karma, although an unorthodox path, touches upon Variable. Only the One Mind can currently enter the chaotic False History, leaving the other Dao Lords dependent on the World-Honored One's reports.

### The Other Shore as stepping stones

The World-Honored One describes the Other Shore as a hierarchy of stepping stones. The Primordial Saint stands on all the other Dao Lords. The Sword Sovereign, Vast Sky, and the Spell Dao Lord stand on the World-Honored One and the Spiritual Power Dao Lord. The Spiritual Power Dao Lord also stands on the World-Honored One, while the World-Honored One has no one beneath Him. This prolonged inability to rise beyond the second layer is a major reason He intends to jump ship.

Before committing to Lü Yang's side, however, the World-Honored One decides to meet True Monarch Sovereign of Fate. He will not abandon one broken ship merely to board another vessel already on the verge of sinking.

### Chongguang's Five Elements reconstruction

At Kan-Yang Lake, the World-Honored One offers Chongguang a new road rather than the dead end of Overturned Lamp Fire. Chongguang's Immortal Spirit body contains Yin Xin-Metal. His four perfected divine abilities are Jia-Wood, Yi-Wood, Chen-Earth, and Si-Fire. Because Chen-Earth has been reversed from Yang to Yin, the four divine abilities contain one Yang and three Yin; adding Xin-Metal creates another Yin attribute and leaves the Grotto-Heaven path to Golden Core locked.

The World-Honored One removes Jia-Wood and adds Gui-Water. Chongguang is left with Xin-Metal, Yi-Wood, Gui-Water, Si-Fire, and Chen-Earth. Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, and Earth are all present, and every component is purely Yin. This gives Chongguang a path to seek the Five Elements. The fusion of the five elemental qi and divine abilities produces a dazzling lightning radiance that settles deep within his eyes.

The World-Honored One explicitly uses Chongguang as a piece placed on the board to test True Monarch Sovereign of Fate. If Lü Yang cannot answer the move, the World-Honored One will not entrust His Dao Path to him. Lü Yang senses the resulting change from Spirit Mountain in Jiangxi.

## Chapter 1248 additions

### Lü Yang's regional Heavenly Lord state

After bringing Jiangxi and the Overseas under the God-Sealing Method, Lü Yang can enter a state of unity between Heaven and man while seated on Spirit Mountain. In this state, he functions as another Heavenly Lord within the territory he controls and can search the Immortal Pivot's vast karma through Drop of Heaven's Essence.

### The Five Elements intruder

Lü Yang detects a person sent into the False History from the mortal world, but a stronger mighty power conceals the person before he can expose them. He preserves one weak strand of karma and sees a figure surrounded by the qi of the Five Elements. The qi transforms into lightning and resonates with the Immortal Pivot's Five Elements Fruition Attainments.

The figure is not seeking an individual Fruition Attainment. They are seeking the fundamental Five Elements themselves. With even True Monarch cultivation and the Heavenly Lord's support, such a figure could use four ounces to move a thousand pounds and influence the twelve Water and Fire Fruition Attainments under Lü Yang's control. The connection also gives Lü Yang a potential path to influence the remaining elemental Fruition Attainments through that same figure.

### The River of Time and the Primordial Saint's foundation

Si Sui reveals that the great net of karma was formerly the River of Time. It was the foundation upon which the Primordial Saint proved the Dao. Time always moves forward, and the past is already Destiny and cannot be changed.

The Primordial Saint initially proved Time, an unorthodox path to Destiny, and only much later truly took command of Destiny. By allowing Myriad Treasures to prove Karma and trace time backward to participate in the ancient war, He personally weakened this foundation.

If the World-Honored One were killed and Karma collapsed, the great net of karma would revert to the River of Time. Because Variables have already taken deep root within the net, the Primordial Saint could then use Destiny to command Variables, hold both Daos alone, and remove the final deficiency from His cultivation. Si Sui presents this as the result of Lü Yang's hypothetical, not as an event already guaranteed to occur.

### The World-Honored One's test

Si Sui confirms that the move involving the Five Elements intruder is a test. More precisely, the World-Honored One is testing whether Lü Yang has truly established contact with Si Sui. The concealment method exploits the limited understanding of the River of Time possessed by cultivators below the Dao Lord level, but a cultivator of sufficient status can see through it as easily as turning over a hand.

`天妖` renders as `heavenly demon`, not `celestial demon`, under the Chapter 1246 owner ruling. The Chapter 1246 linked images must remain visible in English: a tail too large to control (`尾大不掉`), sewing the Sword Sovereign's wedding dress (`做嫁衣`), an ant swallowing a dragon (`蝼蚁吞龙`), the Sea of Light becoming a pot of porridge (`乱成一锅粥`), reversing guest and host (`反客为主`), a wrathful Vajra exterior hiding a heart blooming with laughter (`金刚怒目` / `笑开了花`), and the Other Shore as a sinking broken ship from which the World-Honored One means to jump.

Chapter 1248 locks `滴天髓` as `Drop of Heaven's Essence`, superseding the erroneous reference rendering `Dripping Heaven's Marrow`. The four-ounces-and-a-thousand-pounds image in `四两拨千斤` and the hand-turning image in `易如反掌` should remain visible. `时光长河` is drafted as `River of Time` pending any later owner correction.
