# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1275

## The Other Shore collides with the Underworld

The falling Other Shore rains fire across the Sea of Light. Dao Tianqi places the Underworld above the Sea of Light to bear the impact, while the Foundation Establishment Realm rises into the Underworld and diverts part of the falling fire onto itself.

The Other Shore is falling only after Divine Transformation and Ascension. The force of its descent can smash a Dao Lord to a lower realm, bringing it close to overcoming a Dao Lord's usual immortality.

A Dao Lord's Primordial Spirit remains immortal while anchored within the place where that Dao Lord attained the Dao. The Primordial Saint can defeat the World-Honored One easily because of Their enormous difference in status, but He cannot kill the World-Honored One without driving His Primordial Spirit out of the Other Shore.

The Other Shore has far greater mass than the Underworld. The Underworld may be impossible to rebuild if destroyed, while a shattered Other Shore may leave recoverable fragments.

## The Ancestral Dragon's immortality

Unlike Dao Lords who attained Their status through cultivation, the Ancestral Dragon is an Innate Dao God whose Primordial Spirit is anchored in the Sea of Light. He survives as long as any fragment of the Sea of Light remains and may eventually revive.

Because the Other Shore uses the Ancestral Dragon as its foundation, the World-Honored One suspects that it has usurped His broad immortality. This explains why the Dao Lords are willing to drive it into the Underworld.

The World-Honored One urges Dao Tianqi to withdraw if the defense becomes impossible. Si Sui is certain to escape, and the World-Honored One believes Si Sui can bear the brunt of the later conflict.

## Lü Yang bears the falling heavens with the Sea of Light

Dao Tianqi refuses to treat the living beings behind him as helpless recipients of protection. He recognizes that Lü Yang has reached this point not through his own strength, wisdom light, or especially high Status, but through the people governed by the God-Sealing Method.

Lü Yang walks upward from the mortal realm through the Foundation Establishment Realm, the Sea of Suffering, and the Underworld. An endless multitude bears him aloft and raises his status. The sight resembles One Mind but is fundamentally different, leaving the World-Honored One speechless.

Lü Yang asks Dao Tianqi to conserve his strength and leave the remaining burden to the Sea of Light. Dao Tianqi withdraws the nearly shattered Underworld after it has weakened the falling fire by ninety percent.

Thirty stars form a Great Wall above the Sea of Light. Behind them rise four pillars of divine light containing four figures, surrounded by endless pavilions and palaces. Countless subordinate figures extend downward until the entire Sea of Light and all living beings are joined into the defense.

Countless sparks become a prairie fire like the rising sun driving back the long night. The sense of absolute destruction recedes from the living beings of the Sea of Light and is replaced by safety and calm.

## The four-line proclamation

The closing proclamation praises Lü Yang through four linked lines. He rises in the East and ascends the throne, governs the stars under the Mandate of Heaven, commands the gods and pacifies the seas, wields the sword to guard the pivot, endures tribulation and mortal suffering to prove himself a Perfected Person, pours out his crimson heart for all living beings, and remakes the universe to usher in a radiant new spring.
