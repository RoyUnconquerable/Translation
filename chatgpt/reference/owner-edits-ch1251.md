## Reusable prose rulings

- Prefer compact, forceful action over explanatory expansion. Owner-final examples include `wheeling across the heavens and turning the sun`, `threw back his head in laughter`, `marched straight toward the firmament`, and a sword that `tore free of its sheath`.
- Preserve the precise object of an attack. The Eight Negations Wisdom Sword cuts neither `flesh` nor `spells`; it severs `thought`.
- In a formal decree or immediate reaction, `自裁` is `take one's own life`. Analytical narration may use `suicide` when discussing the rule's operation.
- `鸣道惊玄` in this scene is `their Dao resonance stirring countless Mysteries`, preserving both Dao resonance and the effect on Mysteries without the vague phrase `shaking the profound`.
- When several Fruition Attainments are collectively governed, preserve the plural where the source context requires it, as in `the master of the Fruition Attainments`.
- Keep the formation explanation concise and causal: incoming power is dissolved, transformed into Go stones, and the clash is reduced to a contest of Dao Attainment.
- Displayed decrees retain bold corner brackets in the form `**【Decree: ...】**`. This is a specific exception for formal displayed proclamations and does not change the ordinary call-out rule for techniques and artifacts.
- Direct thought remains italicized and generally present tense. Formal cultivator thoughts may still be concise and forceful.
