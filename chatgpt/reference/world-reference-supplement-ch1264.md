# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1264

## The Other Shore's descent

The Other Shore becomes visible not because a Dao Lord reveals it, but because it is falling toward the Sea of Light. As its outline grows, the Sea of Light groans under the strain. The Immortal Pivot, Celestial Court, Star Palace, Mysterious Wall, and Boundary Heavens great and small suffer collapsing mountains, surging seas, the extinction of spiritual qi, and falling sun and moon.

The Other Shore was previously as elusive as a divine dragon whose head could be seen but never its tail. Preserve this head-and-tail image when describing its former invisibility.

## The Underworld replaces the Five Elements

Dao Tianqi opens the Underworld beneath the Other Shore. Endless death qi surges forth and forms pillars that prop up the heavens. These pillars take the place of the Five Elements, halt the Other Shore's descent, and spare the worlds and billions of living beings below.

The Primordial Saint deliberately created this outcome. He knew Beast-Taming could not allow the Sea of Light to be destroyed while he possessed the power to prevent it. The other Dao Lords will support Dao Tianqi because his intervention benefits Them, preventing the World-Honored One from removing or dissuading him.

This open scheme resolves two parts of Divine Transformation and Ascension at once. Tribulation answers the Ancestral Dragon, while the Underworld replaces the hollowed-out Five Elements as the support beneath the Other Shore.

## Flying Snow's disappearance

The Ancestral Dragon intends to possess True Monarch Flying Snow again through the Usurpation of the Huai. Her Empty Attainment of Flowing Downward would serve as a buffer for drawing upon Tribulation's imagery and using it as fuel for Divine Transformation and Ascension.

At Sacred Fire Cliff, the Ancestral Dragon finds only Flying Snow's empty body. Her soul and Flowing Downward have disappeared. Residual crimson light between her brows reveals that someone drew her away.

The Primordial Saint deduces that this unknown person escaped the Heavenly Dao's restrictions and built a Dao-attainment ground in the Fathomless Void. The arrangement was likely placed during the earlier event in the Overseas and resembles Bu Tianque's methods, but the Primordial Saint rules out Myriad Treasures.

The possibility of a seventh person beyond the six present Dao Lords leads the Primordial Saint to reconsider the mysterious lesser cultivator whose consciousness entered the False History. He identifies the situation as a Variable and descends from the seventh layer of the Other Shore to the sixth.
