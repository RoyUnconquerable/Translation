# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1266

## The Primordial Saint discovers the Heavenly Palace

The World-Honored One's abandonment of His Dao Lord position shocks the Primordial Saint and halts the Other Shore's ascent. The Primordial Saint briefly displays anger, confusion, and shock before expelling those emotions and restoring His usual calm.

By tracing the connection through True Monarch Flying Snow's body, which is occupied by the Ancestral Dragon, the Primordial Saint looks into the Fathomless Void. His Primordial Spirit thoughts illuminate it and reveal the Heavenly Palace, an ordered divine realm resembling a fifth super Boundary Heaven but standing above the mundane world.

The Primordial Saint's eyes contain the full range of mortal emotions. The Sword Sovereign recognizes this as a further advance in His Primordial Spirit attainments, now beyond even Si Sui's.

## The Heavenly Emperor's origin and limitation

The Primordial Saint immediately recognizes Traversing the Mortal Dust and identifies the Heavenly Emperor as a single consciousness separated from another consciousness in the False History. The Heavenly Emperor's temperament resembles the Primordial Saint's: he seeks only the Dao, regards all else as false, and will stop at nothing.

Because Divine Transformation and Ascension has carried the Dao Lords far from the mortal world, the Primordial Saint cannot physically touch the Heavenly Palace. He can only transmit His voice.

The Primordial Saint offers the Heavenly Emperor the Seven Emotions Heaven-Fathoming Art. It first awakens the seven emotions, then casts them aside one by one until the cultivator reaches the Dao Heart state of "Heaven and Earth are not benevolent." The method would allow the Heavenly Emperor to cease being merely the good consciousness, develop humanity and a True Self, and pursue a Primordial Spirit independently. Lü Yang's true body would bear the resulting failure of his own Primordial Spirit cultivation.

The Heavenly Emperor's decisive limitation is lack of talent. He has no wisdom light and cannot understand the offered method. Lü Yang anticipated this and therefore had no reason to fear that his separated consciousness would betray him for the Primordial Saint's offer.
