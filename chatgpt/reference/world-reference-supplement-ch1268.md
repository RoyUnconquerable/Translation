# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1268

## The World-Honored One calls Variables

Lü Yang recognizes that the Book of a Hundred Lives may itself be seeking completeness and therefore gave him the highly targeted Prismatic Talent A Guest Rejected by Heaven and Earth through settling Skyward's experiences. The talent anchors Lü Yang's course within the False History when the Sword Sovereign restarts it.

The World-Honored One had prepared Karma as a response to the Sword Sovereign's hidden method, but Lü Yang's talent saves Him that effort. He instead draws upon every monk and believer cultivating His Buddhist Dharma. Their chants connect the False History and the True History, allowing Karma to expand and call toward Variables.

The World-Honored One's abandonment of His Dao Lord position, the halted Divine Transformation and Ascension, the returning False History, and Karma's call collectively cause a qualitative transformation in the gathered imagery of Variables. The Great Dao of Variables reveals a hazy outline.

## The False History compressed into one instant

Variables compresses the entire 129,600-year False History into a singularity. Past, present, and future churn together while cause and effect exist all at once. The history can span its full length or last no more than one instant, allowing all its accumulation to be condensed into the present moment.

Lü Yang skips the process and directly reaches the result 129,600 years later.

## The False History after one hundred thousand years

Thirty stars representing the Fruition Attainments of the Five Elements replace the extinguished sun and moon above the Immortal Pivot. Gods govern the mountains and rivers, while common people offer incense and raise the gods through their worship. Prosperous worshippers strengthen the gods, forming a subtle symbiotic system among people and gods under the God-Sealing Method.

Chongguang has lived more than one hundred thousand years since forming his Golden Core through the Five Elements. He prefers this stable False History to the True History of the real world because cultivation no longer requires constant intrigue and mortal danger.

True Monarch Sovereign of Fate remains celebrated like the sun at the Immortal Pivot's summit. His conquest of the Four Seas, Jiangdong, Jiangxi, and Jiangbei, establishment of the divine order, enfeoffment of the gods of heaven and earth, and creation of ninety-nine provinces have become the defining historical record.

## The False History returns

Chongguang and countless cultivators in the Sea of Light's real world simultaneously remember the sealed history of True Monarch Sovereign of Fate. Cause and effect altered by the prior history begin returning to their original form. The Destiny radiance anchoring the current True History shatters, leaving the False History's return unobstructed.
