# Owner Style Rulings: Chapter 1264

The owner's edited Chapter 1264 prose is governing. Apply these choices to later chapters where the same terms, imagery, or narrative situations recur.

## Natural catastrophic register

- In scenes of mass destruction, prefer serious but direct wording. The Boundary Heavens beneath the Other Shore are *the most unfortunate of all*, and destruction is already *bearing down upon them*. Avoid casual wording such as *unluckiest* when the scene concerns billions of imminent deaths.
- Let the physical action carry the scale: death qi *surged forth*, became pillars that *propped up the heavens*, *halted* the Other Shore's descent, and *spared* billions from being crushed.
- Use clean participial structure when it clarifies simultaneous action: *Taking the place of the Five Elements beneath the Other Shore, they halted its descent...*
- The owner-final register remains modern and readable even in cosmic scenes. Grandeur comes from the Other Shore, collapsing worlds, the Underworld, and the imagery itself, not from archaic syntax.

## Idiom and image chains

- `神龙见首不见尾` is *as elusive as a divine dragon, its head visible but never its tail*. Preserve the head-and-tail image rather than reducing it to *mysterious* or *elusive* alone.
- `四两拨千斤` remains the standalone beat *Four ounces moving a thousand pounds. That was all there was to it.*
- Preserve the linked effortless-control sequence: the Primordial Saint had not taken *so much as a single step down*, yet *effortlessly* turned the situation to His advantage, *lifting the heaviest burden as though it weighed nothing*, *as simple as turning over His palm*.
- These images work as one progression. Do not translate the four ounces, heavy burden, and turning palm as unrelated generic claims that something was easy.

## Reactions and observation

- After the World-Honored One exhales, use *Once He let out that breath* and allow every trace of emotion to vanish from His eyes. Do not force a cutting action when the source's result is more important than the mechanism.
- `目瞪口呆` is *stared in disbelief* in this scene. Do not force *slack-jawed* when it makes the reaction sound comic or overly literal.
- The Primordial Saint studies the crimson light with *countless deductions flashing through His eyes*, then *gazed into the vast and boundless Fathomless Void*.

## Terminology and thought voice

- `下修` is *lesser cultivator* in the Primordial Saint's private question.
- Preserve the sequence of compact direct thoughts: the Fathomless Void realization, the questions of method and timing, the rejection of Myriad Treasures, the possibility of a seventh person, and the final *Or...*
- `成道地` is *Dao-attainment ground*, `夺淮之变` is *Usurpation of the Huai*, and `润下` is *Flowing Downward*.
- Preserve the hard scene break before *Meanwhile, the Other Shore shook.* The viewpoint shifts from the World-Honored One's analysis to the Ancestral Dragon's next step.
