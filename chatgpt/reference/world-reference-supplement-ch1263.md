# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1263

## Dao Tianqi's exploding pill

Dao Tianqi altered a few strands of the Nascent Soul Pill's imagery at Dragon-Tiger Mountain, turning it into an exploding pill. The pill formula and the Cangjiang Dragon Monarch's refinement remained correct, allowing the change to escape the Primordial Saint's inspection. The pill detonates only after the Primordial Saint takes it to the Other Shore.

Dao Tianqi required three days to design the altered imagery and several months to practice his false display of anger. The performance allowed Him to deceive both the Cangjiang Dragon Monarch and the Primordial Saint until the pill reached its target.

## The Primordial Saint's methods

The Primordial Saint acknowledges that His comprehension is one step below Dao Tianqi and Si Sui. He compensates through plots and schemes. His current position arose step by step: He schemed against the Ancestral Dragon, used the Ancestral Dragon against Si Sui, and then used Si Sui against the Three Foundations.

The Nascent Soul Pill was the planned means of drawing out the Tribulation for Divine Transformation and Ascension. Its destruction initially brings the Ancestral Dragon's clockwork plan to a halt, but the Primordial Saint has prepared for any link in an elaborate plan to fail.

## The Dark Side and the Heavenly Dao

The Primordial Saint infers from clues as faint as spider silk and horse tracks that the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky altered the Five Elements. The fact that repeated inspections reveal nothing leads Him to identify Yin and Yang as the concealment method. Bu Tianque is hidden within the Heavenly Dao on the Dark Side of the Sea of Light, at the level of consciousness, and remains beyond even the Primordial Saint's dharma eyes.

The Primordial Saint strips away the Five Elements' outer shell and exposes the Heavenly Dao forged within it by the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky. Every visible arrangement is laid bare, but Bu Tianque remains unseen. The Primordial Saint therefore tears the entire Heavenly Dao out of the Five Elements and leaves the Great Dao's empty shell behind.

## The Other Shore's collapse and Tribulation

The Heavenly Dao had filled the vacancy inside the hollowed-out Five Elements and helped it support the Other Shore. Once removed, the Five Elements can no longer bear that weight. The Other Shore begins to collapse, threatening to destroy the Sea of Light.

The Five Elements is the Great Dao governed by the Ancestral Dragon. Because the impending destruction is caused by its failure, the karma of that destruction rests upon Him. The resulting great tribulation is sufficient for Tribulation to manifest in the Sea of Suffering and favor the Ancestral Dragon once more, filling the void left by the Nascent Soul Pill's destruction.

The World-Honored One identifies the maneuver as drinking poison to quench one's thirst. It restores Tribulation to the ascension plan while removing the Heavenly Dao that had filled the Five Elements' vacancy, leaving no apparent support for Divine Transformation and Ascension.
