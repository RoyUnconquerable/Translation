# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1278

## Du Xuan descends

The Spiritual Power Dao manifests across the Sea of Suffering like a towering tree. Its crown connects every cultivator in the Sea of Light, while its roots are anchored in the Spiritual Power Dao Lord, Du Xuan.

Du Xuan has fallen from the Other Shore to peak Golden Core, but He retains 129,600 years of cultivation, an exceptional status at that level, and a fragment of the Other Shore. He expects His authority over the Spiritual Power Dao to strip every cultivator of spiritual power.

The God-Sealing Method prevents even the Star Palace's Spiritual Power Dao True Monarchs from responding to Du Xuan. Lü Yang has hollowed out the old Dao Lineage and made its surviving institution an empty shell.

Du Xuan attacks by making Lü Yang's own spiritual power answer Him and form an unavoidable palm. The attack disrupts the circulation of Lü Yang's spiritual power and drives the Mysteries powered by it out of control.

## Lü Yang becomes the Sea of Light

Lü Yang dissolves into the Sea of Light. He no longer needs personal spiritual power because the omnipresent spiritual qi of heaven and earth serves as his power, and every Great Dao within the Sea of Light answers him like his own limbs.

An eye formed from Yin-Yang Creation and Destruction Profound Light, with several Boundary Heavens composing its pupil, reveals the scale of Lü Yang's union with the Sea of Light.

The Five Elements form five fingers and then a fist through Heaven in a Palm. Divine Offerings, Drop of Heaven's Essence, the Sea of Dust and Tribulation, and Fate-Reforming reinforce it. Heavenly Fire, Rampart Earth, Sword-Edge Metal, Great Ocean Water, and Great Forest Wood respond as the five Supreme Fruition Attainments.

The resulting fist seal encompasses the Five Elements, the Heavenly Numbers, the Spiritual Power Dao, and the Spell Dao of Myriad Spells. It is the source and origin of every Dao in the Sea of Light, and cultivators who observe it can comprehend spells or even divine abilities.

The clash is a contest between Primordial Spirits as well as Mysteries, spiritual power, and status. Lü Yang shatters the ocean imagery of Du Xuan's Primordial Spirit, draws a Dao Lord's blood, strikes His true body, and sends Him flying out of the Sea of Light.

The Sword Sovereign, Vast Sky, and the Spell Dao Lord study the fist from the Other Shore. After Du Xuan is expelled, the Dao Lords identify Lü Yang as True Monarch Sovereign of Fate and recognize him as an opponent worthy of contending over the Dao.
