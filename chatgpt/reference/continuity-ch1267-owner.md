# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1267 Owner Final

The Heavenly Emperor rejects the Primordial Saint's offer because he cannot understand the Seven Emotions Heaven-Fathoming Art. The Primordial Saint never considers this possibility, assuming the creator of the Heavenly Palace must possess exceptional wisdom light and comprehension. He instead interprets the refusal as another inexplicable departure from normal self-interest after Beast-Taming and Myriad Treasures changed.

The Primordial Saint considers either continuing Divine Transformation and Ascension or abandoning it to eliminate the Heavenly Palace. He chooses to continue because the plan carries His genuine hopes of reaching the upper realm of Divine Transformation. His pursuit of the Great Dao is the last fragment of His true nature and His True Self.

Descending from the sixth layer of the Other Shore to the fifth, the Primordial Saint uses Destiny to anchor the Sea of Light's current True History. He freezes the inferno from the World-Honored One's passing, the Great Net of Karma, and the False History, temporarily halting its return despite the flood of Variables.

The Primordial Saint buys the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky time to use the Heavenly Dao to fill Karma's vacant position. He threatens to release the False History and let the Heavenly Dao collapse if They refuse, which would force both sides to start again.

The World-Honored One remains within the False History and retains control of Karma, but His cultivation and status have fallen to peak Golden Core. Without His Dao Lord status, He no longer acts as an immovable anchor limiting the Heavenly Dao.

The Heavenly Dao spreads across the False History like an enormous spiderweb, collapses its full 129,600 years, rolls it back to the instant after the war between the Dao Lords, and begins it anew. Emperor Cang, the six Dragon Monarchs, the Heavenly Lord, and the Grand Sword Patriarch reappear like dreams, illusions, bubbles, and shadows given new life.

The Sword Sovereign expects to reshape the restarted False History at will, but it exactly reproduces its prior course. Within the Immortal Pivot, Lü Yang has activated the Prismatic Talent A Guest Rejected by Heaven and Earth, making his history unique and immune to every external force.
