# Owner Style Rulings, Chapter 1254 Onward

Read after the core style guide and earlier owner-style supplements. The owner's Chapter 1254 prose overrides provisional wording.

## Technical narration
- Prefer natural English causality over literal clause stacking. Strong owner-final patterns include `This was what was known as Heavenly Fire`, `forcing him to his knees`, and `revealing to everyone exactly what was suppressing him`.
- Keep compact explanatory transitions and avoid over-formal wording when a direct phrase is clearer.
- In combat, preserve physical pressure and consequence with concrete verbs rather than abstract description.

## Displayed legal penalties
- Formal Inscribing the Heavenly Chapter penalties may appear as bold corner-bracketed labels even when they are not full decree sentences: `**【Beheading, Pending Review】**`, `**【Beheading, Immediate Execution!】**`.
- This extends the Chapter 1251 displayed-decree exception to formal judicial penalty labels. Ordinary techniques and artifacts remain plain call-outs.

## Internal thought
- Italicize unmistakable internal monologue even when the Chinese omits explicit thought quotation marks, including extended self-comparison or resolution at a chapter ending.
- Keep the speaker's ego, humor, and register. Do not flatten self-address such as `I, Lü Yang` into neutral narration.

## Humor and meta register
- Preserve recurring internet/gaming metaphors when the source deliberately uses them. `炸鱼` is `smurf / smurfing` in this arc.
- Preserve the joke chain around smurfing, dimensional suppression, and the `fish` having a chance to retaliate. Do not replace it with solemn xianxia diction.
- Naturalize the English while retaining the source's tonal collision between epic cultivation and irreverent modern slang.

## Owner-final rhythm
- Prefer `remnant shadow` for 残痕留影 in this context.
- Prefer `bringing him into the fold` over bureaucratic phrasing when describing recruitment into Lü Yang's system.
- Strong physical phrasing such as `so crushing it seemed to have reached the very limit of what pressure could be` is acceptable when the source explicitly pushes intensity to an extreme.
