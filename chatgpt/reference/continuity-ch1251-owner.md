## Chapter 1251: March on Jiangbei

Lü Yang advances from Jiangxi upon Jiangbei and the Heaven-Connecting Sea of Clouds with the twelve Water and Fire Fruition Attainment stars. Lian Tiandou and Bu Tianque appear on Sacred Fire Cliff to meet him. Bu Tianque activates the Thirty-Three Heavens Vertical and Horizontal Go Formation, which dissolves incoming external power, converts it into Go stones, and reduces the confrontation to a contest of Dao Attainment.

Lü Yang answers with Illuminating Law through Overturned Lamp Fire. Its radiance exposes every node, operating principle, flaw, and defect in the formation, allowing the other eleven Fruition Attainments to destroy the grand formation in fewer than three breaths.

Lian Tiandou attacks directly. The Profound Heaven Upright Crown briefly freezes the twelve stars, after which he crosses the Fruition Attainment light and strikes with the Eight Negations Wisdom Sword. A three-legged bronze cauldron simultaneously pours Purple-Azure Tushita Fire over Lü Yang. The sword severs thought rather than flesh or spells, while the fire ignites Lü Yang's body, spiritual power, Mysteries, and imagery.

Lü Yang escapes through Celestial River Immortal and sheds the flames with the Sea of Dust and Tribulation. Lian Tiandou recognizes the first as a Heavenly River Water Mystery and judges the second to be tied to Lü Yang's Empty Attainment Great Dao.

Heavenly Fire then becomes sovereign of the twelve Fruition Attainments and issues a displayed decree through Inscribing the Heavenly Chapter, ordering Lian Tiandou to take his own life. The decree rewrites the logic of every action concerning him so that healing, protection, and resistance all lead toward suicide. Swallowing medicine becomes poisoning himself, Bu Tianque's attempt to stabilize his aura becomes a killing strike, and using the jade fly-whisk to sweep away hostile Mysteries also sweeps away Lian Tiandou's own powers.

Once every treasure and Mystery falls silent, Lü Yang seizes Lian Tiandou with Heaven in a Palm.
