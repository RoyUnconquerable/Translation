## Meaning, scope and mechanics

- Degree is not interchangeable: difficulty versus impossibility, certainty
  versus magnitude, deadly versus unmistakable, actual success versus merely
  achieving something. Ch.1306 lost further repair and called an injured Saint
  unharmed. Keep conditional comparisons conditional and escalation explicit.
- Ch.1293: touching existing imagery does not create it. Ch.1295: almost no
  hesitation is not none; attempting interception does not establish success
  or a new sealing method. A sense of pressure does not imply crushing force.
- Ch.1306: entering a world matters because its available Daos differ. Refusal
  to tolerate recovery is not impatience; inevitable is not almost certain.
- Ch.1309/1318: preserve actual reading, posture and smaller cultivation
  problems. Almost felt weakens actually felt. Conception differs from gestation;
  the first Transcendence is an event, not the first transcender as a person.
- Ch.1326: all knowledge must not become only knowledge from the threads.
  Coming to one's senses triggers backlash; escaping is not its prerequisite.
  Keep active borrowed wisdom light distinct from merely having it available.
- Ch.1329: overlapping is not fusion; the domain differs from its surrounding
  void. A source emotion does not establish a visible trembling motion. An
  advantage from thirty to fifty percent is twenty percentage points.

## Referential and lexical traps

- His intervention and his injuries may refer to different people. His
  teacher's ruler invents an owner for a disciplinary implement. Clarify who
  acts, feels, owns or serves as material without adding another artifact.
- Knowing a method means understanding/using it when the scene relies on that
  competence; do not weaken it to knowing the method exists.
- Substrings need grammar: 成道主 is become a Dao Lord, not prove the Dao;
  现世 may be a verb; 心中古怪 does not name an era. Bare and named variants,
  ordinary life versus nature-and-life, and generic variables versus Variables
  follow the glossary's narrow contexts. Never insert a name to satisfy a scan.
- New approved terms/casing outrank draft choices. The fixed survival phrase,
  Sword Seed, explicit-only Dao Lord title and Dao resonance remain in their
  canonical mappings. A dated attachment cannot reverse a newer owner ruling.
- Preserve source strength and actual allusion relationships. A death/survival
  idiom need not imply literal rebirth or a particular weapon. Ch.1295's spent
  crossbow bolt was a draft over-literalism; use spent force in that context.
