## Chapter 1249 additions

### The Primordial Saint's fallback involving the World-Honored One

Chapter 1249 has Lü Yang infer why the Primordial Saint wanted to kill the World-Honored One two lives ago. If Karma collapses with the World-Honored One's death, the great net of karma returns to the River of Time. The Primordial Saint could then use Destiny to command the Variables already rooted within it, holding both Daos and removing the final deficiency from His cultivation.

Lü Yang interprets the World-Honored One as a handpicked Dao Lord-grade piece of material and the Primordial Saint's greatest fallback after a failed Divine Transformation and Ascension. This is Lü Yang's deduction, not an independently confirmed statement of the Primordial Saint's intent.

### The precision of the Primordial Saint's plan

Si Sui judges the Primordial Saint's arrangement too precise to resemble an ordinary plan. Variables stand first among the Great Daos of the Sea of Light, so planning and execution should naturally diverge. The Primordial Saint nevertheless proved Time through Empty Attainment, gave it form as the River of Time, and later allowed Myriad Treasures to prove Karma, transform the river into the great net of karma, and indirectly draw Variables into it.

Because the Primordial Saint normally acts only with complete certainty, Si Sui suspects He knew in advance that Myriad Treasures would prove Karma rather than gambling the foundation upon which He proved the Dao. This remains Si Sui's inference.

### Grandmaster and the seven Remnant Consciousnesses

Lü Yang identifies `Grandmaster` as one of the seven Heavenly Person's Remnant Consciousnesses and says it has reportedly remained under the Primordial Saint's control. `Grandmaster` is a provisional rendering of `大宗师` pending owner approval.

Dao Tianqi suspected that the Primordial Saint's single-handed Empty Attainment of four Daos, Alchemy, Artifact Refinement, Dual Cultivation, and Beast-Taming, was connected to Grandmaster. Lü Yang further suspects that Grandmaster contains a Divine Transformation inheritance or decisive evidence proving the existence of that higher realm. These points are character deductions, not yet confirmed facts.

### Dao Tianqi and the Underworld

Lü Yang tells Si Sui that roughly forty thousand years after the Dao Lord war, Dao Tianqi proved the Underworld. The Dao Lords joined forces to cast him down, after which he remained sunken for tens of thousands of years. Lü Yang only recently awakened him. The Underworld's great undertaking remains incomplete and confined to the Immortal Pivot rather than extending across the Sea of Light as originally envisioned.

### Grandmaster's suspected hiding place

Lü Yang and Si Sui reason that the Primordial Saint would hide His most important secret where any flaw created by Variables remained within His own sight. They identify the River of Time, the foundation upon which He proved the Dao, as the most likely location.

When the World-Honored One later proved Karma and transformed the River of Time into the great net of karma, anything hidden there gained a second layer of concealment. Under this theory, finding Grandmaster would require the World-Honored One's death so Karma collapsed, but Dao Lords remain immortal while the Other Shore stands. This is a joint deduction rather than a confirmed location.

### The Book of a Hundred Lives

After reconstructing the Primordial Saint's suspected arrangement, Lü Yang immediately checks the Book of a Hundred Lives and is relieved that he never closed it. The reason for that relief is not yet explained in Chapter 1249.
