## Chapter 1249 additions

### The Primordial Saint's fallback involving the World-Honored One

Chapter 1249 has Lü Yang infer why the Primordial Saint wanted to kill the World-Honored One two lives ago. If Karma collapses with the World-Honored One's death, the great net of karma returns to the River of Time. The Primordial Saint could then use Destiny to command the Variables already rooted within it, holding both Daos and removing the final deficiency from His cultivation.

Lü Yang interprets the World-Honored One as a handpicked Dao Lord-grade talent and the Primordial Saint's greatest fallback after a failed Divine Transformation and Ascension. This is Lü Yang's deduction, not an independently confirmed statement of the Primordial Saint's intent.

### The precision of the Primordial Saint's plan

Si Sui judges the Primordial Saint's arrangement too ingenious to resemble an ordinary plan. Variables stand first among the Great Daos of the Sea of Light, so planning and execution should naturally diverge. The Primordial Saint nevertheless attained Time through Empty Attainment, gave it form as the River of Time, and later allowed Myriad Treasures to prove Karma, transform the river into the great net of karma, and indirectly draw Variables into it.

Because the Primordial Saint normally acts only with complete certainty, Si Sui suspects He knew in advance that Myriad Treasures would prove Karma rather than gambling the foundation upon which He proved the Dao. This remains Si Sui's inference.

### Grand Ancestral Master and the seven Remnant Consciousnesses

`大宗师` is locked as `Grand Ancestral Master`. It is a place or realm among the seven Heavenly Person's Remnant Consciousnesses, not a person, cultivator, title, or generic grandmaster. Its name is drawn from the chapter `大宗师` of the *Zhuangzi*, parallel to other Remnant Consciousness place-names derived from that text.

Lü Yang says Grand Ancestral Master has reportedly remained under the Primordial Saint's control. Dao Tianqi suspected that the Primordial Saint's single-handed Empty Attainment of four Daos, Alchemy, Artifact Refinement, Dual Cultivation, and Beast-Taming, was connected to Grand Ancestral Master. Lü Yang further suspects that the realm contains a Divine Transformation inheritance or decisive evidence proving the existence of that higher realm. These points are character deductions, not yet confirmed facts.

### Dao Tianqi and the Underworld

Lü Yang tells Si Sui that roughly forty thousand years after the Dao Lord war, Dao Tianqi proved the Underworld. The Dao Lords joined forces to cast him down, after which he remained sunken for tens of thousands of years. Lü Yang only recently awakened him. The Underworld's great undertaking remains incomplete and confined to the Immortal Pivot rather than extending across the Sea of Light as originally envisioned.

### Grand Ancestral Master's suspected hiding place

Lü Yang and Si Sui reason that the Primordial Saint would hide His most important secret where any flaw created by Variables remained within His own sight. They identify the River of Time, the foundation upon which He proved the Dao, as the most likely location.

When the World-Honored One later proved Karma and transformed the River of Time into the great net of karma, anything hidden there gained a second layer of concealment. Under this theory, finding Grand Ancestral Master would require the World-Honored One's death so Karma collapsed, but Dao Lords remain immortal while the Other Shore stands. This is a joint deduction rather than a confirmed location.

### The Book of a Hundred Lives

After reconstructing the Primordial Saint's suspected arrangement, Lü Yang immediately checks the Book of a Hundred Lives and is relieved that he never closed it. The reason for that relief is not yet explained in Chapter 1249.

## Chapter 1250 additions

### Identification of the World-Honored One's test piece

Si Sui infers that the Dao Lord's test piece is only a Foundation Establishment True Person from the Immortal Pivot in the True History, but one with enough talent and brilliance to bypass individual Fruition Attainments and seek the fundamental Five Elements. This higher-level inference lets Lü Yang identify Chongguang.

Lü Yang states that, apart from Demon-Purging True Person, Chongguang ranks first among the Immortal Pivot's Foundation Establishment cultivators and serves as the Saint Sect's acting sect master. Across Lü Yang's restarts, Chongguang has reached the True Monarch realm three times. In one life, he rapidly comprehended the previously unfamiliar Heart Demon Dao and used it to advance. These are Lü Yang's remembered observations across lives.

### Chongguang's Immortal Spirit origin and Jiangbei

Lü Yang reasons that because Chongguang was an Immortal Spirit in his previous life, the karma of his original Immortal Spirit body still exists in the False History's ancient era. Immortal Spirit gestation is measured in ten-thousand-year spans. Chongguang's reincarnated Saint Sect identity also implies that his original Immortal Spirit body was born in Jiangbei.

Lü Yang further reasons that the Heavenly Lord would naturally focus on an Immortal Spirit. The Heavenly Lord's intervention against Lü Yang's earlier karmic calculation, combined with the One Mind's ability to control consciousness, leads Lü Yang to conclude that the World-Honored One has taken control of the Heavenly Lord and selected Chongguang as His piece. This remains Lü Yang's deduction.

### Lü Yang's next territorial objective

After three years spent bringing Jiangxi and the Overseas into order, Lü Yang decides to seize Jiangbei. He expects Chongguang to avoid populated places because he lacks Lü Yang's means of hiding an Immortal Spirit among ordinary cultivators. Once Jiangbei falls under Lü Yang's command, he considers locating Chongguang's original body only a matter of time.

### Lian Tiandou's doubts about the False History

At the Heaven-Connecting Sea of Clouds, Lian Tiandou predicts that Lü Yang will target Jiangbei rather than Jiangnan. Jiangdong and Jiangnan are closely linked, and the Grand Sword Patriarch's presence in Jiangnan remains a deterrent. Lian Tiandou decides to fight Lü Yang because he suspects Lü Yang may hold answers to questions troubling him. Bu Tianque agrees to stand with him.

Lian Tiandou questions the memories that Eldest Senior Brother died and Little Junior Brother disappeared. He silently asks what exists beyond the Immortal Pivot and why the many True Monarchs and Dao Lineages remain confined to it without attempting to enter the Sea of Light. The world in his memories begins to seem illusory. The source does not yet explain the cause of this inconsistency.
