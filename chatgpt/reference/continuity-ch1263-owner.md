# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1263 Owner Final

An endless storm of light erupts across the Other Shore when the Nascent Soul Pill explodes. The sight stuns every Dao Lord. Once the World-Honored One understands what Dao Tianqi has done, He laughs openly at the Primordial Saint with undisguised mockery and delight.

The Primordial Saint swallows the explosion's light with boundless spiritual power and praises Beast-Taming for finally changing his nature, learning schemes and stratagems, and completing his apprenticeship. He determines that the pill formula and the Cangjiang Dragon Monarch's refinement were both correct, leaving Dragon-Tiger Mountain as the only possible point of alteration.

Dao Tianqi explains that he changed only a few strands of imagery. Altering the Nascent Soul Pill into an exploding pill took him three days, while practicing a convincing display of anger took months. He uses the moment to tell the Primordial Saint directly that He lacks the talent to understand the change and would only be a mediocre man making trouble for Himself by dwelling on it.

The Primordial Saint calmly admits that His comprehension falls one step short of Dao Tianqi and Si Sui. He compensates through plots and schemes. Step by step, He schemed against the Ancestral Dragon, used the Ancestral Dragon against Si Sui, and used Si Sui against the Three Foundations to build His current advantage.

In the True History's Overseas, the Ancestral Dragon has just possessed the Old Dragon Monarch. The Nascent Soul Pill's destruction sends Him into a rage because the plan for drawing out the Tribulation, previously running with clockwork precision, has ground to a halt. The Primordial Saint orders Him to wait and warns that elaborate plans must allow for any link in the chain to fail.

At the summit of the Other Shore, the Primordial Saint examines the Five Elements seal. Clues as faint as spider silk and horse tracks have already led Him to suspect the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky, but He has yet to uncover anything concrete. The absence of visible evidence strengthens His conclusion that Heaven-Mending concealed herself on the Dark Side of the Sea of Light through Yin and Yang at the level of consciousness.

The Primordial Saint strips away the Five Elements' outer shell and exposes the Heavenly Dao forged by the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky. Bu Tianque remains invisible within it, but the Primordial Saint forcibly tears the entire Heavenly Dao out of the Five Elements, leaving only the empty shell of the Great Dao.

Without the Heavenly Dao filling its vacancy, the hollowed-out Five Elements can no longer support the Other Shore. The Other Shore begins to collapse, placing the Sea of Light on the brink of destruction. Tribulation manifests as light and shadow within the Sea of Suffering and turns toward the Ancestral Dragon because the Five Elements is His Great Dao and the karma of the impending destruction rests upon Him.

The Ancestral Dragon regains Tribulation's favor, filling the void left by the Nascent Soul Pill's destruction. On the Other Shore's second layer, the World-Honored One recognizes that this is drinking poison to quench one's thirst. Removing the Heavenly Dao restores Tribulation to the plan but leaves the Five Elements empty, raising the question of what will support Divine Transformation and Ascension.
