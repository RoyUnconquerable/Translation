# Owner Style Rulings: Chapter 1275

The owner's edited Chapter 1275 prose is governing. Apply its preference for explicit spatial relationships, concrete metaphysical mechanics, strong physical progression, and elevated but intelligible classical verse.

## Spatial and causal clarity

- State the cosmological geography directly. The palace carries *the aura of the Underworld* and spreads across *the highest reaches of the Sea of Light*.
- When one realm supports another, identify both the direction and the burden: the Foundation Establishment Realm *rose into the Underworld* and diverted part of the falling fire *onto itself*.
- Use *Buddhist chant* for the resounding `佛号` in this scene. When `深深吐气` marks restrained concern, prefer the concrete action *let out a deep breath* over a more clinical *exhaled deeply*.
- Describe the Other Shore as *crashing down in its entirety*. Its descent can smash a Dao Lord to a lower realm, making the threat to immortality concrete.
- `成道地` and `成道之地` are rendered contextually as *the place where [the subject] attained the Dao*. This plain-language form supersedes *Dao-attainment ground* in future chapter prose.
- Preserve causal mechanics without vague compression. The Ancestral Dragon's Primordial Spirit is anchored in the Sea of Light, a surviving fragment permits His return, and the Other Shore may have usurped that immortality because He forms its foundation.

## Character appraisal and collective strength

- In the explicit appraisal *his Status isn't particularly high*, capitalize *Status*. The later dynamic phrase *driving his status ever higher* remains lowercase in the owner's prose, so this ruling is construction-specific rather than a blanket capitalization change.
- Prefer the direct contrast *he was able to come this far not through his own strength*, followed by the isolated answer that it was because of the people behind him.
- Physical progression should remain easy to picture: Lü Yang's footsteps *beat like the pulse of Heaven and Earth*, scarlet spreads in his wake, an endless multitude bears him aloft, and his status rises.
- Use *left the World-Honored One speechless* for the immediate emotional consequence. The comparison is *It resembled One Mind*, followed by the complete tube-and-leopard image and the contrast between a faint trace and the power appearing *in its entirety*.
- When presenting a visual array, identify its members before its surroundings: four figures stand among the pillars of divine light, then endless pavilions and palaces spread around them.
- The thirty aligned stars build a majestic *Great Wall*. Preserve the culturally specific image rather than reducing it to a generic barrier.

## Broader stylistic direction

- Favor explicit destinations, burdens, and physical consequences over abstract verbs. *Rose into*, *divert onto itself*, *crashing down*, *bore him aloft*, and *appeared before Him in its entirety* all make the action legible.
- Repeat a location or actor when doing so prevents ambiguity. Avoid pronoun compression in cosmological scenes involving several nested realms or multiple Dao Lords.
- Refine formal verse for meaning first, then cadence. Preserve its allusions and parallel structure without turning it into either a literal gloss or generic fantasy poetry.
