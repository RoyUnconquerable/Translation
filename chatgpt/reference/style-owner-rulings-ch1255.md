# Owner Style Rulings, Chapter 1255 Onward

Read after the core style guide and prior owner-style supplements. The owner's Chapter 1255 revision is governing.

## Paragraph structure is mandatory

- Preserve the raw paragraph structure exactly: one English paragraph for each Chinese source paragraph, in identical order.
- Do not merge adjacent source paragraphs even when the English reads more smoothly as one paragraph.
- Do not split a source paragraph into multiple English paragraphs unless the source itself contains a scene-break convention already covered by project rules.
- Scene changes may use `---` only where the source itself creates the corresponding break under the project convention.
- This structural rule is a hard constraint, not a stylistic preference.

## Voice and prose

- Prefer direct, polished xianxia English with strong but accurate verbs.
- Maintain the owner's tighter causal flow and avoid explanatory padding.
- Preserve register shifts, including humor and colloquial phrasing, when intentional.
- `心机，有手段` in this context may be expressed as `cunning and capable`, rather than over-explaining both qualities.
- When the source says a figure is being backed or bet on, natural English such as `preparing to back` is preferred over literal gambling syntax if the underlying metaphor remains clear.
- For cultivation-scale description, preserve the grandeur without translationese: `unfolded layer upon layer`, `almost turning illusion into reality`, and similar concrete phrasing are preferred.

## Pronouns for Dao Lords

- Preserve established capitalized pronouns for Dao Lords where the project has already adopted them in formal/narrative reference: He, His, Him, Himself.
- Do not mechanically capitalize pronouns for ordinary cultivators.
- In passages where the World-Honored One's perspective alternates with narration, maintain pronoun capitalization consistently.

## Named call-outs and articles

- Owner-final Chapter 1255 uses `The Heavenly Palace` and `The Wheel of Life and Death` as standalone named call-outs.
- Article use is term-specific and should follow the latest owner ruling rather than a blanket no-article rule.

## Willow Wood cycle

- `盛时` = `Time of Flourishing` in the paired cycle.
- `夭时` = `Time of Withering` in the paired cycle.
- If a later sentence refers to the resulting harmful state rather than repeating the formal paired label, natural wording such as `Untimely Death` may still be appropriate if source context supports it.

## Mechanical discipline

- Obvious typos in owner-pasted prose do not become precedents. For example, `ninety-nine layer` is normalized to `ninety-nine layers`, and missing spaces after punctuation are repaired.
- Owner wording choices remain final in substance.
