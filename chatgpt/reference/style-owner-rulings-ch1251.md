# Owner Style Rulings, Chapter 1251 Onward

Read this after the core style guide and all earlier owner-style supplements. The owner's final prose overrides provisional model wording.

## Direct, polished xianxia action

- Prefer decisive verbs and compact causal movement. Do not pad action with abstract explanation once the source has made the relationship clear.
- Preserve the scale and imagery of cultivation combat without turning it into ornate translationese. Strong owner-final examples include stars `wheeling across the heavens and turning the sun`, a figure `scattered like cloud and smoke`, and a sword that `tore free of its sheath`.
- Keep combat geography explicit: identify the power used, what it crossed, where the user appeared, what the strike targeted, and what changed.
- When a paragraph contains a technical explanation, state the mechanism once. Do not accidentally duplicate a sentence or repeat the same conclusion in new words.

## Cultivation wording

- `鸣道惊玄` may be rendered contextually as Dao resonance `stirring countless Mysteries` when the line describes the resonance acting upon Mysteries.
- In the Eight Negations Wisdom Sword contrast, `身 / 法 / 心念` is naturally `flesh / spells / thought`. Do not force `dharma` when the immediate contrast is between bodily, magical, and mental targets.
- `火行至尊` in Chapter 1251 is `Supreme Fire Element Fruition Attainment`. Preserve the formal capitalization in this status phrase.
- When the source refers to a group of specific Fruition Attainments under one controller, number must follow the context. Chapter 1251 uses `the master of the Fruition Attainments`.
- `自裁` in decrees and immediate thought is `take one's own life`, not the blunter `kill oneself`. Expository reasoning may still use `suicide` where it is the natural technical description.

## Displayed decrees

- A formal displayed decree, edict, or proclamation that the source presents as a bracketed system-like line retains bold corner brackets: `**【Decree: ...】**`.
- This is a narrow exception. Ordinary standalone technique, artifact, realm, and Fruition Attainment call-outs continue to lose source brackets and remain plain Title Case under the core style guide.

## Owner-final rhythm

- Keep short source reaction lines short.
- Let longer combat paragraphs flow through action, consequence, and only then explanation.
- Prefer concrete phrases such as `the strength of their Dao Attainment`, `completely blank`, `crystal clear blood`, and `every trace of flame clinging to his body` when they fit the source, but do not turn these examples into mandatory stock wording.
- Preserve natural contractions except those ending in `'d`.
- Continue to preserve source paragraph structure, direct-thought italics, present-tense immediate thought, Chinese idioms and allusions, and all established terminology.

## Mechanical discipline

Owner edits are final in substance, but obvious slips do not become style precedents. Remove duplicate sentences, repair malformed Markdown, fix unmistakable spelling errors, and normalize accidental punctuation without rewriting the owner's chosen prose.
