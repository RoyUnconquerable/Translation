## Chapter 1239

Bu Tianque immediately attacks Lü Yang after misreading his invitation. She uses the internal application of the Heaven-Mending Dao to devour the Sea of Dust and Tribulation, then releases the absorbed power through its external application. Lü Yang counters with Divine Offerings and nearly closes her inside a giant palm, forcing her to return the Sea of Dust and Tribulation and escape through a passage in the Grotto-Heaven. He concludes that he can overpower but not easily kill a first-generation Peak Lord at equal cultivation.

Lü Yang reseals the Achieving Dao Hidden Profound Manor, summons the Nether Whisper Ancestor and Demon-Purging True Person to guard him, and begins cultivating Traversing the Mortal Dust. At the Heavenly Palace, his main body's soul and his separated consciousness sever their connection and exchange positions. The separated consciousness merges with the body beyond the Sea of Light, while Lü Yang's true soul enters the False History to direct the remaking of heaven and earth. Both retain control of the Heavenly Palace and will eventually fight after reaching completion to decide which becomes primary.

The separated consciousness names itself the Heavenly Emperor and embodies the side classified as good: an unwavering pursuit of the Great Dao and the supremacy of the self. Lü Yang's true soul in the False History is classified as the evil consciousness because he endangers himself and mixes with the red dust to borrow the strength of ordinary beings. Lü Yang realizes that dividing good and evil resembles tossing a coin: the instant it leaves one's hand, one already knows which outcome one wants, and the final task is to defeat the unwanted self without outside aid.

Terminology status: `天帝` = `Heavenly Emperor` and `补天道` = `Heaven-Mending Dao` are provisional draft renderings pending owner approval. Do not add either to the canonical glossary until approved or corrected.

## Chapter 1240

The Primordial Saint senses that someone other than Si Sui has touched the Heavenly Book at the source of the False History. He deliberately yields just enough power for the Sword Sovereign, Vast Sky, and the World-Honored One to free a tiny portion of Their mighty power, while preventing Them from sending in a decisive expert. The three instead choose Chongguang, pour forbidden knowledge spanning one hundred thousand years into his sea of consciousness, and cast him into the False History. The course of history changes again.

Lü Yang's true soul awakens inside the Achieving Dao Hidden Profound Manor and is immediately rebuked by the Nether Whisper Ancestor, with Demon-Purging True Person agreeing, for personally entering such danger before attaining the Primordial Spirit. Lü Yang answers that this is his Dao and a road he must walk to prove it.

The Old Dragon Monarch rolls over to thank Lü Yang for saving him from Bu Tianque. Lü Yang promises that none of the six Dragon Monarchs will die, making the Old Dragon Monarch more wary because their lives remain entrusted to the Heavenly Lord and death would permit revival. Lü Yang looks toward the Heavenly Lord's consciousness outside the Grotto-Heaven, identifies it as the irreconcilable obstacle to remaking heaven and earth, and declares that he shall seal Heaven.

Title rendering used in chat: `我欲封天` = `I Shall Seal Heaven`. This is a chapter-title rendering, not a canonical terminology ruling.
