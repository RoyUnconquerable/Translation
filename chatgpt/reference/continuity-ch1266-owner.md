# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1266 Owner Final

The World-Honored One's self-immolation and departure freeze the attention of every Dao Lord. The Primordial Saint openly displays anger, confusion, and shock because He never expected Myriad Treasures to abandon His Dao Lord position, a choice contrary to the self-interested temperament expected of a Dao Lord. The Other Shore's ascent stops.

After restoring His calm, the Primordial Saint wonders who gave Beast-Taming and Myriad Treasures the courage to change. He considers Si Sui and the unknown rat beyond the Fathomless Void.

The Primordial Saint traces the connection through True Monarch Flying Snow's body, now occupied by the Ancestral Dragon, and opens the Fathomless Void to His gaze. His eyes fill with joy, anger, sorrow, happiness, worry, grief, fear, and shock. The Sword Sovereign recognizes that His Primordial Spirit attainments have advanced beyond Si Sui's.

The Primordial Saint discovers the Heavenly Palace, an ordered realm resembling a fifth super Boundary Heaven but standing above the mundane world as a dwelling of immortals and gods. The Heavenly Emperor emerges from the Palace of Soaring Clouds and meets His gaze.

The Primordial Saint identifies the Heavenly Emperor as a consciousness produced through Traversing the Mortal Dust. Divine Transformation and Ascension has placed Him too far from the mortal world to destroy the Heavenly Emperor or the Heavenly Palace, leaving voice transmission as His limit.

Recognizing a temperament even more like His own than the World-Honored One's, the Primordial Saint offers the Heavenly Emperor the Seven Emotions Heaven-Fathoming Art. The method would awaken and discard the seven emotions, grant the Heavenly Emperor humanity and a True Self, and allow him to pursue a Primordial Spirit independently while causing Lü Yang's own Primordial Spirit cultivation to fail.

The Heavenly Emperor's nature tells him to accept, but the Primordial Saint overlooks his lack of talent. The Heavenly Emperor has no wisdom light and cannot understand the method. He realizes that Lü Yang foresaw this exact temptation and remained unconcerned because the Heavenly Emperor simply lacked the ability to take advantage of it.
