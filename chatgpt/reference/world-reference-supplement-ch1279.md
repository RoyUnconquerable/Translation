# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1279

## Du Xuan's analysis

After Lü Yang knocks Du Xuan out of the Sea of Light, the Spiritual Power Dao Lord remains calm because His purpose in taking the field was to probe Lü Yang's methods. He identifies Lü Yang's fist as dharma in essence.

The fist mobilizes the imagery of nearly every Great Dao in the Sea of Light except the Dharma Body and Yin and Yang. Du Xuan concludes that Lü Yang cultivated the Dharma Body Dao, received Si Sui's true inheritance, and studied the Treasured Life Perfect Form Scripture.

Lü Yang's foundation resembles the Ancestral Dragon's because both use their own Mysteries to encompass the Five Heavenly Numbers. His overall scheme also resembles the Primordial Saint's. Du Xuan is unsettled that Lü Yang has drawn from all three titans who defined the Sea of Light's three ages.

## The Sword Sovereign descends

Vast Sky considers recruiting Lü Yang and using the God-Sealing Dao Lineage to rebuild and strengthen the Heavenly Dao. The Sword Sovereign rejects the idea because Lü Yang already possesses his own place where he attained the Dao, cares about ordinary beings, and would seek to make the Heavenly Dao his own.

The Sword Sovereign leaves the fourth layer of the Other Shore and uses the momentum of its final descent to attack Lü Yang. Her sword manifests the River of Fate, which gathers all Lü Yang's possible futures into one conclusion: death.

Sword Edge Metal can imitate this effect only because it stripped imagery from Fate. It cannot match Fate itself.

## Sanhe's answer

Lü Yang draws Heaven-Slayer, and Sanhe's sacrificed Primordial Spirit appears as a faint, spectral figure. Sanhe understands that the Sword Sovereign shaped and used him, but he does not reject the principles She taught him or deny his own responsibility for purging demons for sixty years.

Because protecting the Sea of Light remains Sanhe's own conviction, he will oppose the Sword Sovereign as readily as he opposed the Ancestral Dragon. Heaven-Slayer was created for this confrontation.

Lü Yang remembers the repeated helpless endings of his previous lives, merges with the sword and the Sea of Light, and advances against the falling Other Shore. His divine will and the Sea of Light's myriad phenomena answer together as he seeks to sever the Dao, slay Heaven, and overturn Heaven's Will.
