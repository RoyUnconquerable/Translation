# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1260 Owner Final

Lü Yang returns from contacting Si Sui and tells the World-Honored One that He must study under Si Sui for a hundred thousand years after the present crisis. The World-Honored One takes this as forgiveness. He isn't truly afraid of Si Sui, but uses the message to make His stance clear as He boards a new ship and abandons His old way of dealing with allies.

The World-Honored One rejects Lü Yang's renewed attempt to recruit Him through the God-Sealing Method. He recognizes the sales tactic from founding the Pure Land, when He hawked the Mahayana True Enlightenment Scripture and promised immediate Dao Lord attainment. Lü Yang concludes that taking Him into the Dao Lineage before refining a Primordial Spirit could let His tail grow too large to control.

Lü Yang then reveals that the Primordial Saint originally proved the Dao through Time, that the River of Time was the foundation upon which He proved the Dao, and that it later became the Great Net of Karma. The World-Honored One begins deducing what would happen if the Primordial Saint personally killed Him, curses Him as a beast, and commits fully to action.

The World-Honored One confirms that He has already seized the Heavenly Lord's body and will not hinder Lü Yang. He will notify Alchemy Cauldron and Heaven-Mending, allowing the God-Sealing Method to spread through Jiangdong and Jiangbei.

He further judges that Sanhe knows the False History is unreal and seeks both awakening from the dream and eternal sleep. Sanhe will not act before Lü Yang refines a Primordial Spirit. The only remaining Variable is Sublime Bliss, the Taiyi Beast raised by Myriad Spells.

Lü Yang and the World-Honored One lure Sublime Bliss with a staged defeat. Lü Yang's aura and twelve Fruition Attainment stars retreat toward Jiangxi while he loudly claims the Heavenly Lord bested him by a move and a half. The defenses of Jiangxi and the Overseas rise as if to receive him.

Sublime Bliss takes the bait. He believes the Heavenly Lord is a donkey at the end of its tricks and needs only his weight to tip the scales. Convinced the two sides cannot be cooperating, he considers blocking Lü Yang's retreat and joining the Heavenly Lord to kill him, preserving the Yellow Millet's last hope of proving the Dao.

The owner-final title and closing thought are *The Only Chance in This Life* and *This might be my only chance in this life!*
