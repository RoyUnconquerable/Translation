# World Reference Correction, Chapter 1250

Read this after `world-reference-supplement-ch1249-onward.md`. It records the owner's final corrections to the provisional Chapter 1250 additions without altering the underlying source facts.

## Chongguang's standing and cultivation history

Chongguang ranks second only to Demon-Purging True Person among the Immortal Pivot's Foundation Establishment cultivators and holds the formal office of Acting Sect Master of the Saint Sect. Across Lü Yang's restarts, Chongguang has reached the True Monarch realm three times. In one life, he quickly comprehended the unfamiliar route of seeking the Golden Core through Heart Demons. The source passage does not establish a formal named `Heart Demon Dao` here.

## The World-Honored One's test piece

Si Sui identifies the intruder's likely realm as Foundation Establishment because bypassing the Immortal Pivot's Fruition Attainments and seeking the fundamental Five Elements directly requires extraordinary talent and brilliance. Lü Yang combines that inference with Chongguang's Immortal Spirit origin, the Heavenly Lord's special attention toward Immortal Spirits, the earlier obstruction of Lü Yang's karmic calculation, and the One Mind. He concludes that the World-Honored One controls the Heavenly Lord and chose Chongguang as His piece. This remains Lü Yang's deduction rather than an independently confirmed fact.

Chongguang's original Immortal Spirit body should have been born in Jiangbei and should still possess karma in the False History's ancient era because Immortal Spirits require tens of thousands of years to gestate. Once Lü Yang controls Jiangbei, he expects to locate Chongguang despite Chongguang isolating himself from human habitation.

## Jiangbei and the Heaven-Connecting Sea of Clouds

Lü Yang has completed the three-year process of bringing Jiangxi and the Overseas into order and now moves to seize Jiangbei. At the Heaven-Connecting Sea of Clouds, Lian Tiandou predicts this target because Jiangdong and Jiangnan are closely linked and the Grand Sword Patriarch's presence in Jiangnan is deterrent enough. Lian Tiandou intends to fight Lü Yang in search of answers, and Bu Tianque agrees to follow his lead.

Lian Tiandou suspects a flaw in the False History's remembered world. He questions what lies beyond the Immortal Pivot and why its True Monarchs and Dao Lineages remain confined within it rather than venturing into the Sea of Light. The source does not yet explain the cause.
