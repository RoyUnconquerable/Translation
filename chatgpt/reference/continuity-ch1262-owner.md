# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1262 Owner Final

Within the Underworld, True Monarch Azure Firmament presides over the Nascent Soul Pill's completion. Purple-Azure Tushita Fire flows throughout the mountain range, drawing wind, clouds, dragon roars, tiger howls, and ancient imagery into the pill furnace. Despite the Variables caused by Skyward's departure, events have followed His script.

When the Nascent Soul Pill's radiance rises, the Sword Sovereign attacks first. Her sword cry carries an inevitable fate forward like a boundless river. Dao Tianqi leaves the Underworld Sovereign's Hall and blocks Her, silencing the sword cries without disrupting the pill's refinement.

The Sword Sovereign warns that True Monarch Azure Firmament is the Ancestral Dragon and that the Primordial Saint laid the entire trap, including Lian Tiandou's Nascent Soul Pill plan. She accuses Dao Tianqi of being schemed against every step of the way to his death while still helping the Primordial Saint.

Vast Sky attacks next from the Outer Heavens. His talismanic characters depict the myriad phenomena and assemble into the character for Heaven. Dao Tianqi cannot block both Dao Lords, but an illusory fair finger from the Primordial Saint stops Vast Sky's attack and stands between its radiant world and the Underworld like a heavenly chasm.

The present attempt differs from the one two lives ago. Lü Yang's advance warning has exposed the Primordial Saint's plan for Divine Transformation and Ascension. The Dao Lords now attack the Nascent Soul Pill while the Primordial Saint protects it. The Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky are primarily testing Him and have already prepared for Divine Transformation and Ascension. The World-Honored One does nothing, while the Spiritual Power Dao Lord and Spell Dao Lord watch coldly.

The Nascent Soul Pill reaches completion with gold-and-white coloring, dragon-and-tiger patterns, and imagery capable of bringing all things to flawless completion. The Sword Sovereign withdraws and allows Dao Tianqi to leave, though his accusation that She deceived him sounds weak and halting.

True Monarch Azure Firmament takes the pill and invokes Tai Yitian, the Primordial Saint's true name. A second finger slips past the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky, whisks the pill away before Dao Tianqi can reach it, and returns it to the Other Shore. The narration identifies the Cangjiang Dragon Monarch as the one who invoked the true name.

Dao Tianqi stops his apparently furious pursuit. Recalling how his Eldest Senior Brother always called him stupid and warned that his neglect of schemes would get him schemed to death, Dao Tianqi insists that he is actually clever but normally chooses not to think about such matters.

Without warning, the Nascent Soul Pill explodes on the Other Shore and unleashes a world-destroying storm. Dao Tianqi had prepared the explosion in advance, overturning the outcome the Primordial Saint appeared to have settled with two fingers.
