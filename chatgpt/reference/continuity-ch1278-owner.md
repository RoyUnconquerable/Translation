# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1278 Owner Final

The Spiritual Power Dao Lord, Du Xuan, descends from the Other Shore with peak Golden Core cultivation, 129,600 years of accumulated progress, and a fragment of the Other Shore. He attempts to strip the entire Sea of Light of spiritual power, but the God-Sealing Method has severed the cultivators' subordination to His Dao.

Du Xuan sees Lü Yang controlling the giant formed from the Sea of Light and attacks by commanding Lü Yang's own spiritual power. The palm disrupts Lü Yang's power and Mysteries, but Lü Yang dissolves into the Sea of Light instead of resisting through personal spiritual power.

Lü Yang now treats the omnipresent spiritual qi of heaven and earth as his power and commands every Great Dao within the Sea of Light. An immense eye formed from Yin-Yang Creation and Destruction Profound Light, with several Boundary Heavens composing its pupil, gazes at Du Xuan.

Heaven in a Palm shapes the Five Elements into a fist. Divine Offerings, Drop of Heaven's Essence, the Sea of Dust and Tribulation, Fate-Reforming, and the five Supreme Fruition Attainments reinforce it. The fist encompasses the Spiritual Power Dao and the Spell Dao of Myriad Spells as the source and origin of every Dao in the Sea of Light.

The clash becomes a contest between Primordial Spirits. Lü Yang's fist shatters Du Xuan's ocean imagery, draws Dao Lord blood, strikes His true body, and sends Him flying out of the Sea of Light.

Lü Yang orders the Dao Lords to get the hell back. The Sword Sovereign, Vast Sky, and the Spell Dao Lord recognize True Monarch Sovereign of Fate, Lü Yang, as an opponent worthy of contending with Them over the Dao.
