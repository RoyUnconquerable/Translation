## Intervening Chapter 1252 context

After capturing Lian Tiandou with Heaven in a Palm, Lü Yang crushes his final resistance despite the first-generation Alchemy Cauldron Peak Lord condensing a Nascent Soul Pill representing the culmination of his lifetime, Great Dao, plane, and supreme immortal pill. Bu Tianque realizes Lü Yang's growth over three years has become terrifyingly fast. The Grand Sword Patriarch and the Heavenly Lord both watch. Lü Yang concludes that allowing Chongguang to attain the Five Elements would slowly destroy his present advantage, so he openly challenges the powers of the Immortal Pivot to come at him together.

## Chapter 1253: Beheading Pending Review

At Extreme Heaven Cliff, the Grand Sword Patriarch and Skyward hear Lü Yang's challenge. The Grand Sword Patriarch questions why Lü Yang chose Jiangbei rather than the easier Jiangdong. Skyward infers that something must have happened to the Heavenly Lord and that Lü Yang has been forced to rise to the challenge. Their exchange remains dry and sardonic: Skyward calls the deduction basic Saint Sect training, while the Grand Sword Patriarch claims he lacks the attention to fight Lü Yang because suppressing the now-recovered Skyward already occupies him.

In Jiangbei, Lü Yang calculates that being forced away from Jiangxi, the foundation territory of the God-Sealing Method, cuts roughly thirty percent from his combat power while the Heavenly Lord can fight at full strength. The scheme seems too intricate for the Heavenly Lord's displayed intelligence, so he suspects the World-Honored One behind it.

Three figures arrive from Jiangdong and are forcibly refined by the Heavenly Lord's lightning into Emperor Cang. Empowerment raises Emperor Cang from Immortal Bridge to Treading the Heavens. He believes the Heavenly Lord's promise that killing Lü Yang will make him supreme ruler of the Immortal Pivot, but had demanded payment in advance.

Emperor Cang unfolds the Immortal Kingdom Daoist Law across Jiangbei. Dense seal script defines the sun and moon, mountains and rivers, stars, living beings, and the myriad phenomena, then invokes their imagery in a destructive strike. Lü Yang tears apart the darkness with a spark that becomes a prairie fire, while the twelve Fruition Attainment colors appear behind him.

The Heavenly Lord empowers Emperor Cang again, raising his status beyond Lian Tiandou's earlier level. Emperor Cang uses the law as an anchor to remake heaven and earth and bar the Fruition Attainment radiance, but heavenly light pierces through the clouds and erases his script and Mysteries.

A displayed decree appears: Cang, True Monarch of Jiangdong, is sentenced to beheading, pending review. Beneath Heavenly Fire, Lü Yang condemns the usurping false emperor for daring to establish statutes and enforce the law, then orders him to stand down.
