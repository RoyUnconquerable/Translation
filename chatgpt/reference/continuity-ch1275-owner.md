# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1275 Owner Final

The falling Other Shore rains fire across the Sea of Light. Dao Tianqi raises the Underworld above it, while the Foundation Establishment Realm rises into the Underworld and diverts part of the impact onto itself.

The World-Honored One recognizes that the Other Shore's descent after Divine Transformation and Ascension can smash a Dao Lord to a lower realm. A Dao Lord remains immortal because the Primordial Spirit is anchored within the place where that Dao Lord attained the Dao.

The Other Shore has much greater mass than the Underworld. Destroying the Underworld may make reconstruction impossible, whereas the Other Shore may merely break into fragments that can later be pieced together.

The Ancestral Dragon is an Innate Dao God whose Primordial Spirit is anchored in the Sea of Light. So long as any fragment remains, He survives and may revive. The World-Honored One suspects that the Other Shore has usurped this immortality through using Him as its foundation.

The World-Honored One urges Dao Tianqi to withdraw if the defense becomes impossible, arguing that Si Sui will escape and can bear the brunt of what follows. Dao Tianqi refuses to gamble with the Sea of Light's survival.

Dao Tianqi sees Lü Yang ascending from the mortal realm through the Foundation Establishment Realm, the Sea of Suffering, and the Underworld. Although Lü Yang has only just refined a Primordial Spirit, lacks wisdom light, and does not possess especially high Status, an endless multitude bears him aloft.

This collective power resembles One Mind while remaining fundamentally different. The World-Honored One had previously glimpsed only a single spot of the leopard through a tube, but now sees the power fully matured and appearing before Him in its entirety.

Lü Yang asks Dao Tianqi to conserve his strength and leave the remaining defense to the Sea of Light. The nearly shattered Underworld withdraws after weakening the falling fire by ninety percent.

Thirty stars form a Great Wall above the Sea of Light. Four pillars of divine light rise behind it, containing four figures and surrounded by endless pavilions and palaces. Their hierarchies extend downward until all living beings in the Sea of Light contribute to the defense.

Countless sparks gather into a prairie fire like the rising sun driving back the long night. The fear of destruction recedes and is replaced by safety and calm.

The closing four-line proclamation declares that Lü Yang rises in the East and ascends the throne, governs the stars beneath the Mandate of Heaven, commands the gods, pacifies the seas, guards the pivot with his sword, endures tribulation and mortal suffering, proves himself a Perfected Person, pours out his crimson heart to save all living beings, and remakes the universe to usher in a radiant new spring.
