# Current continuity: Chapters 1304 to 1308

This is the active causal window for the next translation. It records source
facts independently of whether a chapter's English draft has received owner
approval. Review status belongs in the chapter ledger.

## Prerequisite state

- Lü Yang completed his Primordial Spirit by overcoming the Heavenly Emperor's
  consciousness; their deeper internal unification remains unfinished.
- Sanhe's three innate spirits prevented a stable Primordial Spirit. He chose
  sacrifice, and his temporary Primordial Spirit and the Sword Pavilion's
  mountain gate became Heaven-Slayer.
- The God-Sealing Method reorganized the Sea of Light into ninety-nine
  provinces and let it withstand the Other Shore's fall. The Sword Sovereign
  later broke Lü Yang's collective body and much of the Heavenly Palace, but
  failed to kill him or destroy the Underworld before the Other Shore shattered.
- Si Sui had to await the histories' divergence to recover 129,600 years.
  Their final separation occurs in Chapter 1295. The Heavenly Scripture seal had
  been a possible retreat before His subsequent breakthrough.
- Before the current battle, the World-Honored One destroyed Karma through
  His past life body and the Primordial Saint banished Dao Tianqi into the
  Future. Chapter 1301 explicitly confirms his return.
- Detailed events through Chapter 1303 are in continuity-archive.md; current
  positions and unresolved questions below supersede those earlier snapshots.

## Current causal chain
- Chapter 1304: the Saint recognizes that He cannot stop the Heavenly Scripture.
  Its original page was left by the first transcender; He refined the rest and
  delivered it to Si Sui as part of the seal. He could seal only around the
  page, never interfere with its Mysteries or leave an effective contingency.
  He calmly begins planning His next move.
- Si Sui rises amid unseparated Primordial Chaos. The cover and almost all pages
  dissolve into the void; the original page survives and its characters become
  a talisman. All His Mysteries are anchored in it. Peak Golden Core could make
  a False History, but Dao Lord status is necessary to bear reality's weight.
- The Saint warns that entering tribulation again will further delay true
  Transcendence. Si Sui gives no answer and strikes 129,600 times, cleaving a
  rift. One becomes Two as the Yin-Yang Creation and Destruction Profound Light
  emerges. A new sea forms, compressing millions of years into an instant.
- Si Sui relinquishes His transcendent body to widen the rift. His fall and
  disintegration bring Two to Three, then all things. His hair becomes lofty
  mountains extending 100,000 li, blood a boundless ocean, breath spiritual qi,
  and voice thunder; all His numbered features, apertures, and organs transform.
- He forms one continuous world without separate realms: thirty-six heavenly
  layers and seventy-two earthly regions. Fruition Attainment stars establish
  the Five Elements, followed by Yin and Yang. The Dharma Body and Dao Heart
  together enable life. Trillions upon trillions of beings arrive with their
  environments unchanged. Spiritual Power and finally Tribulation complete it.
- Si Sui's Primordial Spirit remains above the new world. The old Sea of
  Suffering is dry and living beings are gone, but five divine lights remain:
  Destiny, Fortune, Fate, and Spells, retained by the four Dao Lords, plus
  Variables, which He cannot affect. Its favor for Si Sui fades as the balance
  changes. The Saint's power also falls, His figure shakes, and Destiny seems
  grievously damaged after 129,600 years of rule. No final defeat is confirmed.

- Chapter 1305: Lü Yang stands on a seaside cliff in the new world. He reveals
  that he physically gripped the Book of a Hundred Lives panel and suppressed
  its reaction while Si Sui used the Heavenly Scripture, preventing reclamation
  of the missing page and detection by the Saint. He infers that higher status
  grants more access; complete future understanding is his expectation.
- Si Sui appears as one Primordial Spirit thought, having relinquished His
  Dharma Body. His self-cultivated Dao Lord status remains intact and He can
  still fight. Lü Yang feels pressure even from that single thought.
- The old sea has only the enduring Yin-Yang Creation and Destruction Profound
  Light. The Saint uses it to surround the new world and erode its transcendent
  nature. Si Sui had intended to take the new world away into the void.
- Its intact transcendent nature currently prevents entry. Si Sui estimates
  at least several thousand, possibly tens of thousands of years before the
  balance breaks. He suspects the Saint cannot sustain Nascent Soul status;
  Lü Yang links this to the limit of the damaged-Destiny power mechanism.
- Si Sui declines an immediate attack because cornering the Saint could bring
  Variables to His aid. This is a risk, not an assured rescue or victory.

- Chapter 1306: Si Sui explains that a chance of survival can arise before
  danger and be missed; ninety-nine percent of people fail to grasp theirs.
  The Saint's cultivation of Destiny makes Him unusually sensitive to it,
  but even He could fail. Si Sui will not gamble with only His Spirit remaining.
- Lü Yang would gamble because he can restart, but cannot persuade Si Sui
  without disclosing the Book. He also suspects that the Saint is waiting for
  such an attack as a trap. He voices concern that restored Destiny and further
  Other Shore repairs could restore the enemy Dao Lords' status.
- Si Sui proposes that Lü Yang transcend before the Saint recovers. A second
  transcender could also break the balance and let the new world escape into
  the void. Lü Yang doubts his ability; he knows the methods' effects but not
  their underlying operation. No breakthrough is shown.
- Outside, the Saint faces the Taiji diagram enclosing the new world. His own
  viewpoint confirms that He has lost Nascent Soul status. The new world has
  no Variables; entering it would expose Him to Si Sui's overwhelming attack.
  He hoped Si Sui would come out, then abandons a quick victory when He does not.
- The Sword Sovereign, Vast Sky, and Myriad Spells arrive together and surround
  Him. Fate, Fortune, and Spells manifest with concealed but deadly killing
  intent. They question His injuries under a pretense of concern. He postpones
  attacking and claims to be fine for now, not uninjured. Their radiance closes
  like a noose; no strike or outcome is shown.

- Chapter 1307: the three Dao Lords want the undying Saint as a living pillar
  for a rebuilt Heavenly Dao incorporating Destiny. He consumes the Alchemy
  Dao, sacrificing its arts to replenish part of His vitality. Artifact
  Refinement built the Other Shore; Beast-Taming enslaved the Dragon; Dual
  Cultivation linked the void and grounded His true Nascent Soul.
- The enemies remain suspicious until He separates Them through Time. They
  immediately back down, calling Their own threat a joke. He spares Them
  because useful people are scarce, not because They have earned His trust.
- Losing the Dharma Body and Dao Heart rules out both fleshly and conscious
  life in the old sea. The Five Elements Dao remains linked to the Dragon;
  only the Immortal Pivot's Fruition Attainments could be taken. Its weak
  material foundation is not destroyed. The Saint calculates 84,000 years
  for the Dragon's sea of consciousness and Five Elements to regather,
  permitting the Other Shore's reconstruction. Earlier five-light reports
  describe visible manifestations, not the absence of this surviving root.
- Until then, the Saint alone can regain Dao Lord status by damaging Destiny;
  the others lack His method. He turns toward the Heavenly Dao instead.
  Si Sui explains its impasse: only the Saint qualifies as its living pillar,
  but He will not consent. Surpassing its former peak could nurture a Dao Lord.
- Even a restored Saint alone cannot enter the new world, only sustain a
  stalemate. One of Nian Yao, Vast Sky, or Myriad Spells must also regain Dao
  Lord status through a stronger Heavenly Dao or further Other Shore repairs.
  Si Sui rules out the former and says the latter takes a long time. Lü Yang
  finally realizes he may not need to restart this life. No restart occurs.

- Chapter 1308: Lü Yang manifests a divine court and deploys gods throughout
  the new world, extending the God-Sealing Dao Lineage and his perception.
  The Nether Whisper Ancestor, Demon-Purging True Person, Flying Snow, and
  Skyward arrive as Treading the Heavens Great True Monarchs. Suo Huan and
  Empress Xiao are also present. Skyward's Newborn Continent is rejected;
  the Ancestor names the world the Heavenly Wilderness.
- Lü Yang gives the four Traversing the Mortal Dust: only a Primordial Spirit
  stands between them and peak Golden Core. Their advancement raises the
  Heavenly Palace. He welcomes it, unlike the Saint's restricted admissions.
- Above the sun, Lü Yang sees the missing Book page at its core, inscribed
  with one unreadable seal-script character. He believes taking it would
  collapse the world. Though self-interest favors grabbing it and restarting,
  he chooses to continue the life everyone fought to preserve and feels relief.
- The World-Honored One, Dao Tianqi, and the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord arrive
  as peak Golden Core Dao Lords. The latter survived and expects a reward for
  intervening against the Saint; Lü Yang already gave Him a Palace position.
- The present gods cannot govern the whole world. Lü Yang offers the three
  his method to modify if they decline Palace incorporation. The World-Honored
  One and Cultivating Truth agree; half the latter's Primordial Spirit comes
  from the former. Their western territory retains operational obligations
  but is outside direct court rule. Modified methods still raise the Palace.
- Dao Tianqi seeks Underworld repairs. The Ghost Gate survives and supports
  reincarnation. Normal repair requires tens of thousands of years; rapid
  restoration needs vast quantities of source jade (Void Stone). At least
  sixty percent went to the Other Shore, twenty to the Underworld, and the
  remaining twenty to the Dragon's Detached Court. Scraps cannot meet demand.
- The Palace governs above, the two leaders share the west with qualified
  autonomy, and the Underworld conceals itself below. Lü Yang wonders about
  the Saint outside. No new enemy action or repair is shown.

## Live questions

- Can Lü Yang transcend before the Saint recovers, and how would he take that
  step? Can another transcender secure the new world's escape as Si Sui proposes?
- Can the Saint overcome the living-pillar impasse or find another route to
  restore His allies before the projected Other Shore reconstruction?
- Can Lü Yang effectively combine the Dao Heart with Heaven-Slayer in battle?
- How will Variables act if the balance changes again? A chance of survival
  is not automatic victory, and the Saint's ability to grasp it is not certain.
- Who was the first transcender, and how complete is the Primordial Saint's
  account of the Ancient Past and the paths to higher cultivation?
- What role do the first-generation Peak Lords' names play in the Saint's
  cultivation, and can His original name or supporting ritual be exploited?
- Can the World-Honored One fully recover His status through Tribulation?

## Open character hypotheses

- Lü Yang reconstructs the Primordial Saint's three plans in Chapter 1293:
  Divine Transformation and Ascension; personally killing the World-Honored
  One to command Variables through Karma and Destiny; and, as a last resort,
  damaging Destiny in mortal combat to comprehend Variables imagery. He
  infers that the last plan was meant for the fight with Si Sui and that
  damage beyond a critical limit would severely weaken the Primordial Saint.
  Keep this account as Lü Yang's deduction, not a universal numeric limit.
- Lü Yang deduces in Chapter 1292 that the Primordial Saint's sequence is the
  four Empty Attainment Daos in the Past, Time in the Present, and Destiny in
  the Future. The four are Alchemy, Artifact Refinement, Dual Cultivation, and
  Beast-Taming. He rejects placing Time in the Past because that sequence
  would require a Nascent Soul future body, unlike the peak Golden Core body
  facing him.
- From that causal link, Lü Yang connects the four first-generation Peak
  Lords' names, Dao Tianqi, Bu Tianque, Lian Tiandou, and Shi Tianyi, to the
  shared Tian, meaning Heaven, in Tai Yitian. The Primordial Saint praises the
  deduction; the full mechanism and consequences remain undisclosed.
- The World-Honored One suspects that the Other Shore may have usurped some of
  the Ancestral Dragon's broad immortality because the Dragon formed its
  foundation. This is not yet confirmed independently.
- Si Sui considers it possible that the Primordial Saint has combined the
  external path of constructing Dao-attainment systems with Si Sui's inward
  self-cultivation. This is a strategic worst-case deduction, not confirmed
  fact.
- Vast Sky believes His inheritance and the Primordial Saint's came from the
  same Doctrine of Names lineage. He infers that Tai Yitian is an altered name
  supported by a core inheritance from Grand Ancestral Master, that the name
  granted the Primordial Saint an unknown benefit, and that a supporting ritual
  is hidden inside Grand Ancestral Master. He believes recovering the original
  true name or destroying that ritual may create an avenue of attack and cause
  the benefit to turn against the Primordial Saint. This remains Vast Sky's
  deduction.
