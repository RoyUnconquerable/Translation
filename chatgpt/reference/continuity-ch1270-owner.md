# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1270 Owner Final

In the True History's Sea of Light, Lü Yang observes the Yin-Yang Creation and Destruction Profound Light. His completed Primordial Spirit grants him a Dao Lord's perspective, making the once-boundless Sea of Light as clear as the lines on his palm.

Lü Yang realizes that a Dao Lord's true foundation is not cultivation but the Primordial Spirit's ability to encompass the Sea of Light. True Monarch Supreme Dharma once demonstrated this distinction by recklessly raising his Immortal Bridge power and briefly displaying a Dao Lord's bearing.

The True History and False History appear as two torrents converging before they diverge. While converged, both chains of cause and effect remain mixed, simultaneously valid and invalid.

Si Sui must wait until the histories diverge and their cause and effect become anchored. If he escapes earlier through brute force, he will remain missing 129,600 years of cultivation and cannot defeat the Primordial Saint. By flowing downstream after divergence, he can recover all the missing time.

Within the historical torrents, the Grand Sword Patriarch appears as the only brilliant fish capable of briefly leaping free. Lü Yang reads three histories belonging to Sanhe: the current True History, the current False History, and the original history obscured when Dao Lords with Primordial Spirits broke free.

Sanhe was born with three innate spirits, suffered madness and rejection, and nearly became a beggar. The Sword Sovereign, Nian Yao, found him, raised him, transmitted the Dao, and transformed his burden into a cultivation advantage. Sanhe developed filial attachment and reverence toward Her as teacher, mother, and friend.

The Sword Sovereign deliberately taught Sanhe upright principles and brought him to speak with Si Sui, cultivating his instinct to protect mortals. Lü Yang recognizes that She intended to exploit this virtue at a decisive moment.

In the key historical conversation, the Sword Sovereign claims that Si Sui has gone to war because He suspects another purpose behind the Other Shore. She says the Dao Lords will enter the Fathomless Void to mediate, then asks Sanhe to guard every living being in the Sea of Light while They are gone.
