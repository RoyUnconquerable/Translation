# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1273

## Lü Yang returns lost karma

Within the torrent of karma, Lü Yang realizes that his completed Primordial Spirit lets him enter and witness the original first history even though it was later transformed into the False History and erased.

The moment Lü Yang witnesses that past, his existence becomes part of its history. The present Ancestral Dragon will therefore remember Lü Yang's words beside Sanhe's corpse, and Lü Yang can carry the recovered karma back into the mortal world as the False History and True History interact again.

Lü Yang does not judge Sanhe's actions as right or wrong. He acknowledges that the sixty-year purge of demons created many enemies, but refuses to let the deed and its meaning remain buried.

## Sanhe recovers his lost past

The brilliant fish representing the Grand Sword Patriarch leaves the historical torrent and manifests Sanhe. He learns that his strongest moment came when he defeated both the Ancestral Dragon and himself, then took his own life. That victory refined his Dao Heart even after the surrounding history was erased.

Sanhe's three innate spirits permanently block his Dao Path to a stable Primordial Spirit. Even if history returns to its rightful course, he cannot advance beyond his current half-step state.

Sanhe experienced more than one hundred thousand years under the God-Sealing Method when the False History was compressed into an instant. He likes that mortal world and accepts that his first sacrifice belongs to the past.

## The Grand Sword Patriarch becomes a sword

Sanhe warns that Lü Yang's cultivation is sufficient but his methods in battle cannot yet contend with the Sword Sovereign. He therefore offers to become a blade wielded by Lü Yang.

The Sword Pavilion's mountain gate tears free of the earth. Extreme Heaven Cliff becomes its edge, the structure pierces the Immortal Pivot and enters the torrent of karma, and Sanhe's figure is shattered and reforged into the supreme dharma sword.

Sanhe unites his three spirits as one, briefly manifests a Primordial Spirit, and sacrifices it to the sword. He believes this middling plan is far better than slaughtering every True Monarch and then killing himself again.

Sanhe has already left his legacy to Lü Yang's companion, who has refined three Sword Intents and possesses a better chance of attaining a Primordial Spirit.

## Heaven-Slayer

The sword cry reaches the fourth layer of the Other Shore. The Sword Sovereign realizes that Sanhe has died again and taken the Sword Pavilion's mountain gate, the sword She left behind. She sincerely regrets not killing him more thoroughly.

In the Sea of Light's mortal world, the Other Shore resembles a blazing, falling sun. Divine Transformation and Ascension has failed, and the Dao Lords will soon descend one after another.

Lü Yang names the new dharma sword Heaven-Slayer. He draws it into his sleeve, suppresses its boundless edge, and holds its power in reserve for the Dao Lords' arrival.
