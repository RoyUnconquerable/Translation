# Owner-Final Continuity, Chapter 1255

Chapter 1255 begins the direct duel between Lü Yang and the World-Honored One in the False History.

The World-Honored One, appearing as the first-generation Myriad Treasures Peak Lord Shi Tianyi, confirms through His own reasoning that Lü Yang has seen through the Chongguang scheme and likely has a Dao Lord guiding him. He infers Si Sui's approval and treats Lü Yang's sincerity, cunning, and combat strength as the basis for possible cooperation.

The World-Honored One deliberately tests Lü Yang. He gathers the Heavenly Lord's full might, equivalent to three-fifths of the Immortal Pivot's power, while Lü Yang commands only two-fifths. In addition, the World-Honored One retains the experience and Dao Attainment of a Nascent Soul Dao Lord. His standard is explicit: if Lü Yang cannot defeat Him under these disadvantages, he has no basis for challenging the Primordial Saint.

Lü Yang releases Lian Tiandou and enters the duel. He manifests The Heavenly Palace, a ninety-nine-layer divine palace vast enough to resemble a Boundary Heaven. The two combatants rise beyond the normal battlefield until nearly everyone in the False History loses sight of them; only the Grand Sword Patriarch can continue observing directly.

Beyond the False History's heavens, the World-Honored One manifests a colossal Buddha body that is revealed to be a Supreme Treasure refined to compensate for His lack of Dharma Body Dao cultivation. He says He once sought Si Sui's guidance when refining it.

The Buddha body tears Lü Yang's Heavenly Palace in half through brute force. Lü Yang escapes and reforms through Celestial River Immortal and Heavenly River Water. The World-Honored One then uses Willow Wood through The Wheel of Life and Death, placing Himself in the Time of Flourishing and Lü Yang in the Time of Withering, disrupting Celestial River Immortal.

Lü Yang answers with Heavenly River Water and Never-Drying River, using Long Flowing Water's endless source to reinforce the broader Water Element system. The World-Honored One acknowledges that Lü Yang's mastery across the Water Element is comparable to the Old Dragon Monarch's, but says it is still insufficient.

The chapter ends with the World-Honored One changing seals and saying, `Treasure, please reveal yourself.`
