# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1258

## Lü Yang and the World-Honored One's agreement

Lü Yang uses the World-Honored One as a benchmark for the Dao Lords' relative strength but remains wary because he considers His position on the Other Shore too low to make His judgment wholly reliable.

Having earned the right to negotiate through their battle, Lü Yang declares that he intends to claim the entire Immortal Pivot. The World-Honored One agrees to aid him. He will not interfere in Jiangdong or Jiangbei, and the Heavenly Lord will no longer obstruct Lü Yang. Jiangnan is conspicuously excluded.

The World-Honored One infers from Lü Yang's question about the Grand Sword Patriarch and his alliance with Skyward that Lü Yang arose after Skyward. Skyward emerged around thirty thousand years earlier, but records about him were buried when the World-Honored One altered cause and effect and reshaped the mortal world. The World-Honored One estimates that Lü Yang has cultivated for no more than ten thousand years and possibly fewer than five thousand. This is His inference, not an independently confirmed age.

## Sanhe and the altered False History

Sanhe is revealed as the Grand Sword Patriarch's true name. When the World-Honored One utters it, the Heavenly Dao reacts. He spreads murky gray light that blocks outside perception and the Heavenly Dao's descending power before revealing the concealed history.

Because the World-Honored One and Beast-Taming have both attained Primordial Spirits and the True Self is unique, neither has a native counterpart in the current False History. Its course has therefore diverged from what actually happened in the original past.

## Three choices during the war between Dao Lords

When the Primordial Saint revealed His ambition, the Grotto-Heaven method and the Three Foundations turned against each other. Their Dao Lineages had become thoroughly intertwined during the preceding prosperous era, but the Dao Lords' wills made war unavoidable. Treading the Heavens Great True Monarchs had no freedom to choose a side; only Dao Monarchs at peak Golden Core had even a little freedom.

The World-Honored One identifies Himself, Beast-Taming, and Sanhe, who only counted as half, as the three figures able to choose different paths.

Beast-Taming chose the Underworld. Near the war's end, he sought to gather the True Spirits of the countless dead, bring back old friends, and resolve the conflict among the Dao Lords through the Underworld. The Utmost Nature Immortal-Seeking Scripture enabled his Primordial Spirit but also made the fundamental nature of his character almost impossible to change. The World-Honored One says Beast-Taming did not understand the war's true cause.

The World-Honored One chose the Primordial Saint despite already sensing something wrong with Him. He sought a position on the Other Shore because he lacked the strength to protect Beast-Taming, Heaven-Mending, and Alchemy Cauldron otherwise. He ultimately failed to protect them.

Lü Yang privately reviews the four first-generation Peak Lords' fates: Alchemy Cauldron's True Spirit shattered, Beast-Taming remained asleep, Heaven-Mending hid within the Five Elements, and Myriad Treasures became Dao Lord-grade material. Lü Yang believes his intervention is what gives all four any chance to escape their outcomes. This is Lü Yang's evaluation.

## Sanhe, the Ancestral Dragon, and the first cultivators

Sanhe focused not on dead cultivators or the broader future, but on mortals. Soon after the Ancestral Dragon's suppression, the war between Dao Lords gave Him an opportunity to contact the Heavenly Lord and seek freedom through the Overseas True Dragons.

The Immortal Pivot is built entirely on the Ancestral Dragon's body. If He broke free, the trillions upon trillions of mortals living upon Him would die instantly. Sanhe therefore carried out a sixty-year purge of demons, cleansed the Immortal Pivot, hung his sword in the heavens, and suppressed the Ancestral Dragon's final counterattack.

The World-Honored One reveals that the first cultivators were nurtured by the Ancestral Dragon. Every cultivator, regardless of Dao Lineage, ultimately traces their origins to Him. Because He bestowed cultivation, He can seek to reclaim it. The World-Honored One presents this as the reason Si Sui agreed to suppress the Ancestral Dragon.
