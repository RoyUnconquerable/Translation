# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1272

## Sanhe's internal trial

Through his completed Primordial Spirit, Lü Yang enters the true historical scene surrounding the Grand Sword Patriarch's final decision. Sanhe's three innate spirits argue as distinct figures while history and karma intertwine around them.

The spirit filled with concern admits that slaughtering the True Monarchs is not morally right. Sanhe spent two hundred years, beginning in the first year of the Immortal Calendar, asking Pan Huang, Bu Tianque, Alchemy Cauldron, Emperor Cang, and the Heavenly Lord for another solution. None had one beyond waiting for the Dao Lords to return.

Sanhe therefore accepts that the decision reflects the limits of his own abilities. He can only kill every cultivator capable of helping the Ancestral Dragon escape, then kill himself, the strongest remaining point through which the Ancestral Dragon could borrow power.

## Survival as leverage

The cold Sanhe spirit argues that the Dao Lords cannot be trusted and that Sanhe should remain alive. Because the Ancestral Dragon has established a connection with Sanhe, His remaining chance of escape could be turned into leverage against the Dao Lords if They move to kill Si Sui.

Sanhe rejects this as sophistry. If release were truly the goal, he should have kept Pan Huang and the others alive. The survival argument arose only after Sanhe himself became the final necessary sacrifice, exposing that this spirit was afraid to die.

## The Ancestral Dragon's revelation

The demonic third spirit is the Ancestral Dragon Himself. His will crosses the void and descends upon the divided Sanhe, covering His face in fine scales until He resembles a True Dragon in human form.

The Ancestral Dragon reveals that the Primordial Saint and the other Dao Lords intend to create another history, replace the current one, and use the false history to suppress Si Sui. He describes generations of mortals and cultivators as consumable materials to be harvested.

He argues that the Primordial Saint's command of Destiny will prevent any future Dao Lord from rising to defeat Him. If the plan succeeds, Sanhe's sacrifice will become an illusory bubble within karma, altered and buried until no one, including Sanhe, remembers it.

## Sanhe's sacrifice and Lü Yang's answer

Sanhe concludes that his actions still matter to the people alive at that moment, even if karma is later rewritten and history dissolves into an illusory bubble. His three divided spirits merge back into one beneath a single conviction of the Dao Heart.

The Grand Sword Patriarch takes his own life in Jiangnan during the first month of the 264th year of the Immortal Calendar. His spotless blade bears the seal-script inscription that giving one's life for righteousness means valuing righteousness above life itself.

The Ancestral Dragon's fury lingers around Sanhe's corpse for three full months. He cannot accept that the deed will be erased and that the Sword Sovereign would never have allowed Sanhe to survive regardless.

Lü Yang reaches through time, parts karma, and appears before Sanhe's corpse. His Primordial Spirit illuminates history and karma as he tells the Ancestral Dragon that he saw the sacrifice, remembers it, and will bring its meaning back from history.

Lü Yang's Dao resonance flows downstream with the deed's karma and returns to the Sea of Light. He declares that all shall know the sword that is blameless before the world, though not before men.
