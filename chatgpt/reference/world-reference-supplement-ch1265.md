# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1265

## Palace of Soaring Clouds and the Heavenly Emperor

The Palace of Soaring Clouds stands within the Heavenly Palace beyond flights of white jade steps. Its interior is arranged in distinct layers like ranks of court ministers and contains rows of divine statues. Four exalted seats stand at the top.

The Heavenly Emperor is Lü Yang's separated consciousness within the Heavenly Palace. He has the same aura as Lü Yang, but his domineering presence, supreme self-regard, and indifference toward everything beyond himself distinguish him from the Lü Yang whom Flying Snow previously met.

True Monarch Flying Snow arrives as a soul with Fate-Reforming, Flowing Downward, and the protection of a strand of mighty power from the Great Dao of Yin. She takes the Divine Rank corresponding to Divine Offerings. Flowing Downward helps her overcome the rank's resistance and merge with it.

## The ten Great Daos used for ascension

The Primordial Saint uses Destiny to anchor the intended result despite the loss of Flying Snow and Flowing Downward. The resulting structure for Divine Transformation and Ascension assembles all ten Great Daos in an uneven, patched-together form:

- the Five Heavenly Numbers are Fate, Fortune, Destiny, Tribulation, and Variables;
- the Underworld replaces the Five Elements;
- Karma replaces Yin and Yang;
- the Ancestral Dragon's body replaces the Dharma Body;
- Spells and Spiritual Power already have their corresponding Dao Lords.

The Ancestral Dragon draws upon Tribulation, while Divine Transformation and Ascension itself supplies the greatest Variable since time immemorial. The Ancestral Dragon's physical body breaks free when the Immortal Pivot shatters, then disintegrates into fuel for the Other Shore's ascent.

## The World-Honored One abandons His Dao attainment

True Monarch Radiant World Western Light is the historical foundation through which the World-Honored One proved the Dao five thousand years ago. He abandons the final step inside the False History while the World-Honored One burns away and disappears from the Other Shore.

This alters cause and changes effect. In the rewritten history, the World-Honored One never became a Dao Lord, never participated in the battle 129,600 years ago, and therefore never caused Si Sui's death. The False History begins returning, and Karma can no longer fill the position of Yin and Yang, halting Divine Transformation and Ascension.
