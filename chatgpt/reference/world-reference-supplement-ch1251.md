## Jiangbei campaign

Chapter 1251 begins Lü Yang's direct campaign against Jiangbei. He attacks the Heaven-Connecting Sea of Clouds from Jiangxi with the twelve Water and Fire Fruition Attainment stars. The clouds of the True History's notorious demonic stronghold remain an immortal paradise in the False History, where `Saint Sect` is still a term of praise.

## The Heaven-Connecting Sea of Clouds defenses

Lian Tiandou and Bu Tianque defend the mountain gate from Sacred Fire Cliff. Bu Tianque activates the Thirty-Three Heavens Vertical and Horizontal Go Formation. The formation converts external forces into black Go stones and turns the battle away from cultivation and status into a contest of Dao Attainment.

Overturned Lamp Fire's Illuminating Law is a direct counter to hidden mechanisms. It reveals every node, operating principle, flaw, and defect of the formation, enabling the other eleven Fruition Attainments to shatter it in fewer than three breaths.

## Lian Tiandou's displayed methods

Chapter 1251 directly shows Lian Tiandou wielding:

- the Profound Heaven Upright Crown, whose clear profound light can briefly halt the twelve Fruition Attainment stars and suppress the myriad phenomena;
- the Eight Negations Wisdom Sword, which cuts neither flesh nor spells but severs thought;
- a three-legged bronze cauldron that releases Purple-Azure Tushita Fire;
- a jade fly-whisk, unnamed and therefore generic, that sweeps away rules and Mysteries.

The chapter does not establish a separate formal name for the bronze cauldron or the jade fly-whisk.

## Heavenly Fire and Inscribing the Heavenly Chapter

Heavenly Fire appears as the Supreme Fire Element Fruition Attainment and becomes sovereign of the twelve Fruition Attainments. Through Inscribing the Heavenly Chapter, it issues a decree that Lian Tiandou shall take his own life.

The decree does not merely compel a direct act. It rewrites the logical operation of every action concerning Lian Tiandou so that the result tends toward suicide: medicine becomes poison, protection becomes attack, and sweeping away hostile Mysteries also destroys his own methods. Once his treasures and Mysteries cease responding, Lü Yang captures him with Heaven in a Palm.

## Cultural and formatting note

The Chapter 1251 displayed decree retains bold corner brackets in English. This is a specific formal-proclamation format and does not alter the ordinary formatting rule for standalone technique or artifact call-outs.
