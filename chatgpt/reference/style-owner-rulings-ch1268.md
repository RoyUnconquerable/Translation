# Owner Style Rulings: Chapter 1268

The owner's edited Chapter 1268 prose is governing. Apply both its locked diction and its broader handling of temporal chaos, religious cadence, social systems, historical proclamation, and climactic reversals.

## Religious and collective cadence

- Capitalize *Buddhist Dharma* when referring to the World-Honored One's body of teaching.
- The approved response to `善哉。善哉！` is *How virtuous! How virtuous!* Preserve the paired exclamation and devotional register.
- `十万亿佛土` is *ten trillion Buddha-lands*. Preserve the exact scale.
- Keep the collective image chain in order: billions of chants rise, grains of sand gather into a stupa, myriad hearts become one, and the False and True Histories connect.
- Worshippers *offered incense and raised the gods through their worship*. This makes the upward support explicit without overexplaining the mechanism.

## Temporal chaos and cosmic imagery

- Prefer the approved dynamic sequence inside the singularity: time *had fallen into chaos*; past, present, and future *churned together*; cause and effect existed *all at once*; and the images *dissolved into a kaleidoscope of dazzling light*.
- The light *pierced the clouds like heavenly radiance* and shone directly into Lü Yang's eyes. Keep the image physical and directional.
- The compressed False History can span 129,600 years or *last for no more than a single instant*. *All the accumulation of that entire span* can then be condensed into one moment.
- Use *far surpassing the total time* when comparing Chongguang's one hundred thousand years inside the False History with all his earlier cultivation.

## Social-system exposition

- Prefer a concrete worship relationship: the common people offer incense, their worship raises the gods, and their prosperity strengthens the gods they uphold.
- `人财` is *human capital*. Preserve the deliberately modern economic register.
- Chongguang *much preferred* the False History. The following sentence begins *Here,* to make the contrast immediate: cultivation no longer demanded constant intrigue or risking one's life.

## Formal historical proclamation

- The bold bracketed proclamation is *All of you shall know the great deeds by which I surpassed those who came before!*
- Begin the formal account with *The True Monarch*. He *ascended the celestial throne*, commanded the *Mandate of Heaven*, spread the *Divine Dao*, *held the reins of Heaven*, and commanded Creation.
- Preserve the balanced conquest clauses and their approved authority language: *sovereign and ministers alike*, *under His rule*, *surrendered their seals of office*, and *pledged allegiance*.
- `天神地祇` is *the gods of heaven and earth*. The conclusion is *established ninety-nine provinces, and brought peace to all beneath the heavens*.
- Elevated syntax is appropriate within the proclamation, but each clause must retain a clear actor, conquest, submission, and consequence.

## Mechanical normalization

- Normalize the obvious typo `settlling` to `settling` in the phrase *obtained from settling Skyward's experiences*.
- Normalize the stray nonbreaking space between the two *How virtuous!* exclamations. Neither normalization changes the owner's intended prose.
