## Chapter 1250: Everything Becomes Clear!

The more than eighty remaining pages of the Book of a Hundred Lives restore Lü Yang's confidence that repeated lives can expose every trump card the Primordial Saint holds. He asks Si Sui how to answer the Dao Lord testing them.

Si Sui infers that the test piece is nothing more than a Foundation Establishment True Person from the Immortal Pivot in the True History. Directly seeking the fundamental Five Elements requires exceptional talent and brilliance. This higher perspective lets Lü Yang identify Chongguang, who ranks second only to Demon-Purging True Person among the Immortal Pivot's Foundation Establishment cultivators and serves as the Saint Sect's Acting Sect Master. Across Lü Yang's restarts, Chongguang has become a True Monarch three times, including one life in which he rapidly mastered the unfamiliar path of seeking the Golden Core through Heart Demons.

Lü Yang reconstructs the move. Chongguang was an Immortal Spirit in his previous life, so the karma tied to his ancient original body should still exist in this era, most likely in Jiangbei. The Heavenly Lord would watch such a being closely. Its earlier obstruction of Lü Yang's karmic calculation and the One Mind's ability to control consciousness lead Lü Yang to conclude that the World-Honored One controls the Heavenly Lord and selected Chongguang as His piece.

The information Si Sui supplies gives Lü Yang confidence that he can negotiate with the World-Honored One whether He treats him as friend or foe, with a real chance of drawing Him into Lü Yang's camp. Lü Yang withdraws from the Foundation Establishment puppet and decides to seize Jiangbei. Unlike Lü Yang, Chongguang cannot hide in plain sight as an Immortal Spirit and must isolate himself completely, but his identity and birthplace remain clues. Once Jiangbei is under Lü Yang's control, finding him is only a matter of time.

At the Heaven-Connecting Sea of Clouds, Lian Tiandou predicts Lü Yang's approach. Jiangdong and Jiangnan share the same qi, their branches intertwined, while the Grand Sword Patriarch's presence in Jiangnan is deterrent enough to make Lü Yang turn to the next best target. Bu Tianque calls this pinching the soft persimmon first. Lian Tiandou chooses to fight because he believes Lü Yang may hold answers to his questions, and Bu Tianque agrees to follow his lead.

Lian Tiandou then senses that something is wrong with the memories that Eldest Senior Brother died and Little Junior Brother disappeared. Looking at the Immortal Pivot's familiar sky, he once again swallows the question he cannot bring himself to ask: what lies beyond the Immortal Pivot, and why do its many True Monarchs and Dao Lineages remain confined within it, circling endlessly, without anyone thinking to venture into the Sea of Light? The remembered world begins to seem illusory.
