# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1276 Owner Final

The Dao Lords watch the Other Shore fall while the False History and True History converge and transform the mortal world. The Underworld remains intact, Si Sui is about to escape, and Lü Yang's God-Sealing Dao Lineage has severed the Dao Lords' connection to the Sea of Light.

The restored Dharma Body Dao will reunite the Three Foundations and strengthen the Spell Dao Lord and Du Xuan. Fearing the growing number of competitors, the Spell Dao Lord supports the Sword Sovereign's proposal to dismantle the first and second layers of the Other Shore and force Dao Tianqi to intervene again.

The Primordial Saint instead watches Si Sui emerge from the converging torrents of karma. He hopes Si Sui can reclaim the missing 129,600 years, turn from Postnatal back to Innate through cultivation alone, and become a Dao Lord as the Ancestral Dragon did.

Lü Yang meets the fire of the falling heavens with the collective projection created through Divine Offerings. True Monarch Flying Snow contributes her strength and affirms that bending and adapting still counts as relying on oneself.

Drop of Heaven's Essence contributes the Eight Trigrams and the transformations of the myriad phenomena. Demon-Purging True Person joins in silence.

The Sea of Dust and Tribulation brings forth Skyward, who complains about being imprisoned for 120,000 years. Lü Yang promises that surviving the crisis will completely open Skyward's Dao Path.

The Nether Whisper Ancestor enters with Heaven in a Palm. Fate-Reforming draws the Heavenly Palace into the Sea of Light, and both the palace and Lü Yang's true body merge into the projection.

The projection grows until it nearly fills the Sea of Light and then surges upward. The Dao Lords watch in surprise because the Sea of Light has stood up against the falling Other Shore.
