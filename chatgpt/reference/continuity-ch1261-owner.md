# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1261 Owner Final

Lü Yang and the Heavenly Lord continue their staged pursuit toward Jiangxi. Lü Yang theatrically screams, sacrifices the apparent light of successive Water and Fire Fruition Attainments, and claims the Heavenly Lord won dishonorably. Pan Huang rises as if to cover Lü Yang's retreat, forcing Sublime Bliss to decide before Lü Yang reaches safety.

Sublime Bliss intervenes with the Yellow Millet. His Taiyi body forms an illusory pagoda from countless thoughts of consciousness and reinforces the dream plane. Its imagery treats the mortal world as a dream and cultivation as sinking ever deeper. The offered awakening requires surrendering body, soul, memory, thought, spiritual power, and divine abilities, making it death in truth.

Sublime Bliss plans to strike only once and immediately escape. Instead, Great Post Earth's Boundless Dao seals every route through heaven and earth. He finds himself trapped between Lü Yang and Jiangxi. More than ten True Monarchs and Great True Monarchs of the Three Foundations attack from Jiangxi under the Nether Whisper Ancestor and Demon-Purging True Person, while Lü Yang attacks from the front and the Heavenly Lord joins the assault from behind.

The pincer attack shatters the Yellow Millet dream and reduces Sublime Bliss's illusory pagoda to the size of a palm. Heavenly Fire and Heaven in a Palm combine their imagery of Destiny. An imperial edict summons Taiyi Beast Sublime Bliss before the Imperial Court, and Lü Yang takes him into the five immortal mountains, sun, and moon within his palm.

With the last enemy in the Immortal Pivot subdued, Lü Yang needs only time. He intends to use the God-Sealing Method to control the False History, follow time downstream to its end, reverse cause and effect, and restore history to its proper course. Because time is unfixed in the False History, a hundred thousand years can pass in no more than an instant.

The False History undergoes the greatest karmic shift in its existence. Outside it, observers announce that its main channel has changed and its course has been fixed, although the future remains hazy.

The World-Honored One ignores them and watches the Underworld. True Monarch Azure Firmament has assumed the identity of the First-Generation Alchemy Cauldron Peak Lord, and the Nascent Soul Pill is ready. The three allied Dao Lords have accelerated its refinement, completing all preparations for Divine Transformation and Ascension.

The World-Honored One challenges the Primordial Saint in thought. The tiny figure at the end of the Other Shore moves, the Nascent Soul Pill's radiance crashes from the Underworld into the mortal world, and Divine Transformation and Ascension begins once more.
