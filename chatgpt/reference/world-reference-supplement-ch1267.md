# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1267

## The Primordial Saint anchors the True History

The Primordial Saint mistakes the Heavenly Emperor's inability to understand the Seven Emotions Heaven-Fathoming Art for an inexplicable principled refusal. He never considers lack of talent because He assumes that anyone capable of constructing the Heavenly Palace must possess enough wisdom light and comprehension to understand a Primordial Spirit method.

The Primordial Saint considers abandoning Divine Transformation and Ascension to eliminate the Heavenly Palace, but refuses. His pursuit of the Great Dao is the last fragment of His true nature and the foundation of His True Self. Abandoning it would make Him unworthy of pursuing the Dao.

He descends from the sixth layer of the Other Shore to the fifth and uses Destiny to anchor the Sea of Light's current True History. This temporarily halts the returning False History, like a dam holding back a flood of Variables, and buys the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky time to salvage the Heavenly Dao.

## The Heavenly Dao restarts the False History

The Primordial Saint orders the Sword Sovereign to use the Heavenly Dao to replace the gap left by Karma. Otherwise, He will release His suppression and allow the Heavenly Dao to collapse, forcing Them both to begin again.

The World-Honored One remains in control of Karma after leaving the Other Shore, but His cultivation and status have fallen to peak Golden Core. Without His Dao Lord status, He can no longer serve as the immovable anchor that restrained the Heavenly Dao's control over the False History.

The Heavenly Dao manifests at the False History's source like an enormous spiderweb. It collapses the entire 129,600-year history, rolls it back to the instant after the war between the Dao Lords, and begins it anew. Emperor Cang, the six Dragon Monarchs, the Heavenly Lord, and the Grand Sword Patriarch reappear like dreams, illusions, bubbles, and shadows given new life.

## A Guest Rejected by Heaven and Earth

The Sword Sovereign expects the restarted False History to follow Her will, but it perfectly reproduces its prior course. Within the False History's Immortal Pivot, Lü Yang has activated the Prismatic Talent A Guest Rejected by Heaven and Earth.

The talent can be freely activated or deactivated. While active, Lü Yang's history is unique and cannot be affected by any external force. This prevents the Heavenly Dao from rewriting the False History's established course.
