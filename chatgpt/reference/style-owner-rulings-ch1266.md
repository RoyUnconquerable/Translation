# Owner Style Rulings: Chapter 1266

The owner's edited Chapter 1266 prose is governing. Apply not only its locked terms but also its sentence-level judgment, image control, transitions, and comic timing to later chapters.

## Controlled cosmic description

- Prefer concrete visual movement over decorative grandeur. Mighty power *descended from the very summit*, filled the universe, and froze the mortal world in place; the gaze then *fell from an infinitely lofty place*.
- Keep a recurring image lexically stable when the source deliberately repeats it. The Primordial Saint's emotions disturb a *calm lake*, and His restored state has the *calm indifference of an unruffled lake*.
- Use *What met His eyes* for a newly revealed vista. Flights of steps, rows of palaces, rosy light, immortal qi, ordered gods, clear laws, and celestial order should accumulate into one coherent visual progression rather than a static inventory.
- When the source describes an elevated realm by contrast, preserve the full relation: it seems to be a fifth super Boundary Heaven, yet stands above the mundane world as a true dwelling place of immortals and gods.

## Natural causal and character prose

- Favor clear, idiomatic causal phrasing over mechanically mirrored syntax: *both reason and inclination told him to accept*, *seeking only the Dao, regarding all else as false*, and *He saw through the person's origins*.
- Use *person* for a gender-neutral or identity-focused reference when gender itself is not the point. Avoid unnecessarily repeating *man* or *other man*.
- Prefer compact physical outcomes: the Primordial Saint would *crush both the Heavenly Emperor and the Heavenly Palace into ash*. Do not add redundant image layers such as *flying ash* when the simpler form carries the force.
- *His profound voice resounded, carrying a note of patient persuasion* is the approved natural rendering for a formal speaker whose tone is coaxing. Maintain dignity without stiffening the sentence.
- Use *rapid success*, *leaves behind considerable hidden dangers*, and *first awakened the seven emotions, then cast them aside one by one* for clear cultivation logic.

## Thought, capitalization, and comic timing

- A short implicit private question such as *Si Sui?* is direct internal thought and remains italicized.
- `真我` is *True Self* in this context, and Lü Yang's originating self is *My True Body* within the Heavenly Emperor's direct thought.
- Keep `wisdom light` lowercase. The blunt sentence *He simply wasn't talented enough* should immediately establish the practical limitation.
- Preserve the modern profanity and triple exclamation marks in *I can't fucking understand it!!!* The elevated cultivation offer must collapse into this abrupt, concrete failure.
- The final joke depends on restraint. After lofty ambitions of transcending the mortal world, end with the separate, plain verdict *He simply lacked the ability.* Do not embellish or explain it.

## Broader editing preference

- Owner edits are stylistic evidence, not merely substitutions. Future drafts should internalize the tighter visual sequencing, lighter connective tissue, natural referent choice, precise causal language, and restrained punch lines shown here instead of waiting for mechanical phrase replacement.
