# Owner Style Rulings: Chapter 1262

The owner's edited Chapter 1262 prose is governing. Apply these choices to later chapters where the same terms, imagery, or narrative situations recur.

## Natural action and description

- Prefer exact, fluid movement over mechanically tracking the Chinese verb: golden light descends from the *Outer Heavens*, a finger *slips past* the Sword Sovereign and Vast Sky, then *whisks* the Nascent Soul Pill away.
- The approved pill description uses *floodwaters bursting through a dike*, a pill *wreathed in fragrant mist*, and *dragon-and-tiger patterns winding across its surface*. Do not add *talisman* when the immediate wording only requires patterns.
- In the Primordial Saint's seizure of the pill, prefer *Everything had been timed to perfection*, *revealing just how terrifying His methods truly were*, and *two fingers to settle the final outcome*.
- `电光石火间` may be the compact *in that instant* in a fast action transition when the lightning-and-flint image is not developed elsewhere. Do not force a conspicuous literal phrase that slows the beat.

## Logical and rhetorical phrasing

- The Sword Sovereign's accusation is ordered cleanly: everything was a trap laid by the Primordial Saint, even Lian Tiandou's Nascent Soul Pill plan was bait, and the victim was schemed against every step of the way to his death.
- Vast Sky's concern is *Beginning Divine Transformation and Ascension at a time like this could easily cause something to go wrong.*
- The Sword Sovereign says, *No plan can ever keep pace with change*, followed by each Dao Lord relying on Their own abilities to see who has the last laugh.
- Dao Tianqi says he never *accepted* his Eldest Senior Brother's judgment and that he never studied *schemes or maneuvering*.

## Names, titles, and call-outs

- `太易天` is the personal name *Tai Yitian*, the Primordial Saint's true name.
- `幽冥府君殿` is *Underworld Sovereign's Hall* and `冥府之主` is *Lord of the Underworld*.
- When explaining the isolated glyph `【天】`, use *the character for Heaven*. The standalone source paragraph remains the plain call-out *Heaven!*
- Keep *True Monarch Azure Firmament* and subsequent capitalized divine pronouns in this scene. The later revelation that the Cangjiang Dragon Monarch invoked the Primordial Saint's true name does not justify silently changing the source's title sequence.
