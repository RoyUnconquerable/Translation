# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1258 Owner Final

Lü Yang uses the World-Honored One as a benchmark for the relative strength of the Dao Lords but does not trust His assessment completely. After earning the right to negotiate through their battle, Lü Yang asks for help claiming the entire Immortal Pivot. The World-Honored One agrees not to interfere in Jiangdong or Jiangbei and says the Heavenly Lord will no longer obstruct Lü Yang, but excludes Jiangnan.

The World-Honored One uses Lü Yang's question about the Grand Sword Patriarch and his alliance with Skyward to infer that he arose after Skyward. He estimates that Lü Yang has cultivated for no more than ten thousand years and perhaps fewer than five thousand. Lü Yang realizes he exposed part of his origins.

Lü Yang recounts Sanhe's sixty-year purge of demons, during which the Grand Sword Patriarch annihilated the Three Foundations, Dao Court, Saint Sect, and Sword Pavilion before taking his own life. The World-Honored One reveals that Sanhe is the Grand Sword Patriarch's true name. Its utterance triggers the Heavenly Dao, so He seals the conversation from outside perception.

The World-Honored One explains that the False History has diverged from the original past. Because He and Beast-Taming attained Primordial Spirits and the True Self is unique, neither has a native counterpart within the current False History.

During the war between Dao Lords, only the World-Honored One, Beast-Taming, and Sanhe had meaningful freedom to choose, and Sanhe only counted as half. Beast-Taming chose the Underworld, hoping to gather the dead cultivators' True Spirits, bring back old friends, and resolve the Dao Lords' conflict. His Utmost Nature Immortal-Seeking Scripture made the fundamental nature of his character almost impossible to change.

The World-Honored One chose the Primordial Saint in order to obtain a position on the Other Shore and protect Beast-Taming, Heaven-Mending, and Alchemy Cauldron. He failed. Lü Yang privately concludes that without his own intervention, none of the four first-generation Peak Lords could escape their disastrous outcomes.

Sanhe chose the mortals. The Ancestral Dragon sought to exploit the Dao Lord war, contact the Heavenly Lord, and break free through the Overseas True Dragons. Because the Immortal Pivot is built on His body, His escape would instantly kill its trillions upon trillions of mortals. Sanhe's sixty-year purge and sword hung in the heavens suppressed the final counterattack.

The World-Honored One finally reveals that the Ancestral Dragon nurtured the first cultivators. All cultivators trace their origins to Him, and the cultivation He bestowed can be reclaimed. This danger explains why Si Sui agreed to suppress Him.

The owner-final title and echoed counterfactual are *Without Me, the First-Generation Peak Lords Would Have Been Finished!* The perfect conditional is required because Lü Yang is judging completed alternative history.
