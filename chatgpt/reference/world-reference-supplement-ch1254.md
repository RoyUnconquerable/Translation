# World Reference Supplement, Chapter 1254

## Emperor Cang's execution
Heavenly Fire suppresses Emperor Cang's Mysteries and the Immortal Kingdom Daoist Law. Inscribing the Heavenly Chapter manifests the judicial state `Beheading, Pending Review` above him. When the Heavenly Lord declines to intervene, Lü Yang changes the sentence to `Beheading, Immediate Execution!` and kills Emperor Cang. His three avatars split apart and shatter, and his premature death leaves a trace in the river of the False History because of his identity and karma.

## Heavenly Fire imagery
Chapter 1254 opens with a formal description of Heavenly Fire: intertwined radiant spirits blaze upward; its radiance adorns the heavens as the sun and rises from the sea as the moon; after passing the Gate of Heaven and spreading across the land, all illuminated beneath sun and moon become subjects and servants.

## The first-generation Myriad Treasures Peak Lord
The figure manifested within the Heavenly Lord's accumulated thunder is recognized by Lian Tiandou and Bu Tianque as their Eldest Senior Brother, the first-generation Myriad Treasures Peak Lord. Bu Tianque initially calls it a remnant shadow extracted by the Heavenly Lord, but the Grand Sword Patriarch's observation establishes a crucial rule: a true Primordial Spirit leaves no trace in heaven and earth, and even his own briefly condensed Primordial Spirit traces cannot be called upon by the Heavenly Lord without consent.

## The World-Honored One's descent
Lü Yang concludes that the manifestation is not merely a remnant shadow but the World-Honored One genuinely descending by borrowing the Heavenly Lord's power. False History constraints restrict this incarnation to about Treading the Heavens and below peak Golden Core, unlike the World-Honored One's normal Dao Lord descents or His use of the Five Directional Tathagatas of the Vajra Realm.
