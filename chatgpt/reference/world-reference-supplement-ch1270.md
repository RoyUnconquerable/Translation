# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1270

## Lü Yang's Dao Lord perspective

After completing his Primordial Spirit, Lü Yang sees the True History's Sea of Light through the churning Yin-Yang Creation and Destruction Profound Light. The entire Sea of Light becomes as clear as the lines on his palm. He can observe Boundary Heavens being born and destroyed, mortal life within them, and individual cultivators in seclusion.

Lü Yang concludes that the defining foundation of a Dao Lord is not cultivation realm but the Primordial Spirit's perspective, which encompasses the Sea of Light and looks down upon all phenomena. Peak Golden Core cultivators could therefore be called Dao Lords before the Other Shore existed.

True Monarch Supreme Dharma once reached only the Immortal Bridge realm but recklessly raised his power, risked losing himself, and displayed a Dao Lord's bearing for one instant. This demonstrates that the gap below peak Golden Core could be bridged temporarily.

## The convergence of the two histories

Lü Yang sees the True History and False History as two torrents converging ahead of him. During convergence, the cause and effect of both histories remain simultaneously valid and invalid. They must converge before diverging again.

Si Sui cannot recover his missing 129,600 years by escaping through brute force during convergence. He must wait until the histories diverge and cause and effect are anchored, then flow downstream so all missing time is restored. Only in that complete state can he face the Primordial Saint.

## Sanhe's three histories

The Grand Sword Patriarch appears as a brilliant fish capable of briefly leaping free from the torrents. Lü Yang's flawless Primordial Spirit lets him read three versions of Sanhe's history: the current True History, the current False History, and the original history from which Dao Lords with Primordial Spirits later broke free.

Sanhe was born with three innate spirits, suffered madness and rejection, and nearly became a beggar. The Sword Sovereign, Nian Yao, found him and transformed his burden into an advantage. She became his teacher, mother, and friend, deliberately cultivating his upright character and deep concern for mortals.

The Sword Sovereign taught Sanhe tirelessly and frequently brought him to speak with Si Sui. Lü Yang realizes She was exploiting the principle that a gentleman can be deceived through what is right and proper, preparing to cash in on Sanhe's cultivated virtue at a decisive moment.

## The Sword Sovereign's request

Before the war between Dao Lords, the Sword Sovereign tells Sanhe that Si Sui intends to attack the Primordial Saint because the Other Shore has another purpose. She falsely claims ignorance of the deeper conflict and says the Dao Lords will enter the Fathomless Void to persuade Them not to fight.

She then warns that no Dao Lord will remain to guard the Sea of Light and that the recently suppressed Ancestral Dragon may break free. Speaking with apparent compassion for the entire world, She asks Sanhe to protect every living being in the Sea of Light.
