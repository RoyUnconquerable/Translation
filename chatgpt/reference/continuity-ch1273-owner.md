# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1273 Owner Final

Within the torrent of karma, Lü Yang realizes that witnessing the erased first history through his Primordial Spirit inserts his existence into that history. The present Ancestral Dragon will remember Lü Yang's words, allowing Lü Yang to carry the lost karma back into the mortal world.

Lü Yang declines to judge Sanhe's sixty-year purge of demons as right or wrong. He acknowledges the enemies it created but refuses to let the deed remain buried.

The Grand Sword Patriarch manifests from the brilliant fish in the historical torrent and recovers the truth of his forgotten death. His strongest moment came when he defeated both the Ancestral Dragon and himself, took his own life, and refined his Dao Heart.

Sanhe's three innate spirits have permanently sealed his Dao Path to a stable Primordial Spirit. Even if history returns to its rightful course, he cannot break through beyond his half-step state.

Sanhe experienced the God-Sealing Method's mortal world across more than one hundred thousand years of False History and came to love it. He accepts that his first sacrifice belongs to the past and turns his attention to the descending Dao Lords.

Sanhe warns that Lü Yang's cultivation is sufficient but his methods in battle cannot yet contend with the Sword Sovereign. He offers himself as a blade for Lü Yang to wield.

The Sword Pavilion's mountain gate tears free of the earth, Extreme Heaven Cliff becomes its edge, and Sanhe is shattered and reforged into the supreme dharma sword. He unites his three spirits, briefly manifests a Primordial Spirit, and sacrifices it to the sword.

Sanhe considers this a middling plan and welcomes it because only he must die. He has already left his legacy to Lü Yang's companion, who has refined three Sword Intents and has a better chance of attaining a Primordial Spirit.

The sword cry reaches the Sword Sovereign on the fourth layer of the Other Shore. She realizes that Sanhe has died again and taken the sword She left as the Sword Pavilion's mountain gate, then regrets not killing him more thoroughly.

Divine Transformation and Ascension has failed, and the Other Shore is falling toward the mortal world like a blazing sun. The Dao Lords will soon descend one after another.

Lü Yang names Sanhe's dharma sword Heaven-Slayer, draws it into his sleeve, and holds its power in reserve for the coming battle.
