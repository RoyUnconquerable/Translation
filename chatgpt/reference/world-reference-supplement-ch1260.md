# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1260

## The World-Honored One's change of sides

After Lü Yang relays Si Sui's demand that the World-Honored One study under him for a hundred thousand years, the World-Honored One accepts the answer with relief. He is wary of Si Sui but not truly afraid. His earlier plea and show of weakness were meant to establish a new stance because He is boarding a new ship and leaving His old methods behind.

The World-Honored One refuses to cultivate the God-Sealing Method. He recognizes Lü Yang's recruitment tactic because He used the same approach while founding the Pure Land, carrying the Mahayana True Enlightenment Scripture around and promising that anyone who mastered it would immediately become a Dao Lord.

Lü Yang recognizes that accepting the World-Honored One into his Dao Lineage before refining a Primordial Spirit would risk letting His tail grow too large to control. He nevertheless considers the alliance worthwhile while the Primordial Saint remains the greatest enemy.

## Time, Karma, and the Primordial Saint

Lü Yang tells the World-Honored One that the Primordial Saint originally proved the Dao through Time. The River of Time was the foundation upon which He proved the Dao and later became the Great Net of Karma.

The revelation causes the World-Honored One to deduce the consequences if the Primordial Saint personally killed Him. The exact deduction remains unfinished in speech, but it enrages Him and removes His remaining hesitation about acting against the Primordial Saint.

## The Immortal Pivot agreement

The World-Honored One states that He has already seized control of the Heavenly Lord's body and will no longer obstruct Lü Yang. He will also notify Alchemy Cauldron and Heaven-Mending, leaving Lü Yang free to spread the God-Sealing Method through Jiangdong and Jiangbei.

He judges that Sanhe's Dao Heart cultivation is sufficient to recognize the False History as unreal. Because the Sword Sovereign severed Sanhe's path and continued life has become torment, Sanhe seeks both awakening from the dream and eternal sleep. The World-Honored One predicts that Sanhe will not act before Lü Yang refines a Primordial Spirit.

The remaining Variable is Sublime Bliss, whose true body is the Taiyi Beast raised by Myriad Spells. The World-Honored One considers bringing the beast to heel manageable.

## The staged defeat

Lü Yang and the World-Honored One stage Lü Yang's defeat above the firmament. One aura falls, the twelve Fruition Attainment stars dim and flee toward Jiangxi, and Lü Yang loudly accuses the Heavenly Lord of besting him by a move and a half. Divine silhouettes rise throughout Jiangxi and the Overseas, severing the passage between Heaven and Earth as if receiving him back.

Sublime Bliss accepts the deception. He concludes that the Heavenly Lord nearly killed Lü Yang but lacked the final weight needed to decide the battle. Believing the Heavenly Lord and Lü Yang to be irreconcilable enemies, he dismisses the possibility of a trap and considers joining the Heavenly Lord to prevent Lü Yang's escape. He views this as the only chance in his life to preserve the Yellow Millet's hope of proving the Dao.
