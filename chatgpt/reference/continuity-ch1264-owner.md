# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1264 Owner Final

The Other Shore, once as elusive as a divine dragon whose head could be seen but never its tail, emerges into view because it is falling. Its descent strains the Sea of Light and produces near-apocalyptic destruction across the Immortal Pivot, Celestial Court, Star Palace, Mysterious Wall, and Boundary Heavens great and small.

Dao Tianqi opens the Underworld and steps out beneath the falling Other Shore. Endless death qi surges forth and becomes pillars that prop up the heavens. Taking the place of the Five Elements, the Underworld halts the descent and spares the Boundary Heavens below and their billions of living beings from being crushed.

Dao Tianqi denounces the Primordial Saint without calling Him Master. The Primordial Saint remains perfectly at ease because He predicted that Beast-Taming could never stand by while the Other Shore destroyed the Sea of Light.

The World-Honored One recognizes another open scheme. If He tries to dissuade Beast-Taming or drive Him away, every other Dao Lord will turn Their arrows on Him because Dao Tianqi is saving the Sea of Light. The Primordial Saint has solved two problems with one move: Tribulation answers the Ancestral Dragon, while the Underworld replaces the Five Elements.

The Primordial Saint has not taken so much as one step down, yet He turns the entire situation to His advantage as effortlessly as lifting the heaviest burden without weight or turning over His palm. The World-Honored One suppresses His anger and concludes that the true battle will be decided on the road to Divine Transformation and Ascension.

After the hard scene break, the Ancestral Dragon becomes Tribulation's only choice because neither Skyward nor Sublime Bliss contests Him. He reaches Sacred Fire Cliff intending to use the Usurpation of the Huai to possess True Monarch Flying Snow, use her Empty Attainment of Flowing Downward as a buffer, and draw upon Tribulation as fuel.

The Ancestral Dragon instead finds Flying Snow's body an empty shell. Her soul and Flowing Downward have vanished. The Primordial Saint notices residual crimson light between her brows and deduces that someone escaped the Heavenly Dao's restrictions, built a Dao-attainment ground in the Fathomless Void, and drew Flying Snow away.

The Primordial Saint first considers Myriad Treasures, then rejects Him because He has already accounted for nearly every method the World-Honored One possesses. He considers a seventh person beyond the six present Dao Lords and the mysterious lesser cultivator whose consciousness entered the False History.

Recognizing a new Variable, the Primordial Saint finally descends from the summit of the Other Shore, moving from the seventh layer to the sixth.
