# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1279 Owner Final

Du Xuan calmly analyzes Lü Yang's fist after being knocked out of the Sea of Light. He determines that it is dharma in essence, mobilizes nearly all ten Great Daos except the Dharma Body and Yin and Yang, and combines influences from Si Sui, the Ancestral Dragon, and the Primordial Saint.

Vast Sky proposes recruiting Lü Yang so the God-Sealing Dao Lineage can restore and strengthen the Heavenly Dao. The Sword Sovereign refuses. She judges that Lü Yang already has his own place where he attained the Dao, resembles Myriad Treasures, and would try to claim the Heavenly Dao for himself.

The Sword Sovereign leaves the fourth layer of the Other Shore and attacks through the River of Fate. Countless possible futures converge upon Lü Yang's death. Sword Edge Metal can only imitate this Mystery and cannot equal Fate itself.

Lü Yang draws Heaven-Slayer. Sanhe's sacrificed Primordial Spirit appears and affirms that, although he knows the Sword Sovereign exploited his humanity, his values and his sixty-year purge were his own choices. He will therefore turn his sword against Her without hesitation when She threatens the Sea of Light.

As the Other Shore finally falls, Lü Yang recalls the helpless endings of his previous lives, merges with Heaven-Slayer and the Sea of Light, and advances instead of retreating. He intends to sever the Dao, slay Heaven, and overturn Heaven's Will, ending with the challenge: *Bitch, face my sword!*
