# World Reference Supplement, Chapter 1255

## Shi Tianyi and the World-Honored One

In the False History, the World-Honored One personally appears through the identity/body of the first-generation Myriad Treasures Peak Lord Shi Tianyi. He recognizes that Lü Yang has uncovered the Chongguang arrangement and reasons that a Dao Lord, almost certainly Si Sui, is guiding him.

The World-Honored One sees Si Sui's approval as evidence that Lü Yang retains genuine sincerity despite being cunning and highly capable. This becomes the foundation on which He is willing to consider backing Lü Yang against the Primordial Saint.

## Power distribution in the test

For this duel, the World-Honored One mobilizes the Heavenly Lord's full available might. The chapter quantifies the matchup as three-fifths of the Immortal Pivot's power gathered into the World-Honored One versus two-fifths available to Lü Yang. The World-Honored One also retains a Nascent Soul Dao Lord's experience and Dao Attainment.

He deliberately frames the duel as a test: the gap between the Primordial Saint and Himself is greater than the gap between Himself and Lü Yang, so Lü Yang must demonstrate the ability to overturn this disadvantaged matchup before cooperation is meaningful.

## The Heavenly Palace

Lü Yang manifests The Heavenly Palace, a ninety-nine-layer structure supporting heaven and earth, linked by white-jade profound stairways and filled with countless gods. Its scale almost turns illusion into reality and resembles the manifestation of an enormous Boundary Heaven.

The duel rises beyond the False History's normal heavens. Nearly all observers lose direct access to the battlefield; the Grand Sword Patriarch alone remains able to observe.

## The World-Honored One's Buddha body

The World-Honored One manifests a colossal golden Buddha body. Lü Yang identifies it as a Supreme Treasure rather than a natural dharma body. The World-Honored One explicitly says He does not cultivate the Dharma Body Dao and refined this Buddha body to compensate for that deficiency, having once sought Si Sui's guidance during its refinement.

The Buddha body displays brute force comparable to a Treading the Heavens dharma body, physically tearing Lü Yang's Heavenly Palace in half.

## Willow Wood and The Wheel of Life and Death

The World-Honored One employs Willow Wood through The Wheel of Life and Death. Willow Wood governs flourishing and withering, rise and fall, containing both Wood's tomb and vitality and moving with the cycle of the years.

In Chapter 1255, the World-Honored One receives the Time of Flourishing while Lü Yang receives the Time of Withering. This disrupts Celestial River Immortal. Lü Yang stabilizes the Water Element system by drawing on Never-Drying River, a Long Flowing Water Mystery whose endless source reinforces Heavenly River Water and other Water Element Fruition Attainments.
