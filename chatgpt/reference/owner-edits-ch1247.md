## Final terminology and address

- `小友` = `little friend`, not `young friend`.
- This applies to Si Sui, the World-Honored One, and other senior figures addressing a younger cultivator unless a distinct glossary entry or explicit context requires another form.

## Internal-thought tense

- Direct internal thoughts generally use the present tense because they are immediate mental speech, even when the surrounding narration is in the past tense.
- Use the future naturally for plans and predictions.
- Past tense and past perfect remain appropriate when the character is genuinely recalling a completed event, distinguishing an earlier state from the present, reporting remembered facts, or expressing a counterfactual or anterior sequence.
- Do not shift an immediate deduction into past tense merely to match the narration.
