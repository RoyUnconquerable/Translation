# Owner Style Rulings: Chapter 1260

The owner's edited Chapter 1260 prose is governing. Apply these choices to later chapters where the same terms, images, or narrative situations recur.

## Natural prose and register

- Prefer clean, direct phrasing over added imagery or intensity. Governing examples include *each understood what the other was thinking*, *make His stance clear*, *put your worries to rest*, *more than worth it*, and *His icy gaze brimmed with killing intent*.
- In this context, Si Sui's corrective sentence is *spend a hundred thousand years studying under him*, and the habits acquired in the Sea of Light are simply *bad habits*.
- Preserve the World-Honored One's irreverent sales-pitch voice. He *hawked* His scripture, promised immediate Dao Lord attainment, and claimed it was *guaranteed honest, deceiving neither the young nor the old*.
- `秘辛` may be the direct *secret* when no historical modifier is needed.
- `奠定胜局` is *decide the outcome* in Sublime Bliss's question.

## Metaphors and allusions

- Keep the spit drying on the World-Honored One's face in `唾面自干`.
- Keep the World-Honored One *boarding a new ship* when He changes sides.
- Keep the tail that may grow too large to control in `尾大不掉`.
- Keep the donkey in `黔驴技穷`: *a donkey at the end of its tricks*.
- Keep the scales balanced on a knife's edge and Sublime Bliss's own weight tipping them decisively to one side.
- Keep the two-handed image in handing the Immortal Pivot over with both hands.

## Internal thought and free indirect narration

- Do not italicize every focalized deduction merely because it reports a character's reasoning. The Chapter 1260 plan beginning with *If he acted now* remains roman free indirect narration because the source presents it in third person without explicit thought marks.
- Unmistakable direct thoughts still become first-person italics, including *If I'm not careful*, *It's time I dealt with this nuisance*, and the final *This might be my only chance in this life!*
- Let the source's person and tense determine the distinction. Do not mechanically convert third-person free indirect narration into first-person internal monologue.

## Locked capitalization and address

- `大乘正觉根本经` is *Mahayana True Enlightenment Scripture*.
- `本尊` is *This Venerable* in the World-Honored One's self-reference.
- `时光` is *Time*, `时光长河` is *River of Time*, and `因果大网` is *Great Net of Karma*.
- Use the definite articles in *The River of Time... the Great Net of Karma?* when the World-Honored One names the two constructs in full.
- `太一兽` is *Taiyi Beast*.
- The affiliation is *the Great True Monarchs of the Three Foundations*.
