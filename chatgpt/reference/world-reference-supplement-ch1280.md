# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1280

## Heaven-Slayer meets the River of Fate

The giant formed from the Sea of Light condenses into Lü Yang's own shape. Although he appears alone, his body contains the Sea of Light's Boundary Heavens, living beings, and gods.

Heaven-Slayer owes its power to both versions of Sanhe and to the Sword Sovereign. In the True History, She refined the Grand Sword Patriarch into Her personal sword, which became the Sword Pavilion's mountain gate. In the False History, Sanhe sacrificed his Primordial Spirit, seized that sword, and made it his own.

The sword divides its gathered power into two aspects. One contains the Sea of Light's physical foundation and full weight within the eight poles of the world. The other contains its living beings, their thoughts, history, and karma. Together they give Heaven-Slayer an unparalleled edge.

## The battle across time

The Sword Sovereign directs past, present, and future toward Lü Yang's death. Her altered events kill Lü Yang before the False History, erase Skyward within it, destroy Pan Huang and every Three Foundations cultivator during Lü Yang's war against the Heavens, and kill Lü Yang during his first exploration of the Heavenly Dao.

Lü Yang reverses each altered fate. His sword light meets Her first historical strike, a huge hand saves Skyward, and the counter-events preserve Pan Huang and Lü Yang. The competing histories then recede to reveal Heaven-Slayer's point resting against the Sword Sovereign's finger.

The Sword Sovereign has seen that Lü Yang's direct strength comes from making the entire Sea of Light his own power. This makes him a perfect counter to Du Xuan in a direct clash. She instead uses Fate to break apart Lü Yang's timeline and sever his connection to the Sea of Light.

## Si Sui's appraisal

At the source of the False History, the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord asks whether Lü Yang can defeat the Sword Sovereign. Si Sui explains that this is not Lü Yang's power alone. The power of the Sea of Light and all its living beings has gathered within him, making him capable of fighting Nian Yao.

Si Sui admits that Lü Yang has done better than He did. Si Sui possesses greater individual strength, Dao Attainment, and comprehension, but He ultimately stands alone, while Lü Yang carries the collective power of the Sea of Light.
