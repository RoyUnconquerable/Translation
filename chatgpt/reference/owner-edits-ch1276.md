## Style lesson

Prefer precise cultivation and cosmological language over generic abstraction, complete recognizable Chinese allusions, remove vague actor labels, and use direct physical verbs for manifestation and merging. Strengthen rhythm with small connective beats such as *And behind him* and *suddenly*, but simplify observation when an intensified phrase such as *stared fixedly* adds little. Owner edits remain governing stylistic evidence, while obvious grammar, spacing, and established-pronoun slips are normalized rather than propagated.
