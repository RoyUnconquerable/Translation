# Owner Style Rulings: Chapter 1261

The owner's edited Chapter 1261 prose is governing. Apply these choices to later chapters where the same narrative or formatting situations recur.

## Scene structure

- Insert a hard scene break after the False History undergoes its greatest karmic shift and before the outside observers announce that the main channel has changed. The viewpoint and narrative arena have shifted from Lü Yang inside the False History to the Dao Lords monitoring it from outside.
- Do not rely only on explicit location headings to detect scene breaks. A decisive viewpoint shift between separate narrative arenas can require `---` even when the raw moves directly into dialogue.

## Internal thought

- Italicize the World-Honored One's terse private challenge as *Then come.* Although it lacks explicit thought quotation marks in the raw, its brevity, placement, and immediate continuation into first-person thought make it direct internal speech.
- Continue italicizing unmistakable implicit thoughts such as *You teamed up and staged this whole act to trap me?* Do not leave a direct private reaction in roman type merely because the Chinese omits thought marks.
- Keep direct thought compact. The owner prefers *Then come.* over an expanded narratorial line such as *Then let Him come.*

## Panels and call-outs

- Preserve the Chapter 1261 imperial edict as a bold bracketed panel: `**【Edict: Taiyi Beast Sublime Bliss, present yourself before the Imperial Court!】**`. This is an exception to the general plain call-out rule because the brackets mark a formal God-Sealing edict rather than an ordinary named technique.
- The identity and pill revelations take definite articles as standalone call-outs: *The First-Generation Alchemy Cauldron Peak Lord.* and *The Nascent Soul Pill!*

## Natural prose and imagery

- Prefer *more than ten True Monarchs and Great True Monarchs* over the stiffer *numbering in the double digits*.
- Preserve the dream progression directly: sink deeper into the dream, cast everything aside, awaken, then reveal that the supposed awakening is actually death.
- In the Yellow Millet contrast, the approved phrase is *the true world beyond it*. This does not globally replace the established *real world* in other contexts.
- Preserve the linked river imagery in *follow the flow of time downstream*, the surging river of the False History, its main channel, and its fixed course.
- Preserve the palm chain through the edict, *take a tour inside my palm*, *the size of a palm*, *lay in another's palm*, and the Primordial Saint knowing the Sea of Light as clearly as the palm of His own hand.
- The coordinated phrase *led by the Nether Whisper Ancestor and the Demon-Purging True Person* is accepted in this sentence. It does not overturn the standing bare-title treatment of *Demon-Purging True Person* in ordinary narration.

## Tense and contractions

- In direct thought, use natural contractions such as *There's no chance he'll be able to keep me here.* The ban applies only to contractions ending in apostrophe-d.
- The completed historical restart is announced in simple past: *Divine Transformation and Ascension began once more!*
