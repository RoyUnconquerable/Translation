# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1280 Owner Final

The Sea of Light's giant condenses into Lü Yang's form. He appears alone, but the Sea of Light's Boundary Heavens, trillions of living beings, and countless gods have forged the body he now wields.

Lü Yang draws Heaven-Slayer against the Sword Sovereign's River of Fate. The sword combines the Sword Sovereign's years of nurturing, the Grand Sword Patriarch refined into Her personal sword in the True History, and Sanhe's Primordial Spirit sacrifice and seizure of that sword in the False History.

Their clash spreads through past, present, and future. The Sword Sovereign rewrites key moments so Lü Yang, Skyward, Pan Huang, and the Three Foundations cultivators die before they can create the present outcome. Lü Yang reverses every altered fate and restores those events, revealing the actual clash between Heaven-Slayer's point and Her finger.

The Sword Sovereign avoids a direct contest with the full mass of the Sea of Light. She uses Fate to break apart Lü Yang's timeline and sever the source of his power instead.

At the source of the False History, the Cultivating Truth Dao Lord is stunned that Lü Yang can contend with Nian Yao so soon after refining a Primordial Spirit. Si Sui explains that Lü Yang carries the combined power of the Sea of Light and all its living beings. He admits that Lü Yang has done better than He did because Si Sui, despite His greater personal strength and comprehension, ultimately remains one man standing alone.
