# World Reference Supplement, Chapters 1203 to 1244

## Scope and authority

The verified owner world reference covers the edited manuscript through Chapter
1202. Use it as the factual and terminology baseline for earlier material, then
apply this supplement and the chapter continuity files for later revelations.
The canonical glossary controls English renderings. Later source revelations may
clarify or contradict an earlier entry, but they must never silently erase the
older attested statement.

## Update policy

When extending the world reference:

1. add or extend entries only with facts directly stated in the new source and
   cite the chapter number;
2. when a later fact contradicts or clarifies an earlier entry, retain the
   earlier statement and add an Errata or Clarification note rather than
   silently overwriting it;
3. when the glossary and manuscript wording differ, use the glossary for the
   entry name, record the manuscript variant, and log unresolved conflicts;
4. never merge identities because they resemble one another; merge only when
   the text establishes the identity;
5. recheck first attestation whenever an alias changes.

## Post-1202 clarifications and additions

### Traversing the Mortal Dust

The verified pre-1203 reference records Traversing the Mortal Dust as Alchemy
Cauldron's ancient Primordial Spirit method based on the information available
through Chapter 1202. Chapter 1231 later has Si Sui call it his own method and
explain the principles and intent behind its creation. Treat the older
attribution as incomplete rather than deleting it: Alchemy Cauldron is associated
with the inherited method, while Si Sui is revealed as its originator. Add an
Errata or Clarification note to the main entry when the full world reference is
next regenerated.

### The Cultivating Truth Dao Lord

Chapters 1225 to 1227 reveal that the Nameless Dao Lord arose from the
World-Honored One's evil consciousness and the Spiritual Power Dao Lord Du
Xuan's good consciousness, forming a new True Self. He chooses the name
Cultivating Truth Dao Lord. Dao Lord pronoun capitalization applies.

### Si Sui and the Primordial Spirit

Chapters 1230 to 1232 establish Si Sui as a Saint of the Sea of Light, never a
Sage. His Traversing the Mortal Dust refines the True Self through good and evil
consciousnesses. Good and evil are subjective: Si Sui's good consciousness
wanted the Dharma Body Dao to reign supreme and destroy the other ancient paths,
while his evil consciousness wanted to spread the Dao throughout the Sea of
Light. He chose the latter and says only his True Self remains.

### The Heavenly Book

Chapters 1233 to 1235 reveal that the Heavenly Book remains in Si Sui's
possession inside the seal. The Primordial Saint imposed a restriction under
which it reveals no Mystery to anyone who recognizes it or intends to use it.
Its underlying function is to create a separate causal history and, with enough
power, transform illusion into a real world. Its exact relationship to the Book
of a Hundred Lives remains unresolved.

### Lü Yang's divided consciousness

Chapter 1239 establishes that Lü Yang severs his main soul from the consciousness
outside the Sea of Light under Traversing the Mortal Dust. The separated
consciousness remains in the Heavenly Palace and calls itself the Heavenly
Emperor, while Lü Yang's true soul enters the False History. Both retain access
to the Heavenly Palace and must eventually contend to determine which becomes
primary. `Heavenly Emperor` remains provisional until owner approval.

### Fire and Water in the March on Heaven

Chapters 1241 to 1243 establish that the six Dragon Monarchs transfer the Water
Element away from the Heavenly Lord and into Lü Yang's system. Jiangxi's
conversion to the God-Sealing Method lets Lü Yang seize the Fire Element as
well. Mountaintop Fire, Overturned Lamp Fire, Thunderbolt Fire, Molten Fire, and
Furnace Fire elevate Heavenly Fire, while six Fire and six Water Fruition
Attainments form twelve stars that shatter the Heavenly Lord's thunderclouds.

### Jiangxi under the God-Sealing Method

Chapter 1244 jumps forward three years. The Heavenly Lord can no longer directly
enter Jiangxi, but blockades it, cuts off spiritual qi and rainfall, damages its
earth veins, and generates repeated natural disasters. After bringing order to
the Overseas, Lü Yang enters Jiangxi with a visible sea of clouds carrying
spiritual qi and rain. The twelve Fire and Water Fruition Attainment stars and
the converted God-Sealing cultivators regulate the region's spiritual workings,
causing cultivation, artifact refinement, plants, birds, and beasts to flourish.

Lü Yang states that mortals are useful material within his Dao Lineage and lays
out a social order providing land, homes, education, work, care in old age, an
eight-hour workday, one rest day after every four workdays, and one hundred days
off each year. The joke turns dark when he requires their entire lives, including
future reincarnations, to support the God-Sealing Method.

## Cultural rendering note

Later chapter records must preserve classical and religious imagery rather than
reduce it to plot summary. Chapter 1242's `黔驴技穷` retains the donkey at the
end of its tricks. Chapter 1243's `绝地天通` retains the severing of passage or
communication between Heaven and Earth, and `梦幻泡影` retains the complete
dream, illusion, bubble, and shadow image chain. Chapter 1244's title
`吕阳的人尽其材` deliberately alters `人尽其才` by replacing talent with
material; the translation must preserve both putting everyone to use and the
exploitative treatment of people as resources. The chapter also retains the
horse-at-the-cliff image in `悬崖勒马`.
