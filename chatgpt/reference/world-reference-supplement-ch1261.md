# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1261

## Yellow Millet and Taiyi

Sublime Bliss's Taiyi body is an innate consciousness entity that takes spells as its body. When he commits all of his power to the Yellow Millet, it manifests as a dream plane centered on a towering illusory pagoda. Every brick and stone of the pagoda consists of countless seething thoughts of consciousness.

The Yellow Millet's imagery regards the mortal world as a dream and cultivation as sinking ever deeper into it. Its promised awakening requires abandoning body, soul, memories, thoughts, spiritual power, and divine abilities to seek the true world beyond the dream. This awakening is death in truth.

## Capture of Sublime Bliss

Great Post Earth's Mystery is Boundless Dao. It can seal every route through heaven and earth, preventing Sublime Bliss from escaping after his single full-powered strike.

The Three Foundations cultivators in Jiangxi who switched to the God-Sealing Method now include more than ten True Monarchs and Great True Monarchs. Led by the Nether Whisper Ancestor and Demon-Purging True Person, they attack Sublime Bliss from behind while Lü Yang attacks from the front and the Heavenly Lord joins the assault.

After the Yellow Millet dream shatters, Sublime Bliss's pagoda body shrinks to the size of a palm. Heavenly Fire and Heaven in a Palm both contain imagery of Destiny. Their combination seals off his paths to survival while a formal edict summons Taiyi Beast Sublime Bliss before the Imperial Court. Lü Yang then confines him within the five immortal mountains, sun, and moon of his palm.

## Completion of the False History plan

With Sublime Bliss subdued, no enemy remains in the Immortal Pivot of the False History. Lü Yang plans to establish complete control through the God-Sealing Method, travel downstream through time from the present to its end, reverse cause and effect, and restore the False History to its proper course.

Past, present, and future are unfixed in the False History. Consequently, a process lasting a hundred thousand years can pass in no more than an instant. Lü Yang's capture of Sublime Bliss triggers the greatest karmic shift in the False History's existence, changing its main channel and fixing its course even though the future remains hazy to outside observers.

## Divine Transformation and Ascension restarts

In the Underworld, True Monarch Azure Firmament has donned a new identity as the First-Generation Alchemy Cauldron Peak Lord. The Nascent Soul Pill is ready, and the three allied Dao Lords have secretly accelerated its refinement.

Once every preparation is complete, the Primordial Saint's tiny figure at the far end of the Other Shore moves. The Nascent Soul Pill's radiance surges from the Underworld into the mortal world, beginning Divine Transformation and Ascension once more.
