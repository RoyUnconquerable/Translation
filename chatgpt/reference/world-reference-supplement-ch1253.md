## Extreme Heaven Cliff and Skyward

Chapter 1253 shows the Grand Sword Patriarch seated at Extreme Heaven Cliff when Skyward manifests across his tea table. Skyward declines to join Lü Yang's battle because Lü Yang has not summoned him and therefore, in Skyward's judgment, the time for him to emerge has not yet come.

The Grand Sword Patriarch considers Jiangdong the strategically best target after Lü Yang's conquest of Jiangxi and the Overseas. Skyward instead infers from Lü Yang's abnormal choice of Jiangbei that the Heavenly Lord must have acted and forced Lü Yang to rise to the challenge. The Grand Sword Patriarch continues suppressing Skyward and claims the recovered Skyward now requires enough attention that he has none to spare for Lü Yang.

## Emperor Cang's Heavenly Lord empowerment

Chapter 1253 confirms that Emperor Cang's single body possesses three avatars, each originally at Immortal Bridge cultivation. The Heavenly Lord's lightning forcibly refines the three into a single form and raises Emperor Cang to Treading the Heavens.

Emperor Cang has sided with the Heavenly Lord. He believes it has promised to make him supreme ruler of the Immortal Pivot if he kills Lü Yang, but he does not trust the promise blindly and therefore demanded benefits in advance.

## The Immortal Kingdom Daoist Law in Jiangbei

The Immortal Kingdom Daoist Law is Emperor Cang's Empty Attainment Great Dao and the foundation of the Jiangdong Dao Court. In Chapter 1253 it crosses from Jiangdong into Jiangbei. Dense seal script defines and invokes the imagery of the sun and moon, mountains and rivers, stars, living beings, and the myriad phenomena of heaven and earth.

Its destructive manifestation turns the surrounding world dark enough that observing True Monarchs retreat and even Great True Monarchs must be cautious. Lü Yang answers by kindling a faint spark into a prairie fire that tears open the darkness, after which the twelve Fruition Attainment colors manifest.

Under a second empowerment from the Heavenly Lord, Emperor Cang's status surpasses Lian Tiandou's prior displayed level. The Immortal Kingdom Daoist Law anchors manifested flowers, birds, fish, insects, dragons, serpents, celestial bodies, mountains, rivers, lakes, and seas in an attempt to remake heaven and earth and bar Lü Yang's Fruition Attainment radiance.

## Beheading pending review

The Fruition Attainment radiance pierces the clouds, erases the Immortal Kingdom Daoist Law's writing and Mysteries, and reveals a formal decree sentencing Cang, True Monarch of Jiangdong, to `beheading, pending review` (`斩监候`). Lü Yang, with Heavenly Fire overhead, condemns Emperor Cang as a usurping false emperor who dares to establish statutes and enforce the law.

`斩监候` is a historical legal expression and is locked here as `beheading, pending review`; the Chapter 1253 title is `Beheading Pending Review` unless later owner wording supersedes it.
