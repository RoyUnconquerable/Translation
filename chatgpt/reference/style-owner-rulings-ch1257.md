# Owner Style Rulings: Chapter 1257

The owner's edited Chapter 1257 prose is governing. Apply the following reusable rulings in later chapters.

## Immediate internal-thought tense

Do not mechanically backshift an entire direct thought merely because the surrounding narration is past tense. Let each proposition keep its own time relationship:

- A completed exchange may take past tense: *It was only a duel between opponents at the same realm.*
- An ability that remains unused at the present thought-moment may take present perfect: *The World-Honored One hasn't even drawn on his Primordial Spirit yet.*
- A standing evaluation or general truth stays present or modal present: *A victory like this carries no real weight in the Immortal Pivot.* / *A hair's advantage in cultivation can place one a whole heaven higher.*
- Use natural contractions such as *I'm*, *it's*, *hasn't*, and *couldn't*. Continue avoiding contractions ending in apostrophe-d.

Current-state deductions in dialogue also resist automatic backshift. In *I suspect it's no longer the original Heavenly Lord*, the suspected identity remains a present condition, while the earlier descent can be expressed in the past perfect.

## Precision and register

- Prefer *a duel between opponents at the same realm* and *invincible among those at the same realm* when the point is exact realm parity.
- Render 真实世界 as *real world* in the False History contrast.
- Render 苦海 as *Sea of Suffering*.
- Render 六四开 as *six to four*, not *sixty-forty*.
- *Farther removed they were* is the approved state description in the firmament passage.
- Preserve compact explanatory apposition when useful: *false imitations shaped by Karma, things that appear real but are not.*
- In this context, 含金量 becomes *carries no real weight*, and 斗法第一 becomes *unrivaled in combat*.
- Preserve idiomatic and allusive color such as *What great winds and waves have I not weathered?*
- Preserve pointed comic diction such as *descending from above to crush those beneath Him* when the source makes the joke explicit.

## Structure

Preserve exactly one English paragraph for each Chinese source paragraph, in the same order. Do not merge or split source paragraphs for English flow.

Use a standalone `---` for a hard scene transition when warranted by the source and project convention, as before *At the far end of the firmament.* The scene-break line does not authorize any paragraph merge or split.

## Mechanical normalization

Obvious transcription slips in owner-provided prose may be corrected without turning them into style rules. For Chapter 1257, normalize *think I', an idiot* to *think I'm an idiot* and restore the missing space in *yet. A victory*. A single local lowercase divine pronoun does not override the established Dao Lord pronoun policy without repeated owner evidence.
