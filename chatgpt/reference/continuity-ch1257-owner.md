# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1257 Owner Final

The battle above the firmament ends without either lofty aura weakening. At Extreme Heaven Cliff, Skyward remains unable to see the exchange, while the Grand Sword Patriarch discerns the outcome and detects a true Primordial Spirit aura from the figure occupying the Heavenly Lord's identity.

The Grand Sword Patriarch reasons that the original Heavenly Lord could not possess a Primordial Spirit because it never cultivated a Dao Heart. He therefore concludes that a Dao Lord from the real world has descended into the False History. Skyward identifies the World-Honored One immediately from His habitual style of descending into lower realms.

The Grand Sword Patriarch judges that Lü Yang held the upper hand. He distinguishes defeat from killing and estimates Lü Yang's odds in a prolonged battle at six to four. Before he can observe further, someone blocks his sight.

At the far end of the firmament, Lü Yang studies the False History's deficiencies. Beyond its Immortal Pivot there is no Sea of Light, and its firmament cannot be escaped. It also lacks the Foundation Establishment Realm, Sea of Suffering, dark side of the Sea of Light, and Underworld, possessing only karmic imitations. Lü Yang considers whether filling those gaps could turn the False History into reality.

The World-Honored One praises Lü Yang's methods and asks how he proved Fate-Reforming, which lies closer to Variables than Karma. Lü Yang offers Him the Mystery and a cultivation jade slip. The World-Honored One refuses and reminds Lü Yang that winning a single same-realm exchange does not settle a true fight.

Lü Yang is not complacent. He views same-realm invincibility as carrying little weight when higher status and cultivation can decide a battle. His thought distinguishes the completed duel from the World-Honored One's still-unused Primordial Spirit and states the broader evaluation in the present tense.

At Lü Yang's request, the World-Honored One compares the six Dao Lords' combat strength. The Spiritual Power Dao Lord is weaker than Him; the Spell Dao Lord is roughly comparable apart from status; Vast Sky is somewhat stronger; the Sword Sovereign is second only to the Primordial Saint and grows stronger under the Heavenly Dao; and the Primordial Saint remains the supreme combat benchmark. Lü Yang concludes that his current same-realm prospects are respectable.
