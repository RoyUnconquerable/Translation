# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1269 Owner Final

As the False History returns, one hundred thousand years compress into a single instant. Lü Yang and the Heavenly Emperor reach the final struggle of Traversing the Mortal Dust on opposite sides of a pitch-black abyssal surface.

The good and evil consciousnesses synchronize all cultivation, methods, and Dao Attainment, keeping their strength in absolute equilibrium. Lü Yang breaks this equilibrium because the Book of a Hundred Lives and its talents are bound only to him as the true body.

Lü Yang activates Supreme Dao Transformation. Its wisdom light lets him use methods the Heavenly Emperor cannot understand or mirror, and he begins refining the Heavenly Emperor away bit by bit.

The Heavenly Emperor argues that temporary force cannot create a stable Primordial Spirit. True unity requires one consciousness's ideals to overwhelm the other's because their cultivation strength will always synchronize. Lü Yang dismisses these concerns on the grounds that he can repair his foundation after breaking through.

Divine Offerings gathers the thoughts of the countless gods under Lü Yang's Dao Lineage. The collective thoughts become murky chains and suppress the Heavenly Emperor. He cannot reproduce the operation because he lacks Supreme Dao Transformation.

A golden Primordial Spirit flame appears in Lü Yang's chest, illuminates the darkness, and prevents him from losing himself. Lü Yang becomes the dominant consciousness, while the Heavenly Emperor's manifestation within the Heavenly Palace shatters like an illusory bubble.

Lü Yang recognizes that complete internal unity will still require time and slow, patient tempering, invoking the line "Long have I contended with myself, and still I would rather be myself."

The False History breaks through the Primordial Saint's blockade and converges with the real world. Lü Yang returns through the Fathomless Void, and his Status surges beyond the limits of Treading the Heavens.
