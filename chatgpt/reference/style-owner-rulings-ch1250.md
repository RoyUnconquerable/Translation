# Owner Style Rulings, Chapter 1250 Onward

Read this after the core style guide and all earlier owner-style supplements. The owner's final prose overrides a provisional model draft.

## Natural handling of idioms and allusions

Preserve Chinese texture, but do not confuse preservation with word-for-word calque. The target is the allusion's force, image, joke, or structural role in natural published English.

- Keep a source image when it remains vivid and natural, such as the soft persimmon in `捏软柿子` and the shared qi with intertwined branches in `同气连枝`.
- Use a natural established English idiom when it accurately carries the source function and the owner has selected it. In Chapter 1250, `大隐隐于市` is `hide in plain sight`, not the clumsy literal draft about the greatest hermit in the marketplace.
- Preserve repetition without reproducing visual character play mechanically. In Chapter 1250, `又双叒叕` is `time and time again`.
- Do not force every component of a compact idiom into the sentence when the result becomes labored. `手到擒来` may become `almost effortless` when the surrounding trap imagery already preserves the action and outcome.
- `雾里看花` is `viewing flowers through the fog` in the established Chapter 1250 wording.

## Concision without lost meaning

- Prefer the shortest natural construction that preserves every source detail.
- Use `second only to X`, not `ranked first after X`.
- Use `merits consideration`, `falls far short`, `friend or foe`, and similarly compact contrasts when they match the source.
- Remove explanatory duplication introduced by the draft, but do not remove source repetition, escalation, contrast, or causal links.
- Avoid turning one compact Chinese judgment into two English sentences unless clarity genuinely requires it.

## Thought and narrative voice

- Direct thought remains italicized and generally present tense.
- Recollections about prior lives, completed breakthroughs, and earlier conditions may use past or past perfect.
- Lü Yang's deductions should move quickly from clue to conclusion. Do not pad each inferential step with redundant explanation.
- Formal speakers remain polished and complete, while Lü Yang's thoughts may remain brisk, sardonic, or blunt.
