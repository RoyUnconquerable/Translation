# Owner Style Rulings: Chapter 1265

The owner's edited Chapter 1265 prose is governing. Apply these choices to later chapters where the same terms, imagery, chronology, or narrative situations recur.

## Modern descriptive flow

- Prefer concrete, flowing description over a ceremonial rendering. Clouds *churned, white as snow*, white jade steps *rose one after another*, and they converged before the *Palace of Soaring Clouds*.
- For an imposing arrival, the approved progression is that the figure is *wreathed in boundless radiance*, has magnificent robes, and takes steps *heavy with power*. Keep the physical details moving rather than arranging them as a static inventory.
- True Monarch Flying Snow has *nothing remaining of her but her soul*. The man she remembers had *spoken with her so warmly*. These direct formulations are preferred over heavier descriptions of state or relationship.
- Use *distinct layers like ranks of court ministers* for the ordered interior of the palace. In this context, Flying Snow takes her place within *the Divine Rank corresponding to Divine Offerings*.

## Dao Lord action and cosmic scale

- The World-Honored One *had been watching coldly all along* before He *finally made His move*. He *brought His palms together*, with a fierce smile gleaming in His eyes.
- His golden body is *as radiant as the noonday sun*, then bursts into flames and *slowly burns away to nothing*. Preserve the sun-at-its-height image while keeping the sentence modern and direct.
- The Primordial Saint's use of Destiny ignores *the process, the methods involved, and every factor* that could affect the outcome. The Ancestral Dragon's body disintegrates amid *a furious dragon roar*.
- Fate, Fortune, Destiny, Spells, and Spiritual Power all have Dao Lords *presiding over them*. Use this natural authority wording when `在位` describes Their occupancy of the corresponding Daos.

## Rewritten-history tense

- When the World-Honored One changes the past by abandoning His Dao attainment, use past perfect for the newly established historical facts: He *had willingly given up proving the Dao from five thousand years ago*, *had never taken part* in the battle 129,600 years ago, and Si Sui *had never died*.
- The World-Honored One frees Himself *from it*, meaning His earlier act of proving the Dao, and thereby ceases to be a Dao Lord. Preserve that causal link.
- The formal paired beat is *Altering cause, changing effect!* The next line remains the direct consequence: *The course of history changed once more.*

## Structure and mechanical normalization

- Preserve the hard scene break between the Heavenly Palace and the Primordial Saint's descent, and the second hard scene break before *Almost simultaneously, within the False History.*
- The doubled period after *Palace of Soaring Clouds* in the supplied edit is an obvious paste slip and is normalized to a single period. The raw paragraph mapping remains unchanged.
