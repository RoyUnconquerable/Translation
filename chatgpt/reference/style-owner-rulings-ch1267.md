# Owner Style Rulings: Chapter 1267

The owner's edited Chapter 1267 prose is governing. Apply its terminology and its broader control of sentence structure, causal progression, cosmic action, metaphor, and reveal timing to later chapters.

## Character logic and philosophical register

- The Primordial Saint *regarded all things as false and Him alone as true*. His pursuit of the Great Dao is the last fragment of His *true nature*. If He abandons it, nothing of His *True Self* remains. Preserve the contextual distinction between `本真` and `真我`.
- `唾手可得` is *close enough to grasp* in the flowers-and-moon thought. Keep the physical immediacy before preserving both images in `镜花水月`: *flowers in a mirror and the moon reflected in water*.
- The emphatic denial is *Impossible, absolutely impossible!* with a comma. Preserve its spoken rhythm without over-punctuating the first half.
- Prefer *He had placed genuine hopes upon it* and *until failure became absolute* for the Primordial Saint's sincere ambition. Do not reduce Divine Transformation and Ascension to a stratagem when the source identifies it as His true dream.

## Cosmic action and causal clarity

- In pressure imagery, let cause and duration land cleanly: Variables *gathered in a frenzy*, crashed against the obstruction *like a flood against a dam*, and the barrier *held firm for the moment* but couldn't endure.
- Prefer compact loss-of-status prose: the World-Honored One stands *tall and proud*, His palms pressed together; the grandeur of a Dao Lord *was gone*, and His cultivation and status had fallen to peak Golden Core.
- Use precise control verbs according to context. The Heavenly Dao *took control of* the False History and could determine its changes, while the Sword Sovereign's claim that it *commanded everything* carries the grander declarative beat.
- For converging power, make the destination explicit: mighty power and Mystery gather at the source, *converging upon the Heavenly Dao itself*.
- Keep the reset sequence in distinct beats: the history collapses, rolls back 129,600 years, *Then it began anew!*, and only afterward resumes from its source.

## Natural metaphor and system panels

- In the control metaphor, the approved natural English is that the False History was *nothing more than a toy for the Sword Sovereign to play with however she pleased*. Do not retain an awkward gendered object metaphor merely because it is literal.
- `彩色天赋` is *Prismatic Talent*, not *Colored Talent*.
- The approved panel wording is: *This talent may be freely activated or deactivated. While active, your history will be unique and cannot be affected by any external force.*
- Preserve bold bracketed system-panel formatting and the hard scene break before *Meanwhile, within the Immortal Pivot of the False History.*

## Broader editing preference

- Treat owner edits as models for prose decisions. Favor sentences whose agents, actions, and destinations are immediately clear; remove needless repetition; keep explanatory logic compact; and allow major reversals and talent reveals to arrive through short, isolated beats.
