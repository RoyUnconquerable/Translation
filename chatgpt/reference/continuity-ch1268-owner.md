# Continuity Supplement: Chapter 1268 Owner Final

Lü Yang's Prismatic Talent A Guest Rejected by Heaven and Earth, obtained from settling Skyward's experiences, anchors his entire historical course after the Sword Sovereign restarts the False History. Lü Yang suspects the Book of a Hundred Lives deliberately produced this highly targeted talent because it is seeking completeness.

The World-Honored One had gathered Karma to counter the Sword Sovereign's hidden method, but Lü Yang overcomes the crisis without His intervention. The World-Honored One preserves that power for a more decisive use.

Monks and believers cultivating the World-Honored One's Buddhist Dharma chant across the False and True Histories. Their incense faith and collective voices allow Karma to expand and call toward Variables.

The World-Honored One's departure from the Other Shore, the halted Divine Transformation and Ascension, the returning False History, and Karma's call cause the gathered imagery of Variables to undergo a qualitative change. The Great Dao of Variables manifests a hazy outline.

Variables compresses the full 129,600-year False History into a singularity where past, present, and future churn together and cause and effect exist all at once. Lü Yang uses this opportunity to skip the process and reach the result 129,600 years later.

More than one hundred thousand years after Chongguang formed his Golden Core through the Five Elements, the Immortal Pivot has become a peaceful realm governed by gods under the God-Sealing Method. Thirty Fruition Attainment stars replace the extinguished sun and moon. Common people offer incense and raise the gods through worship, while prosperous people make the gods stronger.

Chongguang prefers the False History to the True History of the real world. True Monarch Sovereign of Fate remains honored like the sun at the summit of the Immortal Pivot, and His conquests and establishment of ninety-nine provinces define the historical record.

Chongguang and countless cultivators in the real world remember this sealed history simultaneously. Altered cause and effect return to their original form, shattering the Primordial Saint's Destiny radiance and leaving nothing to obstruct the False History's return.
