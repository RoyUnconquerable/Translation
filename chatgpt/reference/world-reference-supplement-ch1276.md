# World Reference Supplement: Chapter 1276

## The Dao Lords respond to the Sea of Light

On the falling Other Shore, the Sword Sovereign, Vast Sky, the Spell Dao Lord, and Du Xuan recognize that the Underworld survived, Si Sui is about to escape, and Lü Yang's completed God-Sealing Dao Lineage has severed Their connection to the Sea of Light.

The return of the False History will revive the Dharma Body Dao and reunite the Three Foundations. This will greatly strengthen both the Spell Dao Lord and Du Xuan, but the Spell Dao Lord fears that the growing number of competitors will force one of the existing Dao Lords from His position.

The four Dao Lords agree to dismantle the first and second layers of the Other Shore, intensifying its fall so that Lü Yang and the Sea of Light will be crushed or Dao Tianqi will be forced to intervene again.

The Primordial Saint ignores Their proposal and watches Si Sui take shape where the torrents of karma belonging to the False History and True History converge. He wonders whether Si Sui can reclaim 129,600 years, turn from Postnatal back to Innate through cultivation alone, and become a Dao Lord as the Ancestral Dragon did.

## Lü Yang raises the Sea of Light

Lü Yang charges into the fire of the falling heavens and invokes the principle that times shift, all things change, and the old must be cast off to usher in the new.

Divine Offerings gathers countless figures into a collective projection. True Monarch Flying Snow appears and affirms that accepting collective aid still counts as relying on oneself because human strength has limits and one must sometimes bend and adapt.

Drop of Heaven's Essence manifests the Eight Trigrams and the transformations of the myriad phenomena. Demon-Purging True Person joins the projection without speaking.

The Sea of Dust and Tribulation manifests with Skyward, who complains that Lü Yang left him imprisoned for 120,000 years. Lü Yang answers that the capable must do more and promises that Skyward's Dao Path will open completely after the crisis.

The Nether Whisper Ancestor enters the projection as Heaven in a Palm gathers the Mysteries together. Fate-Reforming then draws the Heavenly Palace fully into the Sea of Light, where both the palace and Lü Yang's true body merge into the projection.

The projection expands until it nearly fills the Sea of Light, fuses with its boundless radiance, and surges upward against the falling Other Shore. The Dao Lords watch in surprise because the Sea of Light itself has effectively stood up.
