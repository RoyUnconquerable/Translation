# Condensed continuity archive through Chapter 1268

This file is searched only when an earlier event becomes relevant. Git history
retains the former detailed chapter supplements.

## Through Chapter 1210

Sublime Bliss, whose true body is the collective Taiyi, was trapped through
Yellow Millet. Lü Yang entered the False History behind Skyward, freed Rampart
Earth, reconnected the God-Sealing Dao Lineage to the Heavenly Palace, reached
Treading the Heavens under his Mountaintop Fire identity, and changed history.
The Dao Lords' descent pulled the Other Shore toward the mortal world. The
Primordial Saint alone saw Variables entering the False History and allowed the
change to continue. Lü Yang reached the Heavenly Dao while Si Sui struck its
seal from the other side.

## Chapters 1211 to 1220

No durable source, translation, or owner-review evidence for this range is

## Chapters 1221 to 1249

Lü Yang expanded the God-Sealing Method through the Underworld, Dragon
Monarchs, gods, incense, and Fruition Attainments while navigating the competing
designs of the Dao Lords. Later revelations separated provisional appearances
from true identities and clarified the roles of the Heavenly Palace, Heavenly
Dao, and False History. Exact English approval is incomplete for parts of this
range; consult the ledger before treating prose as precedent. `补天道` appeared
in this range, but `Heaven-Mending Dao` remains a provisional rendering.

## Chapters 1250 to 1259

The struggle over the False History, Five Elements, Heavenly Numbers, and Dao
Lord plans accelerated. Lü Yang's God-Sealing system became capable of
reordering the Immortal Pivot and supporting a new social and cultivation
order. Sanhe's buried history, the Sword Sovereign's long manipulation, and the
conditions for Si Sui's return came into focus. Chapters 1252, 1256, and 1259
remain pending owner review.

## Chapters 1260 to 1268

The Primordial Saint restarted Divine Transformation and Ascension through the
Other Shore. The World-Honored One abandoned His Dao Lord position, breaking
the ascent and restoring the False History's path of return. The Heavenly
Emperor rejected the Primordial Saint's Seven Emotions Heaven-Fathoming Art
because he lacked the wisdom light to understand it. The Heavenly Dao tried to
restart and rewrite the False History, but A Guest Rejected by Heaven and Earth
made Lü Yang's history unique. The World-Honored One then called forth the
Great Dao of Variables, compressing 129,600 years into one moment. The
God-Sealing order developed through that entire span, and its altered karma
began returning to the True History.
