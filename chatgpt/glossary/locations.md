# Place and cosmology reference

This is a convenience view. `terminology.tsv` is canonical for exact forms and
notes.

- **Other Shore (彼岸):** usually takes `the`; numbered strata are layers, not
  tiers.
- **False History (伪史)** and **True History (正史):** capitalized and usually
  take `the`.
- **mortal world (现世):** usually `the mortal world`; distinct from lowercase
  `red dust` (红尘).
- **Underworld (冥府), Sea of Light (光海), Three Realms (三界), Vajra Realm
  (金刚界), Heavenly Palace (天宫), Heavenly Dao (天道), Great Dao (大道),** and
  **Immortal Pivot (仙枢):** use the articles recorded in the terminology TSV.
- **Yellow Millet (黄粱):** the dream plane.
- **Old Lord Temple (老君庙):** a bare call-out is `Old Lord Temple.`
- **Achieving Dao Hidden Profound Manor (成道隐玄府):** Skyward's Grotto-Heaven;
  `Grotto-Heaven` is capitalized.
- **Extreme Heaven Cliff (极天崖):** in Jiangnan; the barrier that remained in
  the False History during Chapter 1209.
- **Jiangnan (江南), Jiangdong (江东), Saint Sect (圣宗), Sword Pavilion (剑阁),**
  and **Boundary Heaven (界天):** fixed renderings.

Do not collapse distinct cosmological terms merely because English synonyms
sound smoother.
