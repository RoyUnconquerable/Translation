# Character and identity reference

This is a browsable view of current identity and pronoun rulings. The canonical
term authority remains `terminology.tsv`.

- **Lü Yang (吕阳):** protagonist; never `Lu Yang`. Mu Changsheng is his alias,
  and Mountaintop Fire is his True Monarch identity in the False History.
- **Nameless Dao Lord (无名道主):** singular He/Him; the full name is capitalized
  everywhere under the final Chapter 1210 owner ruling.
- **World-Honored One (世尊):** Dao Lord; He/His/Him.
- **Sword Sovereign (剑主 / 剑尊 / 剑君):** one Dao Lord under three source names;
  He/His/Him.
- **Primordial Saint (初圣):** Dao Lord; He/His/Him.
- **Vast Sky (昊天 / 苍昊):** one Dao Lord under two source names; He/His/Him.
- **Spell Dao Lord (法术道主 / 万法):** distinct from the Spiritual Power Dao
  Lord; addressed as Fellow Daoist Myriad Spells.
- **Spiritual Power Dao Lord (法力道主):** aids the Spell Dao Lord.
- **Beast-Taming (豢妖):** Dao Lord; `Senior Beast-Taming` in that address.
- **Sublime Bliss (极乐 / 妙乐):** the Spell Dao Lord's eldest disciple; true
  body is Taiyi, a collective of bodies of consciousness.
- **Skyward (凌霄):** post-Chapter 1171 identity; earlier identity is Soaring
  Firmament (昂霄).
- **Rampart Earth (城头土):** True Monarch.
- **Flying Snow (飞雪):** True Monarch who speaks formally and completely.
- **Si Sui (司祟), Dao Tianqi (道天齐), Emperor Cang (帝苍), Pan Huang (盘皇),
  and Bu Tianque (补天阙):** use their recorded English names; Dao Tianqi and
  Emperor Cang take ordinary lowercase pronouns.
- **Ancestral Dragon (祖龙):** He/His/Him for the being; `it/its` for the
  guided-out consciousness.

When Dao Lords act collectively in narration, capitalize Their/Them. The
consciousness that slipped into the False History in Chapter 1210 takes
`it/its` under the owner's final ruling.
