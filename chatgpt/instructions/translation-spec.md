# Translation source contract

Draft once from the exact supplied Chinese. Keep title, ending and one English
paragraph per source paragraph in the same order. Do not merge, split, omit,
reorder, summarize or invent content. Compose grammatical modern English within
those boundaries, preserving every detail and the Chinese cultivation setting.

`reference/style-guide.md` is the sole macro prose authority. The terminology,
entity and phrase records govern names, identities, approved wording and idiom
scope. The compact inventory from prepare.py plus relevant historical entries
is the shared input for drafting and the single review in qa-rules.md.

Execution, caching, review deadlines, patching and delivery are specified only
in workflow.md. Do not treat this source contract as an additional drafting or
review pass. Chapter prose stays outside Git and is delivered in chat.
