finds Skyward's hidden Grotto-Heaven, the Achieving Dao Hidden Profound

Lineage through Divine Offerings to the Heavenly Palace, and rockets to

anchors Rampart Earth's karmic light a thousand years into the past. The
Dao Lords outside freeze: the flow of history has been changed.

## Chapter 1210

The Dao Lords' combined descent drags the Other Shore down toward the
mortal world. The Spell Dao Lord, suppressed by the World-Honored One,
Sword Sovereign, and Vast Sky despite the Spiritual Power Dao Lord's help,
first suspects Sublime Bliss of changing the False History's flow, then
publicly redirects suspicion to the consciousness that slipped in under the
Primordial Saint's watch. The Primordial Saint stays silent: He alone sees
Variables streaming into the False History's dim karma net, and He wants
more. Lü Yang, borrowing Rampart Earth's power through Fate-Reforming,
fights upstream through time, refusing to expose A Guest Rejected by Heaven
and Earth early. Si Sui's ceaseless hammering breaches the obstruction, and
Lü Yang reaches the Heavenly Dao: the channel that let the World-Honored
One alter cause and effect 129,600 years ago, with Si Sui's silhouette at
its dark end.
