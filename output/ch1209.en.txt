Chapter 1209: The Flow of History Was Changed!

Heaven and earth stretched on.

A formless shadow drifted above the sea of clouds. It existed there plainly enough, yet no one could sense its presence.

As if the two occupied different planes of existence.

And it was indeed so. To Him, the False History was like a soap bubble, real enough in its existence, but an environment that broke at the slightest touch.

Throughout the False History, aside from the source where Si Sui dwelt, there was only one place He couldn't yet enter: Jiangnan's Extreme Heaven Cliff. It was occupied by a consciousness that had transcended halfway, and its glory was so great that even the False History couldn't conceal it. He had no intention of stirring up trouble there.

*No leads...*

He wandered thus through the sea of clouds, reviewing his recent exchange with Sublime Bliss. He could see that Sublime Bliss had harbored no intention to deceive.

*That's far better than with Skyward.*

*After all, he's an ancient cultivator, easy to read, easy to deceive.*

*In comparison, when exchanging words with that Skyward, He had to keep all His wits sharp each time, avoiding any slip, always wondering if the other was lying.*

*Speaking of which, where is he now?*

The Nameless Dao Lord looked around. Skyward's aura was visible everywhere, yet He couldn't find the main body. This strange situation furrowed His brow.

Yet at that very moment.

BOOM!

As if some sealed lock had suddenly been unlocked by a key, the scattered auras of Skyward throughout heaven and earth abruptly found their master's location.

*Hmm?*

The Nameless Dao Lord raised His head, surprise crossing His eyes. *This... a Grotto-Heaven? A Fruition Attainment? What has he hidden?*

It wasn't only Him. At that moment, the disturbance rippling through the Immortal Pivot drew every Immortal Pivot True Monarch's gaze. Where they looked, a bright light was falling through the sky point by point. In its depths, a floating mist sea was faintly visible, and within it, a grand manor.

Achieving Dao Hidden Profound Manor.

*It's Skyward's Grotto-Heaven.*

At this sight, the Nameless Dao Lord's understanding crystallized. His surprise deepened. *I see. He came early. But how long ago?*

He calculated on his fingers.

*So it was. He came thirty years early.*

*In that case, I'll go back thirty years to find him.*

Thinking this, the Nameless Dao Lord immediately moved to leave the current time point and press deeper into the False History. Yet He stopped abruptly before He could depart.

*...Nothing?*

The Nameless Dao Lord furrowed His brow, for going back thirty years, He found no trace of Skyward. All that met His gaze was blank, empty scenery.

But quickly He figured it out, His gaze turning toward Jiangnan. *There it is. Was it him? Did he sever that part of the record from the False History? It seems he's already understood that his location is the False History. So Skyward and he have a collaboration? The Saint Sect and Sword Pavilion are working together?*

The Nameless Dao Lord's thoughts deepened.

Immediately after, He turned His gaze toward the Achieving Dao Hidden Profound Manor that was gradually emerging from its hidden state, and a darkened look crossed His eyes.

*I'd better go take a look.*

Skyward's actions gave Him pause.

*He's done so much, yet hid only this one Grotto-Heaven. He must have ulterior motives. I need to see for myself, at least know what he's really thinking.*

Jiangdong.

While countless True Monarchs and even the Nameless Dao Lord trained their gaze on the Achieving Dao Hidden Profound Manor, Lü Yang had already slipped in through a back entrance.

*Come to think of it, this is the first time I've entered here.*

*I didn't expect it to be so large.*

Lü Yang raised his head. He quickly saw five Fruition Attainments hanging high above the grand manor: the five Fruition Attainments controlled by Skyward.

Beyond that, there was also a pillar reaching to the heavens within the hall, dividing the yin and yang breaths, fixing the two cosmic poles: this was Crooked and Straight. The Great Dao of Empty Attainment ran through it, harmonizing yin and yang, ordering the auras, which let the five Fruition Attainments merge as one. The space seemed merely expansive, yet contained countless small and great cosmic formations within.

Lü Yang walked through the manor with keen interest.

What met his eyes was a long corridor, decorated simply but elegantly, with doors closed on both sides.

Behind each door was a small Boundary Heaven.

Lü Yang paused at each door, peering through the window into the landscape beyond. Each held a wholly different vista.

*This is Skyward's treasure vault, filled with the collection of a lifetime!*

*This should be his private meditation chamber, with the environment finely adjusted. Sss... somehow I sense Wisdom Light about to manifest?*

*And there's this one...*

*This one...*

Lü Yang didn't skip a single chamber. After all, Skyward wasn't in the Grotto-Heaven now, and Lü Yang had both the obligation and responsibility to protect his property.

Before long, he'd reached the end of the corridor.

There stood another door. Peering through the window, Lü Yang's gaze met with dazzling, brilliant rainbow light.

Rampart Earth!

At the sight, Lü Yang drew a deep breath. Then he placed his hand gently on the door, and a potent sense of closure and seal washed over him.

*The seal is quite tight.*

It was the double application of Great Forest Wood and Crooked and Straight. If he were right, this door that appeared firmly sealed had originally been open.

The Open state had merely been inverted to the Closed state.

Thus this door came to be.

And on top of that, Skyward had added a Cognitive Barrier, preventing anyone from perceiving the truth, and finally formed this seal.

With this special design, any method to break a seal was useless against this door. It had never been closed to begin with, yet the door truly existed, so no one could enter. To undo this seal, one either had a preset key like his own...

...or power strong enough to overwhelm the technique.

As Lü Yang marveled, he activated Fate-Reforming once more. Sensing this Mystery, the formerly sealed door began to fade.

The Mystery receded.

Just as Lü Yang had calculated, the room had never been locked to begin with. The door swung wide, and he entered easily.

In an instant, Rampart Earth blazed with light!

Skyward had no cultivation related to it, so after seizing this Fruition Attainment from the Heavenly Lord, he had simply suppressed it by force.

Now that the door was gone, it naturally sought to escape!

But Lü Yang had anticipated this. Before it could flee, he gestured, drawing the rainbow light into his embrace with casual ease and quickly calmed its turbulence.

Unlike Skyward, he possessed cultivation in Rampart Earth!

Of course, the more critical tool was Fate-Reforming. This Mystery could harmonize all phenomena, transforming others' things into his own, exactly suited to the task.

After calming Rampart Earth, Lü Yang discovered the room contained more than one Fruition Attainment. There was also a glazed sphere placed on the other side.

Lü Yang approached the glazed sphere and peered closely. His eyes brightened at once.

Within the glazed sphere was a world complete with mountains, rivers, and forests. Within it, he sensed millions of God-Sealing Method cultivators!

*This is it!*

Joy crossed Lü Yang's eyes. He knew this was the incense believers Skyward had left for him, enough to let him rapidly restore his cultivation.

With that thought, Lü Yang picked up the glazed sphere.

Time was limited. He didn't converse with these cultivators hidden in the Grotto-Heaven but immediately restored the Dao Lineage connection between them.

In an instant, Lü Yang's aura surged!

The radiance transformed by Divine Offerings illuminated upward, piercing the darkness, and Lü Yang immediately sensed the grand Heavenly Palace beyond the Sea of Light!

The next instant, the Heavenly Palace poured down infinite mighty power, wildly elevating his status. His identity as Mountaintop Fire True Monarch and the karma of his millions of False History believers became the perfect cover, allowing this enormous elevation to create no ripple in the False History.

True Monarch. True Monarch middle stage. Great True Monarch!

Entering the Dao. Merging with the Dao. Immortal Bridge.

Treading the Heavens!

Whew...

After a long while, Lü Yang exhaled, stretched his limbs, and satisfaction crossed his face. Then, with full confidence, he looked at the Rampart Earth in his hand.

The next instant, the rainbow light of Rampart Earth suddenly condensed.

Amplified by Lü Yang's Treading the Heavens status, white-hot karma light was forcibly condensed. Then it directly anchored to the dim history of a thousand years past!

BOOM!

At once, outside the False History, the Dao Lords abruptly halted their clash of mighty powers. They turned their gazes in unison, watching the False History with unprecedented gravity.

*As expected... a Variable has appeared.*

*Who is it?*

*The flow of history has been changed!*
