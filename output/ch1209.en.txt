Chapter 1209: The Course of History Has Been Changed!

Heaven and earth stretched on.

A formless shadow drifted above the sea of clouds. It existed there plainly enough, yet no one could sense its presence.

It was as if the two existed in different dimensions.

And it was indeed so. To Him, the False History was like a bubble, real enough in its existence, but an environment that broke at the slightest touch.

Throughout the False History, aside from the source where Si Sui dwelt, there was only one place He couldn't yet enter: Jiangnan's Extreme Heaven Cliff. It was occupied by a consciousness that had transcended halfway, and its glory was so great that even the False History couldn't conceal it. He had no intention of stirring up trouble there.

*No leads...*

He strolled through the sea of clouds just like that, reviewing his recent exchange with Sublime Bliss. He could see that Sublime Bliss had harbored no intention of deceiving Him.

*In that respect, he's much better than Skyward.*

*After all, he's an ancient cultivator, easy to read, easy to deceive.*

*By comparison, whenever He exchanged words with Skyward, He had to keep His wits about Him at every turn, guarding against the slightest slip and constantly wondering whether the other party was lying.*

*Speaking of which, where is he now?*

The Nameless Dao Lord looked around. Skyward's aura was visible everywhere, yet his main body was nowhere to be found. This eerie situation made Him furrow His brows.

However, at that very moment.

BOOM!

As if some sealed lock had suddenly been unlocked by a key, the scattered auras of Skyward throughout heaven and earth abruptly found their master's location.

*Hmm?*

The Nameless Dao Lord raised His head, surprise crossing His eyes. *This... a Grotto-Heaven? A Fruition Attainment? What has he hidden?*

It wasn't only Him. At that moment, the disturbance resounding through the Immortal Pivot drew the gaze of every True Monarch in the Immortal Pivot. Wherever they looked, a streak of bright light hung upon the firmament, falling bit by bit. In its depths, a sea of drifting mist was faintly visible, and within it, a grand manor.

The Achieving Dao Hidden Profound Manor.

*It's Skyward's Grotto-Heaven.*

At this sight, everything became clear to the Nameless Dao Lord at once, and the surprise in His eyes deepened. *So that's how it is. He arrived ahead of time. But just how much earlier?*

He calculated on his fingers.

*I see now. He came thirty years early.*

*In that case, I'll go back thirty years to find him.*

With that thought, the Nameless Dao Lord immediately moved to leave the current point in time and press deeper into the False History, only to stop short before setting out.

*...Nothing?*

The Nameless Dao Lord furrowed His brow. Even after tracing events back thirty years, He found no sign of Skyward. All that met His gaze was a blank, empty expanse.

But He quickly figured it out, His gaze turning toward Jiangnan. *There it is. Was it him? Did he sever that portion of the record from the False History? It seems he's already realized that he's in the False History. So he and Skyward are working together? The Saint Sect and Sword Pavilion have joined forces?*

The Nameless Dao Lord's thoughts deepened.

Immediately after, He turned His gaze toward the Achieving Dao Hidden Profound Manor that was gradually emerging from its hidden state, and a darkened look crossed His eyes.

*I'm afraid I'll have to go take a look.*

Skyward's behavior left Him with some misgivings.

*He's done so much, yet hidden only this one Grotto-Heaven. He must have some ulterior motives. I need to see it for myself, if only to find out what he's really thinking.*

---

Jiangdong.

While countless True Monarchs and even the Nameless Dao Lord had their eyes fixed on the Achieving Dao Hidden Profound Manor, Lü Yang had already slipped in through the back door.

*Come to think of it, this is my first time in this place.*

*I didn't expect it to be so large.*

Lü Yang raised his head. He quickly saw five Fruition Attainments hanging high above the grand manor: the five Fruition Attainments controlled by Skyward.

Beyond that, there was also a pillar that reached the heavens within the hall, dividing Yin and Yang, anchoring the two poles of heaven and earth: this was Crooked and Straight. Only with this Empty Attainment Great Dao harmonizing Yin and Yang and ordering the auras could the five Fruition Attainments merge as one. The space appeared merely vast, yet in truth it held countless greater and lesser worlds within.

Just like that, Lü Yang wandered through the manor with keen interest.

Before his eyes stretched a long corridor, decorated with antique elegance. Along both of its sides stood one tightly closed door after another.

Behind each door was a small Boundary Heaven.

Lü Yang paused at each door, peering through the window into the landscape beyond. Each held an entirely different scene.

*This is Skyward's treasure vault, filled with everything he's accumulated over a lifetime!*

*This should be his private meditation chamber, with the environment finely adjusted. Hsss... why does it feel like I'm about to sprout Wisdom Light?*

*And there's this one...*

*This one...*

Lü Yang didn't skip a single chamber. After all, Skyward wasn't in the Grotto-Heaven now, and Lü Yang had both the obligation and responsibility to protect his property.

Before long, he had reached the end of the corridor.

There stood another door. Peering through the window, Lü Yang's gaze met with dazzling, brilliant rainbow light.

Rampart Earth!

At the sight, Lü Yang drew a deep breath. Then he placed his hand gently on the door, and an intense imagery of locking and sealing away swept over him.

*The seal is quite tight.*

It was the double application of Great Forest Wood and Crooked and Straight. If he were right, this door that appeared firmly sealed had originally been open.

The Open state had merely been inverted to the Closed state.

Thus this door came to be.

And on top of that, Skyward had added a Cognitive Barrier, preventing anyone from perceiving the truth, and finally formed this seal.

Under this special design, any means of breaking a seal was useless against the door, because it had never been closed in the first place. Yet the door itself truly existed, so no one could enter either. Whoever wanted to undo such a seal either had to hold a prearranged key like he did...

...Or possess power strong enough to overwhelm the technique.

As Lü Yang marveled, he activated Fate-Reforming once more. Sensing this Mystery, the formerly sealed door began to fade.

The Mystery receded.

Just as Lü Yang had calculated, the room had never been locked to begin with. The door swung wide, and he entered easily.

In an instant, Rampart Earth blazed with light!

Skyward had no Dao Attainment related to it, so after seizing this Fruition Attainment from the Heavenly Lord, he had simply suppressed it by force.

Now that the door was gone, it naturally sought to escape!

But Lü Yang had anticipated this. Before it could flee, he reached out and beckoned, effortlessly gathering the radiance into his arms, and quickly settled its turmoil.

Unlike Skyward, he did possess Dao Attainment for Rampart Earth!

Of course, the more critical tool was Fate-Reforming. This Mystery could harmonize all phenomena, transforming others' things into his own. Applied here, it suited his purpose perfectly!

After calming Rampart Earth, Lü Yang discovered the room contained more than one Fruition Attainment. There was also a glazed sphere placed on the other side as well.

Lü Yang approached the glazed sphere and peered closely. His eyes brightened at once.

Within the glazed sphere was a world complete with mountains, rivers, and forests. Within it, he sensed millions of God-Sealing Method cultivators!

*This is it!*

Joy crossed Lü Yang's eyes. He knew this was the incense believers Skyward had left for him, enough to let him rapidly restore his cultivation.

With that thought, Lü Yang picked up the glazed sphere.

Time was limited. He didn't converse with these cultivators hidden in the Grotto-Heaven but immediately restored the Dao Lineage connection between them.

In an instant, Lü Yang's aura surged!

The radiance transformed by Divine Offerings illuminated upward, piercing the darkness, and Lü Yang immediately sensed the grand Heavenly Palace beyond the Sea of Light!

The next instant, boundless power poured down from the Heavenly Palace, rapidly elevating his Status. His identity as the Mountaintop Fire True Monarch, together with the karma of more than a million believers native to the False History, formed the perfect cover, allowing this immense rise to pass without stirring the slightest ripple throughout the False History.

True Monarch, mid-stage True Monarch, Great True Monarch!

Entering the Dao, Merging with the Dao, Immortal Bridge.

Treading the Heavens!

Whew...

After a long while, Lü Yang exhaled, stretched his limbs, and satisfaction crossed his face. Then, brimming with confidence, he looked at the Rampart Earth in his hand.

The next instant, the rainbow light of Rampart Earth suddenly condensed.

Amplified by Lü Yang's Treading the Heavens status, blazing karmic light forcibly condensed. It traced directly into the depths of history and anchored itself one thousand years in the past.

BOOM!

At once, outside the False History, the Dao Lords abruptly halted their clash of mighty powers. In unison, They turned Their gazes toward the False History, watching it with unprecedented gravity.

"As expected... a Variable has appeared."

"Who is it?"

"The course of history has been changed!"
