Chapter 1210: Heavenly Dao Lockdown

Beyond the False History.

As the False History roiled, the pitch-black cosmos that had been merely an imagery gathering, Dao Lords' wills crisscrossing, suddenly began to collapse, revealing a clear outline.

The Other Shore had drawn near.

When all the Dao Lords raised the mighty power of their descent, the Other Shore, high and unseen until then, sank in turn, drawing closer still to the Sea of Light in the mortal world.

Among the Dao Lords' descending mighty powers, one radiance that gathered countless hues was suppressed most visibly: the Spell Dao Lord. Although the Spiritual Power Dao Lord worked to bear some of the burden, the World-Honored One, Sword Sovereign, and Vast Sky joined forces and still weakened His mighty power by more than half.

The reason was straightforward.

*Is it Sublime Bliss?*

Amid the roiling radiance, thought raced: *Before, I sensed he'd already begun merging the Yellow Millet into the False History. Did he change the course of history?*

The thought carried surprise and doubt.

It shouldn't have been this quick.

Sublime Bliss might make misjudgments about the False History's specifics, but He, as a Dao Lord, would not. He knew all too well the grave flaw in Sublime Bliss's Dao Heart cultivation.

In fact, from the start He'd never expected Sublime Bliss to expand the Yellow Millet across the entire False History during that era of gathered talents. His real intent was to let Sublime Bliss wait, to wait until the Grand Sword Patriarch had slaughtered all under the heavens and Dao Heart cultivators were utterly extinct, and only then would Sublime Bliss emerge to clean up what remained.

He wasn't concerned that Sublime Bliss might be slain by the Grand Sword Patriarch.

After all, Sublime Bliss's true body was a strange beast called Taiyi, possessing formidable deathlessness of its own. At the same status, it would be nearly impossible for anyone to slay.

Sublime Bliss in the False History was merely an Immortal Bridge Great True Monarch, so he perished. But in the True History, he was already a Treading the Heavens Great True Monarch who had created a perfect plane.

Ideally, the best approach for the Spell Dao Lord would have been to send Sublime Bliss directly into the False History to the era after the Grand Sword Patriarch had slaughtered all under the heavens. But those sent into the False History were not Sublime Bliss alone. Other people had also been sent by Dao Lords, going to even earlier times.

So Sublime Bliss had no choice but to follow.

Otherwise, if someone changed history at the source, Sublime Bliss downstream would only feed on scraps. Whatever he did would be meaningless.

"Fellow Daoists... not quite right."

At this thought, the Spell Dao Lord immediately transmitted His confusion: "The course of history couldn't possibly be Sublime Bliss's doing. There must be someone else!"

This wasn't directed at the Sword Sovereign and the others.

Besides Sublime Bliss, the other two were clearly related to the Sword Sovereign, Vast Sky, and World-Honored One. Those three would be even more pleased if someone else had altered history.

So the Spell Dao Lord's voice primarily echoed upward.

Directed straight at the Primordial Saint.

That consciousness that slipped into the False History under your watch, it must be his doing. Fellow Daoist, don't you want to know what he's been up to?

"..."

At the highest point of the Other Shore, the tiny figure offered no reply. His eyes remained cool and detached as He watched the chaos below, the False History barely visible in the confusion.

His perspective was entirely different from the other Dao Lords'.

To the other Dao Lords, the False History's turbulence was a crisis, a risk of things spiraling beyond their control. In His view, however, it was something altogether different.

*Variables...*

The Primordial Saint's eyes wavered like a still mirror, falling upon the False History and suddenly illuminating a sight no other Dao Lord could perceive.

They were countless points of light.

One after another, in streams, they came racing like meteors from every corner of the Sea of Light. They were Variables of persons, Variables of things, Variables of events.

Their numbers multiplied until they formed an indescribable sea of stars. The great net of karma corresponding to the False History hung suspended at the center like a bottomless black hole, devouring and absorbing the Variable stars within it without restraint.

The Variables were converging.

The other Dao Lords' concerns were correct. A vast Variable was indeed brewing in the False History, but it was He who had changed the False History's course.

Yet the Primordial Saint paid it no heed.

Or more precisely, this was exactly what He desired.

*The more, the better.*

---

Boundless and endless, the vast river surged on.

Lü Yang's form was like a fish, sustained by Rampart Earth's incandescent law-light, pushing against the torrent as he struggled upstream toward the source.

*Never thought the False History would have a great net of karma too.*

Lü Yang strained his eyes. The vast river gradually unveiled before him a webwork of currents that mirrored the great net of karma in the mortal world.

But unlike the mortal world, the great net of karma in the False History carried no radiance, every karmic thread was dim, exerting no effect on the true mortal world. It was remarkably fragile. From that angle, the so-called False History was actually more like an immense karmic landscape.

Lü Yang marveled inwardly and pressed onward.

As time passed, the pressure of fighting the current mounted steadily, and the incandescent law-light surrounding him began to show subtle signs of fracturing.

*Nearly exhausted.* Lü Yang's brow furrowed slightly.

He was not Rampart Earth True Monarch at this moment, only relying on Fate-Reforming combined with Treading the Heavens' elevated status and his Dao Attainment to forcibly activate Mystery.

Such usage had its limits.

Bluntly put, it was using strength to overpower technique: leveraging his superior power to drive Rampart Earth's superior mechanism, expending ten units of force for one unit of result.

*But still, it's burning through too fast.*

How long had this taken? He hadn't even gone back a thousand years, and at Treading the Heavens status he was nearly depleted. The true blockade must lie at the source.

Of course, there was a simple solution.

A Guest Rejected by Heaven and Earth.

If he opened this talent, Lü Yang was confident the obstruction from the False History would immediately vanish. But after a moment's thought, he chose not to.

*Not the right time.*

*This talent must be kept for the final, decisive moment. Exposing it now would only introduce more Variables into my plan.*

At this thought, Lü Yang formed a spell gesture and drew additional Mystery from the Heavenly Palace, surging powerfully against the mounting obstruction in the False History.

Originally he'd been prepared to retreat if need be. However, at that very moment, a thunderous sound seemed to echo from the False History's source.

BOOM!

In that instant, the obstruction from the False History sharply dropped by several tenths. Lü Yang seized the opportunity, surged forward, and finally broke through.

*This is... Si Sui?*

Lü Yang understood. Even while trapped at the False History's source, Si Sui had never ceased struggling, endlessly pounding this cage of time.

It seemed pointless.

Yet now, this relentless struggle persisting over countless years became the straw that broke the camel's back, bringing Lü Yang to this point.

Lü Yang looked up. Before his eyes was a boundless passage that seemed to contain the Sea of Light's infinite hues, yet appeared primordial and hazy. One end was anchored in the False History, the other lost in darkness, filled only with the sound of heavy blows echoing within.

The Heavenly Dao.

*This is the place.*

Never had Lü Yang been this close to the truth of 129,600 years ago, like a tiny ant that had strayed to the feet of a giant.

He gazed with all his might.

*Back then, it was this Heavenly Dao that linked history to the Dao Lord battlefield, drawing in the World-Honored One to participate in the great war and complete that alteration of cause and transformation of effect.*

Thus, one end of the Heavenly Dao was anchored in the False History.

As for the other end lost in darkness, though he couldn't see clearly, a faint silhouette was visible. Without question, it was Si Sui.
