Chapter 1210: The Heavenly Dao Sealed

Beyond the False History.

As the False History shuddered, the pitch-black cosmos that had been merely images converging and Dao Lords' wills crossing suddenly began to collapse, revealing clear contours.

The Other Shore drew near.

When all the Dao Lords elevated their mighty power, the Other Shore, once too remote to perceive, would sink accordingly, drawing closer to the Sea of Light in the mortal world.

And among the Dao Lords' descending mighty power, the one suppressed most visibly was a rosy glow that had gathered the colors of billions. The Spell Dao Lord, though the Spiritual Power Dao Lord was doing his best to share the burden, still had his mighty power reduced by over half when the World-Honored One, Sword Sovereign, and Vast Sky acted in concert.

The reason was simple.

*Is it Sublime Bliss?*

In the roiling light, his thoughts turned urgent: *I sensed before that he was already fusing the Yellow Millet into the False History. Did he alter the course of history?*

The thought carried surprise and doubt.

It shouldn't have happened this quickly.

Sublime Bliss would make misjudgments about the specifics of the False History, but as a Dao Lord he would not. He knew all too well the vast flaw in Sublime Bliss's Dao Heart cultivation.

In fact, from the beginning he had never counted on Sublime Bliss to expand the Yellow Millet across the entire False History in an age when heroes gathered. His real intent was to have Sublime Bliss wait, to wait until the Grand Sword Patriarch had slaughtered all under the heavens and Dao Heart cultivators were utterly extinct, and only then have Sublime Bliss emerge to clean up the remainder.

He wasn't concerned that Sublime Bliss would be slain by the Grand Sword Patriarch.

After all, Sublime Bliss's true body was a strange creature called Taiyi, inherently possessing exceptionally strong immortality; at the same status, it was extremely hard for anyone to slay it.

Sublime Bliss in the False History was merely an Immortal Bridge Great True Monarch and so could die, but the one in the True History was already a Treading the Heavens Great True Monarch who had created a perfect plane.

Ideally, the best approach for the Spell Dao Lord would be to send Sublime Bliss directly into the False History at the time after the Grand Sword Patriarch had slaughtered all under the heavens, but more than just Sublime Bliss had been sent into the False History. Other people had also been sent in by Dao Lords, and they had gone to earlier eras.

So Sublime Bliss had no choice but to follow.

Otherwise, if someone upstream altered history, Sublime Bliss downstream would only get the scraps, and whatever he did would be pointless.

"Sirs... something's not right."

At this thought, the Spell Dao Lord immediately transmitted his confusion: "The flow of history isn't likely to have been changed by Sublime Bliss. There's probably someone else!"

This wasn't directed at the Sword Sovereign and the others.

Besides Sublime Bliss, the other two were clearly connected to the Sword Sovereign, Vast Sky, and World-Honored One, so they would be even more pleased if someone else had changed history.

Therefore, the Spell Dao Lord's voice primarily echoed upward.

Directly toward the Primordial Saint.

"That consciousness that fled into the False History under your watch must be his doing. Fellow Daoist, surely you want to know what he's been up to?"

"..."

At the apex of the Other Shore, the small figure didn't respond, merely keeping His gaze indifferent, serenely watching the chaotic, hard-to-discern False History below.

His perspective was entirely different from that of the other Dao Lords.

To the other Dao Lords, the False History's turbulence was a crisis, a risk of things slipping beyond their control. Yet to Him it was altogether different.

*Variable...*

The Primordial Saint's gaze rippled like a clear mirror as it fell upon the False History, and it suddenly illuminated a sight no other Dao Lord could perceive.

They were countless points of light.

One after another, stream after stream, they flew from every corner of the Sea of Light like shooting stars. They were Variables of people, of things, of events.

Their numbers grew and grew until they formed an indescribable sea of stars, and the great net of karma corresponding to the False History hung suspended at the sea's center like a bottomless black hole, swallowing like a whale and guzzling like an ox, shamelessly consuming and absorbing the Variable starlight within.

The Variables were converging.

The other Dao Lords' worries were justified. There truly was a vast Variable brewing in the False History, and it was indeed he who had altered the history's flow.

Yet the Primordial Saint didn't care.

Or more precisely, this was exactly what He hoped for.

*...The more, the better.*

Boundless and fathomless, churning torrents.

Under the brilliant white law-light of Rampart Earth, Lü Yang's form was like a fish struggling upstream against the surging current, pressing with effort toward its source.

*I didn't expect the False History to have its own great net of karma.*

Straining his vision to the limit, Lü Yang watched as the vast river gradually revealed a network of veins identical to the great net of karma in the mortal world.

But unlike the mortal world, the False History's great net of karma bore no radiance and every karmic thread was dim, exerting no influence on the true mortal world and leaving it quite fragile. In a sense, the so-called False History was really more like a cosmos-scale landscape of karma.

Lü Yang sighed inwardly and pressed onward with resolve.

As time wore on, the pressure from struggling upstream grew more severe until even the brilliant white law-light cloaking him began to show signs of collapse.

*My strength's running out.* Lü Yang's brow furrowed slightly.

He wasn't Rampart Earth True Monarch in this moment. He was merely relying on the Fate-Reforming gift, combined with the high status of his Treading the Heavens realm and forcibly channeling his cultivation to activate the Mysteries.

This method of use ultimately had limits.

Put simply, it was using brute force to overcome technique, leveraging his superior stats to drive Rampart Earth's superior mechanism, expending ten units of strength to extract one unit of effect.

*But... it's still too fast.*

*How long has it been? I haven't even traced back a thousand years of time, yet with my Treading the Heavens realm status, I'm already nearly exhausted. Most of the obstruction must be at the source.*

Of course, he also had a simple solution.

A Guest Rejected by Heaven and Earth.

If he activated this talent, Lü Yang was certain the obstruction from the False History would vanish at once, but after a moment's thought, he chose to abandon the idea.

*Not yet.*

*This talent must be kept for the final moment when everything hangs in the balance. Revealing it before then would only create more Variables in my scheme.*

At the thought, Lü Yang sealed his hand signs, drawing more Mysteries from the Heavenly Palace, and launched a violent surge against the mounting obstruction in the False History.

He had been prepared to retreat if it didn't work, but at that very moment, a sound of impact seemed to resound faintly from the source of the False History.

BOOM!

In that instant, the obstruction from the False History abruptly weakened by several tenths, and Lü Yang seized the chance, surged through in one motion, and finally emerged into clarity.

*This is... Si Sui?*

Lü Yang knew well that even trapped at the source of the False History, Si Sui had never given up struggling, ceaselessly pounding against this temporal prison.

It had seemed futile.

Yet now, this futility sustained for countless ages became the final straw, allowing Lü Yang to reach this place!

Lü Yang looked up, and what met his eyes was a vast passage without limit, seeming to contain all the infinite luminescence of the Sea of Light, paradoxically rendering it primordial and chaotic in appearance. One end was anchored on the False History, the other end vanished into darkness. Only the heavy sound of impacts echoed through the passage.

The Heavenly Dao.

*This is it.*

Never had there been a moment when Lü Yang drew so close to the truth of 129,600 years ago, like a tiny ant that had wandered to the feet of a giant.

His gaze stretched to its furthest reach.

*Back then, it was this Heavenly Dao that bridged history and the Dao Lord battlefield, drawing the World-Honored One into the great war to complete that alteration of cause and transformation of effect.*

So one end of the Heavenly Dao was anchored on the False History.

As for the other end disappearing into darkness, though he couldn't see it clearly, he could dimly make out a silhouette. Without doubt, it was Si Sui's form!
