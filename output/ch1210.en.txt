Chapter 1210: The Heavenly Dao's Blockade

Beyond the False History.

As the False History shook, the pitch-black cosmos that had merely gathered the imagery of Dao Lords' wills crisscrossing suddenly began to collapse, revealing a clear outline.

The Other Shore was descending.

As the Dao Lords unleashed the full might of Their descent, the Other Shore, until then lofty and unseen, sank with Them, drawing ever closer to the Sea of Light in the mortal world.

Among the mighty powers descending from the Dao Lords, one radiance woven from countless hues was suppressed most severely: the Spell Dao Lord's. Although the Spiritual Power Dao Lord helped shoulder some of the pressure, the combined efforts of the World-Honored One, the Sword Sovereign, and Vast Sky still weakened His mighty power by more than half.

The reason was straightforward.

*Is it Sublime Bliss?*

Amid the roiling radiance, a thought flashed through His mind: *Earlier, I sensed that he had already begun merging the Yellow Millet into the False History. Did he alter the course of history?*

The possibility surprised Him, but it also filled Him with doubt.

It shouldn't have been this quick.

Sublime Bliss might have misjudged the circumstances within the False History, but the Spell Dao Lord would not. He knew perfectly well the fatal flaw in Sublime Bliss's Dao Heart cultivation.

In fact, from the start He had never expected Sublime Bliss to expand the Yellow Millet across the entire False History during that era of gathered talents. His real intent was to let Sublime Bliss wait, to wait until the Grand Sword Patriarch had slaughtered all under the heavens and Dao Heart cultivators were utterly extinct, and only then would Sublime Bliss emerge to clean up what remained.

He wasn't concerned that Sublime Bliss might be slain by the Grand Sword Patriarch.

After all, Sublime Bliss's true body was a strange beast called Taiyi, possessing formidable deathlessness of its own. At the same status, it would be nearly impossible for anyone to slay.

Sublime Bliss in the False History was merely an Immortal Bridge Great True Monarch, so he perished. But in the True History, he was already a Treading the Heavens Great True Monarch who had created a perfect plane.

Ideally, the best approach for the Spell Dao Lord would have been to send Sublime Bliss directly into the False History to the era after the Grand Sword Patriarch had slaughtered all under the heavens. Unfortunately, Sublime Bliss was not the only person entering the False History. The other Dao Lords had sent in Their own pieces, and those pieces had been placed at earlier points in history.

So Sublime Bliss had no choice but to follow.

Otherwise, if someone altered history closer to its source, Sublime Bliss would be left with nothing but their scraps farther downstream. No matter what he did, it would all be meaningless.

"Fellow Daoists... something's not right."

At this thought, the Spell Dao Lord immediately voiced His doubt: "It is unlikely that Sublime Bliss changed the course of history. There may be someone else!"

This wasn't directed at the Sword Sovereign and the others.

Besides Sublime Bliss, the other two were clearly related to the Sword Sovereign, Vast Sky, and World-Honored One. Those three would only be pleased if someone else had altered history.

So the Spell Dao Lord's voice primarily echoed upward.

It was aimed directly at the Primordial Saint.

"That consciousness slipped into the False History beneath your very gaze, Fellow Daoist. This must be its doing. Do you not wish to know what it has done?"

"..."

At the highest point of the Other Shore, the tiny figure offered no reply. His eyes remained cool and detached as He watched the chaos below, the False History barely visible in the confusion.

His perspective was entirely different from the other Dao Lords'.

To the other Dao Lords, the False History's turbulence was a crisis, a risk of things spiraling beyond their control. In His view, however, it was something altogether different.

*Variables...*

The Primordial Saint's eyes rippled faintly like a clear mirror, falling upon the False History and suddenly illuminating a sight no other Dao Lord could perceive.

They were countless points of light.

One after another, in streams, they came racing like meteors from every corner of the Sea of Light. They were Variables of persons, Variables of things, Variables of events.

Their numbers multiplied until they formed an indescribable sea of stars. The great net of karma corresponding to the False History hung suspended at the center like a bottomless black hole, devouring and absorbing the Variable stars within it without restraint.

The Variables were converging.

The other Dao Lords' concerns were correct. A vast Variable was indeed brewing in the False History, and it was precisely this variable who had changed the False History's course.

Yet the Primordial Saint paid it no heed.

Or more precisely, this was exactly what He desired.

*The more, the better.*

---

A vast river surged without end.

Lü Yang moved through it like a fish, wrapped in the blazing white dharma light of Rampart Earth as he struggled against the racing current and pushed toward its source.

*I didn't expect the False History to possess its own great net of karma.*

Lü Yang strained his eyes. The vast river gradually unveiled before him a webwork of currents that mirrored the great net of karma in the mortal world.

But unlike the mortal world, the great net of karma in the False History carried no radiance, every karmic thread was dim, exerting no effect on the true mortal world. It was remarkably fragile. From that angle, the so-called False History was actually more like an immense karmic landscape.

Lü Yang marveled inwardly and pressed onward.

As time passed, the pressure of fighting the current mounted steadily, and the incandescent dharma light surrounding him began to show subtle signs of fracturing.

*I'm nearly out of strength.*

Lü Yang frowned slightly.

He wasn't the Rampart Earth True Monarch at this moment. He was merely relying on Fate-Reforming, his Treading the Heavens cultivation, and the corresponding Dao Attainment to forcibly activate the Mysteries.

Such usage had its limits.

Bluntly put, it was using strength to overpower technique: leveraging his superior power to drive Rampart Earth's superior mechanism, expending ten units of force for one unit of result.

*But still, it's burning through too fast.*

How long had it taken? He hadn't even traced back a full thousand years, yet his Treading the Heavens status was already nearly exhausted. The true blockade had to lie at the source.

Of course, there was a simple solution.

A Guest Rejected by Heaven and Earth.

If he opened this talent, Lü Yang was confident the obstruction from the False History would immediately vanish. But after a moment's thought, he chose not to.

*I need to save this talent for the final, decisive moment. Exposing it now would only introduce more Variables into my plan.*

I need to save this Talent for the final, decisive moment. Exposing it now would only introduce more Variables into my plan._

At this thought, Lü Yang formed a hand seal and drew more Mysteries from the Heavenly Palace. He hurled himself against the mounting resistance within the False History.

Originally he had been prepared to retreat if need be. However, at that very moment, a thunderous sound seemed to echo from the False History's source.

BOOM!

In that instant, the obstruction from the False History sharply dropped by several tenths. Lü Yang seized the opportunity, surged forward, and finally broke through.

*This is... Si Sui?*

Lü Yang understood. Even while trapped at the False History's source, Si Sui had never ceased struggling, endlessly pounding this cage of time.

It seemed pointless.

Yet now, this relentless struggle persisting over countless years became the straw that broke the camel's back, bringing Lü Yang to this point.

Before him stretched an incomparably vast passage. It seemed to contain every color in the Sea of Light, yet with all those colors mingling together, it instead appeared murky and indistinct, like Primordial Chaos. One end was anchored in the False History, while the other vanished into darkness, from which only the echoes of heavy blows could be heard.

The Heavenly Dao.

*This is the place.*

Never had Lü Yang been this close to the truth of 129,600 years ago, like a tiny ant that had strayed to the feet of a giant.

He gazed with all his might.

*Back then, it was this Heavenly Dao that linked history to the Dao Lord battlefield, drawing the World-Honored One into the great war and completing that alteration of cause and transformation of effect.*

Thus, one end of the Heavenly Dao was anchored in the False History.

As for the other end lost in darkness, though he couldn't see clearly, a faint silhouette was visible. Without question, it was Si Sui.
